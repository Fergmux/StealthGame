# Spike S12: the cull delay and un-cull lead recomputed from measured L99, 2026-10-25

> **Story:** `production/epics/information-visibility-policy/story-006-spike-s12-cull-delay-vs-measured-l99.md` (TR-ivp-004)
> **Governs:** IVP F3 (`ConcealCullDelayMs`), F4 (`T_uncull`), ADR-0003 §5.3 and the Risks row "Measured L99 exceeds the F3 assumption" · **Engine:** UE 5.8.4 Development editor binary in `-game`, legacy `IpNetDriver`, 60 fps cap on every process
> **Verdict:** **CONFIRMED: `ConcealCullDelayMs` = 250 ms and `T_uncull` = 250 ms** under StealthLeak1 and StealthLeak2 (measured packet-level L99 = 133.4 ms on both; with S10's measured T_reopen p99 ≈ 20 ms, F3 gives 250 ms and F4 gives 220 ms ≤ 250 ms). No larger pair is recorded for TDC or Concealment. **CONCERN:** F3/F4 count delivered packets only; at the data level, a re-open whose open bunch is lost waits one resend, so the end-to-end un-cull → client actor time has a p99 of ~0.4 s under StealthLeak2 (≈ 2 % of re-opens) and ~0.17-0.4 s under StealthLeak1.

## Measurement

The S9 cull rig (S10 scenario: 3 crowd subjects cycling cull → re-open every ~3 s for the whole run, 600 cycles per run) with 3 client processes, **640 s per profile** (≥ 10 min), packet-level timing on every process (`-StealthPacketTiming`: each packet's flush time at the server and processing time at the client, matched by packet id, one QPC clock). L99 = p99 of the server→client one-way latency of every delivered packet (receive processing at the client, so it includes the client's frame quantisation at 60 fps).

| Profile | Packets (3 clients) | One-way server→client: median / p90 / **p99** / max | Loss | Loss bursts (consecutive ids) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| StealthLeak1 (PktLag 100, 1 %) run `s10-l1b` | 106,430 | 116.9 / 130.2 / **133.4** / 573.7 ms | 1.0-1.1 % | 1: 1081, 2: 5, 5-6: 3 |
| StealthLeak2 (PktLag 100, 2 %) run `s10-l2b` | 104,826 | 117.0 / 129.8 / **133.4** / 264.8 ms | 1.8-2.0 % | 1: 1962, 2: 40, 3-5: 5 |

Earlier runs with a shorter-lived rig (`s10-l1`, crowd exhausted after 2 min, 640 s of packets): L99 132.5 ms (Leak1). Listen-host frame time (60 fps cap): mean 16.7 ms, p99 of per-second p99 43.7-54.6 ms, max 1.23-1.27 s (crowd spawn).

`f_net` = 15 Hz (1/f_net = 66.7 ms). `T_reopen` from S10 (`production/qa/spikes/2026-10-25_S10.md`): channel-open processing + client actor creation to its first rendered frame, median ≈ 11.5 ms, **p99 ≈ 20 ms** (packet receipt → actor spawned p99 4.3-5.9 ms; spawn → first frame p99 12-15 ms).

## Recomputation

| Formula | StealthLeak1 | StealthLeak2 | Default | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F3 `D_cull = 50 · ceil((L99 + 1/f_net) / 50)` | 50 · ceil((133.4 + 66.7)/50) = 50 · ceil(4.002) = **250 ms** | **250 ms** | 250 ms | **Confirmed** (note: L99 sits 0.1 ms above the 133.3 ms point where F3 would give 200 ms) |
| F4 `T_uncull = L99 + 1/f_net + T_reopen` | 133.4 + 66.7 + 20 = **220 ms** (median T_reopen: 212 ms) | **220 ms** | 250 ms | **Confirmed** (250 ≥ 220) |

The provisional L99 of 150 ms was conservative; the measured value leaves 30 ms of F4 headroom at the 250 ms floor that Concealment's `BushExitCrossSec` / `BushStrikeEmergeSec` inherit. No TDC or Concealment change request is raised.

## Data-level tail (CONCERN)

End-to-end un-cull (server state change) → re-created actor on the client, same-step un-cull (S10 case A), 300 cycles × 3 clients per profile:

| Profile | median | p99 per client | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| StealthLeak1 | 143-146 ms | 169, 169, 169 ms | 419 ms |
| StealthLeak2 | 142-146 ms | 396, 375, 169 ms | 437 ms |

The ~0.4 s cases are re-opens whose packet was lost (retransmitted after a NAK, ~one RTT later). F4 as written bounds the delivered-packet case; whether `T_uncull` should cover the resend tail (≈ 1-2 % of re-opens arriving ~230 ms late, i.e. a body that appears after its exit transition has begun) is a Visibility Policy / Concealment decision: recorded for story 029 (un-cull lead bound) rather than changing the pair here.

## Limits

- One machine, loopback, emulated (Bernoulli) loss; 3 clients; uncooked editor binary; prototype pawns.
- L99 includes the client's frame quantisation (60 fps) because the receive time is when the client processes the packet; a faster client frame would lower it by up to ~8 ms.
