# Spike S10: a re-opened pawn is a new actor; NetGUID, presentation and the in-flight race, 2026-10-25

> **Story:** `production/epics/information-visibility-policy/story-005-spike-s10-reopen-behaviour-and-netguid.md` (TR-ivp-004)
> **Governs:** ADR-0003 §5.6-5.8, E10 (in-flight race), E11 (re-open), L32, IVP F4 `T_reopen` · **Engine:** UE 5.8.4 Development editor binary in `-game`, legacy `IpNetDriver`, 60 fps cap, StealthLeak1 and StealthLeak2 on every process
> **Verdict:** **FAIL** — measured and recorded as specified, but two of the conditions ADR-0003 relies on do not hold on the current engine path and one is not met with the engine's default clock:
> 1. **In-flight race is not safe**: when a cull is followed by an un-cull before the client acknowledges the close, ~1 % (StealthLeak1) / ~2 % (StealthLeak2) of such re-opens leave the client **without the pawn** while the server holds an open channel (`LogNet: Warning: UActorChannel::ProcessBunch: ReadContentBlockPayload failed to find/create ACTOR`), until the next cull/un-cull. A candidate fix (hold the re-open per connection until the closed channel is acknowledged) removed every occurrence (0 engine warnings over 810 race re-opens) but adds a long hold tail.
> 2. **Visible pop on the first rendered frame**: every re-created prototype pawn renders its first frame in an un-evaluated (collapsed) pose, standing from the next frame (frame sequence retained). The root is exact (≤ 0.5 cm).
> 3. **Pose and death times** derived from replicated server times are off by ~150 ms with the engine's default `GetServerWorldTimeSeconds` (≈ one-way latency): the 16.7 ms bound needs ADR-0002 §9's estimator, whose stated budget (p99 ≤ 30 ms) is itself above one frame.
>
> **PASS** parts: a new client actor on every re-open (3,572 of 3,572 re-creations in the two unguarded runs), the **same NetGUID** every time (no new NetGUID allocated by 1,800 re-opens), 0 ensures, 0 crashes, no registry faults (the prototype has no significance/audio/decal registries; pawn census stable), `T_reopen` median ≈ 11.5 ms, p99 ≈ 20 ms (F4 provisional 33 ms). A failure is handled by a superseding ADR (story rule), so dependents stay Blocked.

## Resolution (2026-10-25)

Decided by **ADR-0003 amendment "S10 resolution 2026-10-25"** (an amendment, not a superseding ADR: the core decision stands; rationale in the amendment) and ADR-0002 amendment "S10 resolution 2026-10-25" (budget unchanged). Items 1-3 below map to S10-R2/R3/R4 (guard on the in-sequence close acknowledgement, bounded by `ReopenAckTimeoutMs` 350 ms, forced fresh channel, client-reported repair), S10-R5 (warm-up) and S10-R6 (estimator budget p95 ≤ 15 ms / p99 ≤ 30 ms replaces the 16.7 ms bound). Note on item 1: in 5.8.4 the in-sequence close acknowledgement and the channel leaving `OpenChannels` are the same event (`UChannel::CleanUp`), so Q5g's tail is not explained by the signal; the re-run decomposes it. Fix and re-run: `production/epics/information-visibility-policy/story-056-s10-fix-implementation-and-rerun.md`; dependents are Blocked on story-056.

## Needed design changes (as reported; see Resolution above)

1. **Re-open waits for the close acknowledgement per connection** (E10). The cull state machine must not let a pawn become relevant again to a connection while that connection's previous channel for it is still closing. Use the close bunch's own packet acknowledgement (the channel's close reliable ack), not the channel's presence in `OpenChannels` (whose cleanup tail reached 8 s here). Until then, an un-cull within ~1 RTT of a cull can delete the pawn on the client.
2. **`RefreshPresentation` must evaluate the animation before the first render** of a re-created pawn (or keep the mesh hidden until its first animation update): the prototype's AnimBP renders one collapsed frame. Re-prove on `AStealthPawn` with the production AnimBP.
3. **Presentation times need the ADR-0002 §9 server-clock estimator**, and the S10 bound (16.7 ms) and ADR-0002's estimator budget (p95 15 ms, p99 30 ms) must be reconciled: either the estimator reaches ≤ 16.7 ms at p99 under `LeakNetProfile`, or the S10 pose bound becomes the estimator budget. The death fast-forward depends on the same clock.
4. Registries: none exist on the prototype; the production significance/audio/decal registries must be re-run through this rig when they land.
