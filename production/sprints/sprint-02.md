## Capacity

- Unit: planning-days, same yardstick as Sprint 1: implementation story S 0.25 / M 0.5 / L 1.0; spike story S 0.5 / M 1.0 / L 2.0 (spikes: iif-008..011, st-002..004, ivp-005/006, tdc-071). cpa-003 is counted at 0.5 (only the UE import remains). - **Measured Sprint 1 velocity (the calibration result):** all 14 Must (8.0 planning-days) and all 14 Should (9.0 planning-days) stories, 28 stories and 17.0 planning-days, closed within the first sprint day (2026-10-25) by serialized AI build agents on one UBT lane, in batches of about 6-8 stories at about 1-2 hours per batch. That is a peak of **17.0 planning-days and 28 stories per working day**, against the 0.8 planning-days per day Sprint 1 planned (8.0 over 10 days): about 21 times the plan. The measured actual-to-planned ratio is at most 0.06 working days per planning-day (about 0.5 hour wall-clock per planning-day), against the 0.6 agent-compression factor in `production/estimates/2026-10-25_mvp-rebaseline.md`, which this replaces.
- **Planning assumption for Sprint 2 (stated, not measured):** sustained throughput is **8 planning-days per working day, about 47 % of the measured peak** (about 14 stories per day). Reasons for not using the peak: one data point (N=1) from one day; Sprint 1's mix was script, lint and skeleton work, the producer gate notes that spikes are wall-clock-bound (6-10 hours of exclusive machine windows), while Sprint 2 adds six or more 1.0-2.0 day spikes (V1/V2/V3/V5, S3, S4, net-emulation), L-size integration stories and multi-process (Gauntlet) runs; the single UBT lane and one editor instance stay serial; human review of ADR amendment requests and spike verdicts adds latency; fatigue or ramp-down over ten days was not observed. The estimate yardstick itself is unchanged, only the conversion of planning-days to wall-clock changes.
- Total days: 10 working days (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-20)
- Gross capacity: 10 days x 8 planning-days = 80 planning-days
- Buffer (20%): 16 planning-days for unplanned work (UE rebuilds after module changes, editor crashes, asset-resave recovery, ADR amendment rework after spikes)
- Available: 64 planning-days
- Must Have 46.25 days (86 stories, 72 % of available); Should Have 19.0 days (54 stories); Must + Should 65.25 days (102 % of available, 82 % of gross); Nice to Have 41.5 days (78 stories, an ordered pull-forward queue). Total planned 106.75 days across 218 stories. Only Must is a commitment; Should is cut first; Nice to Have is not expected to finish.
- Single-machine constraint (unchanged from Sprint 1): UE builds, PIE and multi-process runs are serial and one editor instance exists. Script-only stories (TDC doc lint 029-037, hooks, CI) run without the editor while a build is running. Per D41 one `/dev-story` session may batch adjacent small stories of one epic into one commit listing all story IDs; the measured 6-8 stories per batch is the planning unit.
