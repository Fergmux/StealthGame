## Summary

Assassination gives the game its one decisive action: a single press at arm's length (about 2 m) that kills whoever the server resolves as the victim, with no aim test, lock-on or warning, and an Owner-only plate that tells the attacker only "rival" or "not a rival". Its counterweight is the defence: a visible, planted brace of up to 4 s that burns a 60 s cooldown from activation and reverses any strike that meets it, eliminating the attacker into an informant. It exists so every encounter resolves in one server-ordered outcome that bystanders can read and nobody can take back (P3), while striking and bracing stay the loudest tells in the crowd (P2) and look identical for every kind of victim (P1).

## Overview

Assassination & Defence Reversal ("Assassination") is the server-authoritative system that gives the two `Prepare` and `Attack` commands of the Input & Interaction Framework (IIF) their meaning. It owns: **victim resolution** (which pawn, if any, an `Attack(TargetPawn | TargetSpot)` reaches, including blind strikes on concealed pawns); the **one-strike outcome** (kill, reversal or whiff, decided at the Attack's queue slot); the **defence** (the protected window of a held `Prepare`, its 60 s cooldown from activation, the counter after a reversal); the **attacker lockout**; the **strike, counter and death presentation contract** (clip timings, warp limits, body lifetime and removal); the server-only per-pawn defence state; and the Owner-only **kill confirmation**. It answers "NPC or player?" through the Roster's server-only `ResolveVictimKind`, requests `RequestTransition(..., Assassinated | ReversedAgainst)`, and emits `OnPawnKilled(Victim, Killer, VictimKind, Cause)` inside the resolving slot of Round's single queue. It never counts strikes (NPC-Kill Penalty), never replaces bodies (Crowd), never decides concealment (Concealment) and never draws UI (HUD).

## Player Fantasy

**Framing: direct** (a Gameplay system the player operates; recommended option in autonomous mode).

*You watched them for twenty seconds. You close to arm's length, one press, and the city exhales.* The strike is the single most consequential thing a player does: it is the confession the whole round has been withholding. Before it, the attacker is a citizen; after it, everyone in sight knows a hunter stood there. The fantasy is **certainty bought with exposure**: a correct read is final and unambiguous (the plate says *Target eliminated*), a wrong read costs something that never comes back (a strike, one's own active life, or at the least the exposure of a strike every watcher saw), and nothing about aim or reflexes decides it. Anticipation is the only skill.

The defender's fantasy is the **bait**: the stranger who stops, plants their feet and raises their forearms when nobody seems near. Bracing is a held, visible gamble: it is a tell to every watcher, it burns a minute of safety, and it only pays if someone is already committed. When it pays, the reversal is the loudest moment in the game: the striker lunges, meets the counter, and falls.

Anchor moments (each maps to an acceptance criterion): (1) a kill at arm's length with no reticle, no lock and no warning to the victim (AC-ASN-01, 05); (2) a reversal that every bystander reads as "the striker fell" (AC-ASN-03, 30); (3) the kill plate that tells the attacker exactly one thing: rival, or not a rival (AC-ASN-16); (4) a blind strike into a bush that is a commitment to a bush, never an aim test (AC-ASN-12).

*Pillar service*: P3 (decisive resolution in one action; mistakes stick), P2 (strike and brace are the two loudest tells), P1 (identical clips, outcomes and feedback for every victim kind), anti-pillar "not a reflex game" (no parry window: the brace is sampled at execution, before any swing is visible).

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

*Specialists consulted (full review mode, budget 3)*: systems-designer (formulas, edge cases, knob values), ue-replication-specialist (presentation replication, leak vectors, network tests), art-director (clips, reversal readability, audio, plates). The IIF, Round, Roster, Visibility Policy and Concealment contracts are inherited and not restated; this section gives them meaning.

**A. Who may act, and when**

**ASN-R1. Legality is the IIF's; meaning is Assassination's.** `Attack` and `Prepare(Begin)` are legal only for legality class `Active`, not running, not traversing (IIF R7), and `Prepare` not while concealed (IIF R9, CON-R7: `NotNow`). Assassination registers two `CanBegin` predicates and nothing else:
- `Prepare`: `Reject(Cooldown)` iff F3 says the cooldown is running; else `Allow`.
- `Attack`: always `Allow`. The lockout is enforced by the action slot (`Striking` is busy for `AttackAttemptLockoutSec`, IIF R9 → `Busy`). **No Attack is ever rejected for a reason about its target** (IIF R6), so no reply can confirm a target's kind, team, brace or concealment.

**ASN-R2. Re-check at resolution.** The resolver runs at the Attack's queue slot (IIF R5.2, Round R14) and reads only live state at that slot: the attacker's legality class (an attacker who became `Informant` earlier in the drain is voided with `NotNow`, Roster OQ9), the victim candidates and the defender's slot state. Nothing is evaluated at submission.

**B. The defence**

**ASN-R3. Prepare is a protected window, not a charge** (confirms IIF R8, resolves IIF OQ2). It starts at `Prepare(Begin)` execution and ends at the executed `End` (arrival + `ReleaseDebounceSec`) or at `HoldTimer(CapEnd)` after `DefenceDurationSec` (4.0), whichever is first (F2). The cooldown stamp `LastPrepareActivation` is written at execution, so an unused, tapped or early-released brace burns the full `DefenceCooldownSec` (60). Re-pressing during the window is a no-op; re-pressing during the cooldown is `Cooldown`; nothing resets, extends or refunds it (IIF R8). Art bible 7.3's "charge then armed" ring reading is superseded (change request).

**ASN-R4. The brace is planted.** While `Bracing` the pawn neither translates nor turns; only head-look moves (confirms SCL R5.2, CD ruling D3; resolves IIF OQ3 and SCL OQ9). Facing has no effect on the outcome: a brace protects from every bearing. Planting makes the brace a committed, readable stance (P2) and prevents brace-walking as mobile armour; the brace stays an anticipation decision because it is sampled at execution (R5). No brace-pivot clips are needed.

**ASN-R5. The defence is sampled at Attack execution.** A defender is *braced* for an Attack iff, at the Attack's slot, its IIF slot state is `Bracing` (F2 membership: execution key after the Begin's, before the window's end item). A pending End inside its debounce still counts as braced. Nothing during the strike animation can change the outcome: there is no parry window (anti-pillar "not a reflex game").

**ASN-R6. The target gets no warning** (presentation rule; revised at design review). No presentation cue on the target's own client (victim or defender) precedes the local contact frame of a strike aimed at it: no widget, owner-only audio, haptic, camera change, own-pawn animation change or loss of control. Exempt: the attacker's visible strike pose and its positional world audio, which every viewer at that distance sees and hears alike. Being targeted is undetectable by any system channel (IIF R8); the brace is chosen by reading the approach. Owner- and Team-audience **data** caused by the outcome (Roster state, IIF replies, `FHoldStatus`, `FDefenceResult`) may reach the wire at the slot; it is never **presented** before the local contact frame (ASN-R23), and a modified client learning it up to `T_v` early is an accepted residual (IVP L10 change request), because the outcome is already settled and nothing the target does can change it (R5).

**ASN-R7. NPCs brace through the same path.** Crowd issues real `Prepare` commands (Tell Matrix T4: flinch and startle braces, same cooldown and planted stance). A braced NPC reverses an attacker exactly as a braced player does; the resolver has no controller, binding or kind term. NPCs never `Attack` (Tell Matrix T6, rate 0): the strike is the intended confession.

**C. The strike**

**ASN-R8. Victim resolution** (server, at the Attack's slot; F1). Candidates exclude the attacker, every pawn whose `GetRelation(Attacker, Pawn) = Teammate`, every pawn for which `ResolveVictimKind` returns `NotTargetable`, and every pawn whose strike role is `Dead` (killed or reversed in an earlier slot, including one whose death write is still pending; F4). Then, first match wins:
1. **Honoured `PawnRef`** (IIF R10: `CanViewerKnow(Attacker, Pawn, Position)` and within `PawnRefAgreementCm` of the `WorldPoint`): that pawn, if it passes the F1 reach test; otherwise the strike is a **whiff**. A `PawnRef` naming a `Dead` pawn is also a terminal whiff: it never falls through to a spot rule, so striking at a body can never kill a bystander beside it and charge a strike.
2. **Bush spot**: the `WorldPoint` lies in a bush's spot zone (CON-R12, F4): `GetSpotOccupants(BushId, Attacker)` first filtered by the full F1 reach test at each candidate's server position at the slot, then one survivor picked by an unbiased `UniformBelow(|Reachable|)` draw from Concealment's server-secret `BushSpotPick` stream (`FSecretStream(GetServerSubSeed(BushSpotPick, LayoutAttempt), "CON_SPOT")` keyed by `(ServerTickIndex, AttackerCommandSeq, BushId)`; Concealment F4, ADR-0006 domain 14; never `RoundSeed`; replaces the former `SpotSalt` hash). This rule is **terminal**: if no occupant qualifies, the strike is a whiff and never falls through to rule 3 (a blind strike is a commitment to a bush).
3. **Open spot**: non-concealed candidates (a concealed pawn is a candidate only through rule 2) whose capsule axis lies within `r_c + OpenSpotToleranceCm` of the `WorldPoint` (horizontal) and pass F1 reach; the nearest to the `WorldPoint` wins; an exact tie goes to the lower pawn spawn serial (a value every pawn has; never a kind or team).
4. Otherwise a **whiff** (no victim).

A visible pawn named by an honoured `PawnRef` beats any spot. A culled or forged `PawnRef` is identical to a `TargetSpot` at the same point (IIF R10, IVP R7.4). Hit tests use the capsule, never animated bones.

**ASN-R9. Teammates are never victims, and a teammate strike is never rejected** (resolves IIF OQ4, Concealment's provisional ally rule). The honest client never issues `Attack` at a marked ally (IIF R10). A modified client's forged ally `PawnRef` falls through to rule 2-4 with allies excluded, so it is usually a whiff. There is no friendly fire, no reply code and no presentation difference.

**ASN-R10. One strike, one outcome, decided in the slot.** With the victim V (or none) fixed by R8:

| Condition at the slot | Outcome | Gameplay effects, all inline in the Attack's slot (Round R11, R14) |
|---|---|---|
| no victim | **Whiff** | none beyond the attacker's lockout and pose |
| V braced (R5) | **Reversal** | the attacker A is eliminated: if A is bound, `RequestTransition(A, Informant, ReversedAgainst)`; `OnPawnKilled(Victim = A, Killer = V, VictimKind = ResolveVictimKind(A), Cause = Reversal)`; V's brace is **consumed** (Prepare cancelled, reason `Reversed`) and V enters `Countering` |
| otherwise | **Kill** | if `ResolveVictimKind(V) = Player`: `RequestTransition(V, Informant, Assassinated)`; then `OnPawnKilled(Victim = V, Killer = A, VictimKind, Cause = Strike)` for every kind (`Player`, `Npc`, `Informant`) |

The transition is requested before `OnPawnKilled` is emitted, so Round's elimination check and Penalty's strike count see a consistent Roster. No outcome is deferred to a later item (answers Round OQ8): the contact frame is presentation only. **Nothing observable changes on the victim in the slot** (revised at design review): a concealed victim only leaves Concealment's server-only occupancy record there (CON-R9), so a second strike in the same tick draws from the survivors. Every visible consequence on the victim (fall, `Disabled`, re-opening a culled pawn) happens at the death write (ASN-R21). A victim killed while **concealed** re-opens to every viewer at the death write like any other culled victim, already falling, with `FDeathState.bDiedConcealed = true` written in the same step, so every non-owner client keeps the body presentation-hidden (IVP R7.5, CON-R9; field adopted by user decision 2026-10-25, replacing the earlier interim rule of keeping it relevancy-closed). A victim culled for any other reason re-opens at the death write, already falling, with `bDiedConcealed = false`.

**ASN-R11. Strike accounting is not Assassination's.** Assassination never counts strikes. NPC-Kill Penalty counts one strike for `Cause = Strike ∧ VictimKind = Npc` only, subject to its own further conditions (PEN-R2: an `Active` bound killer, a body outside its release grace): an informant kill (`VictimKind = Informant`) is not an NPC kill, and a reversal never charges the defender anything, even when the reversing defender is an NPC.

**ASN-R12. Running and traversing pawns can be struck.** Speed and parkour are tells, never protection (P2). The F1 reach test applies to the server position, including the vertical limit `StrikeVerticalReachCm`; a climber above it is out of reach (whiff). On a kill the traversal clip stops at the death write and the body falls from its server position (SCL `Disabled`). Because the victim keeps its own motion until the death write (R16), a runner fleeing straight away is reachable only from d0 ≤ ~152 cm (F1): running buys a little distance, never immunity, and is itself the loudest gait tell (creative-director ruling, design review 2026-10-24). The attacker itself cannot strike while running or traversing (IIF R7).

**ASN-R13. Both braced, mutual and chained strikes.**
- **Braced attacker**: `Attack` preempts the attacker's own brace (IIF R9, `Superseded`) at execution, so a braced pawn striking a braced defender is reversed. Bracing never protects a striker.
- **Mutual strike in one drain**: the lower key resolves; the other is voided at its slot because its attacker is no longer `Active` (`NotNow`). Exactly one pawn falls (unless the first was a whiff).
- **Striker struck mid-lunge**: its own outcome was fixed at its execution and stands; it is killed (it is not braced). Both bodies fall.
- **Countering defender struck again**: the counter is not protected (consumed brace, R14), so a second attacker kills it. A pair can trade one life for a braced defender; one attacker cannot.

**ASN-R14. Counter.** On a reversal the defender's slot becomes `Countering` for `CounterDurationSec`: `ActionPose = Counter`, pose-locked (SCL `PoseLocked`), not braced, and every discrete command is `Busy`. Its cooldown keeps running from the original activation. It then returns to `Idle`. An NPC defender counters identically and Crowd resumes it afterwards.

**ASN-R15. Attacker lockout and pose.** Every executed Attack, whatever its outcome, puts the attacker in `Striking` for `AttackAttemptLockoutSec` (1.75 s, F1): `ActionPose = Strike`, Move ignored, pose root motion and warp only, every discrete command `Busy`. The lockout is the probe limiter; there is no extra cooldown on misses (IVP L28: the probe cost is the strike count and the exposure).

**ASN-R16. Strike, warp and contact contract** (presentation timing, F1). The strike clip reaches contact at `StrikeContactSec` after execution and is identical up to contact for every outcome and victim kind (art bible 5.5). One of three entry variants (0°, ±60°) is chosen from the bearing θ to the victim (or the `WorldPoint` for a whiff) relative to body facing: `Strike_F` for |θ| < 30°, otherwise `Strike_L60` or `Strike_R60` by the sign of θ (θ = ±30° exactly takes the side variant); warp rotation is capped at `StrikeWarpYawLimitDeg` (30°, Camera OQ8) and warp translation at F1's bound. **The victim is untouched until the death write** (revised at design review): from the slot to `ContactServerTime − StrikeNetLeadSec` its input, movement and pose are its own, for every outcome, so neither a bystander nor the victim can tell a hit from a whiff by the target's motion. At the death write the victim becomes `Disabled`, brakes by its own gait's baseline braking and starts the fall at `DeathServerTime` (= `ContactServerTime`). A reversing defender is never `Disabled`: it is already planted and enters `Countering` (R14). Clients warp the attacker toward the fixed replicated `WarpPoint` (R21); the server's lunge also warps to that fixed `WarpPoint` and never re-tracks the victim's capsule (no outcome-dependent re-track; ADR-0018 §2.2, E8), and the attacker's residual is reconciled by ordinary movement smoothing. Strike warp targets are set locally on every machine from replicated state; the pawn's motion-warping component does not replicate its warp targets (ADR-0018 §7.2). From a bush, the `BushStrike` variant (its pose start is `FActionState.StartServerTime`, the execution time, a field since the 2026-10-25 decision; the earlier client-side derivation `ContactServerTime − (BushStrikeEmergeSec + StrikeContactSec)` is retained only as the consistency check for ADR-0017 §8 slot A, and `StrikeContactSec` keeps audience `All`) prefaces the same contact segment (CON-R9). **The death write never interrupts the attacker's lunge** (second review 2026-10-25, clarification): for a reversed attacker the write sets `FDeathState` and cancels input only; the server-side warp to the fixed `WarpPoint` runs to `ContactServerTime`, and the body falls from that position, so server and client agree on where the attacker drops (to confirm with SCL and ADR-0018 at implementation).

**D. Bodies, state and feedback**

**ASN-R17. One body sequence for every victim.** A killed pawn (by strike or reversal) plays one fall clip (`DeathFallSec`), lies for `BodyLingerSec`, then is removed by the single shared removal method (Visual/Audio), identically for NPC, player and informant victims (art bible 5.2.1 rule 3). It blocks nothing after the fall (collision off pawn channels, -> ADR). A body killed while concealed is never shown to anyone but its owner: it re-opens at the death write carrying `bDiedConcealed`, and every non-owner client (allies and opponents) keeps it presentation-hidden through fall, linger and removal (R10, CON-R6b, CON-R9). The body is never re-possessed: informant respawn takes over an unseen crowd pawn (decided rule C1), and Crowd emerges the replacement (CNB-R9.2).

**ASN-R18. Defence state lives on the pawn, server-only** (IIF change request 5). A per-pawn component (provisional `UDefenceStateComponent`, not replicated) holds `LastPrepareActivation` and `LockoutEnd` for every pawn, NPC or player. The owner's cooldown end time goes to the owning PlayerController only (IIF R12). Possession rules: rejoin into the same pawn keeps it; a takeover pawn keeps its own stamp (irrelevant while `Informant`); `ReleaseToCrowd` keeps it (Crowd's NPC braces honour it); a `PawnRecovery` re-pawn of an `Active` player copies the lost pawn's stamp, or stamps now if it is unknown (never a free brace).

**ASN-R19. Kill confirmation: what the attacker learns, and when** (P3 feedback clarity, P1). After every executed Attack the attacker's connection (null sink for AI) receives exactly one Owner-only `FStrikeResult {ClientCommandSeq, Result, TallyAfter, bExiled, ContactServerTime}`, enqueued at the end of the resolving drain with every other reply (IIF R6) as an entry of the batched owner-only `ClientCommandReplies` RPC, not a stand-alone RPC (ADR-0011 §8, ADR-0018), and shown by HUD at the local contact frame (ASN-R23). `TallyAfter` (uint8, saturating at 255) and `bExiled` (bool) are filled by NPC-Kill Penalty inside the slot (Penalty CR-2, PEN-R3): the tally rises by one for every `WrongTarget`, whatever the victim kind, and `bExiled` is true only on the kill that triggers the strike-limit removal. The payload has the same shape and size for every `Result`. *(Penalty CR-2; the Owner-only "Wrong targets" tally replacing the concept's live strike counter was confirmed by the user 2026-10-25, Penalty OQ-NKP-1 closed.)* **`ContactServerTime`** (double, the strike's contact server time, the same value as the attacker's own `FActionState.ContactServerTime`; added by user decision 2026-10-25, HUD OQ15 closed) rides in the same payload so HUD shows the plate at contact without matching it to the strike write (HUD F2); for `NoContact` it carries the whiff's nominal contact time, so the shape stays identical for every `Result`.

| `Result` | When | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| `RivalEliminated` | Kill, `VictimKind = Player` | the victim was an Active rival |
| `WrongTarget` | Kill, `VictimKind = Npc` **or** `Informant` (byte-identical payload) | "not an Active rival" (Roster R10 informant-kill invariant) |
| `NoContact` | Whiff (empty spot, ally-only bush, out of reach, already-resolved body) | nothing about any pawn |
| `Reversed` | the attacker was reversed | that the target was braced; **never** the defender's kind |

A bound defender who reverses receives Owner-only `FDefenceResult {Reversed}` in the same drain (the attacker was, necessarily, a rival: NPCs never strike and allies are never victims). Because neither result carries a time and the defender may not hold the attacker's actor, the defence plate is scheduled from the defender's **own** `FActionState` Counter write (its `ContactServerTime`, R21), never on receipt. A victim learns its own elimination through the Roster's Owner-audience state, Camera CS-R12 and HUD; no field names the killer. Nobody else receives any outcome field.

**ASN-R20. Exposure is the price.** Assassination adds no marker, ping, sound cue or UI for observers. What observers learn comes only from the world: the strike pose, the fall, the counter (Visual/Audio), at the line-of-sight of each viewer (Camera, IVP).

**E. Replication of the moment** (ue-replication-specialist; -> ADR, all API details verify in 5.8)

**ASN-R21. One outcome-blind cue at execution, outcome writes at contact.** Two layers, the same for every pawn:
- **At the Attack's slot** only the attacker's pawn changes on the wire: the IIF's replicated action state, extended to one struct `FActionState {Pose, Seq, Variant, StartServerTime, ContactServerTime, WarpPoint}` with a single RepNotify (change request to IIF R12 / SCL R13, replacing a bare `ActionPose`). `StartServerTime` (64-bit, added by user decision 2026-10-25, Concealment CR-10 b) is the server time the pose began, written in every `FActionState` write for every pawn kind (for `Strike` the execution time); it lets a re-created or late-relevant actor seek any clip from server time. `WarpPoint` is always filled and never reads a pawn the attacker cannot know (revised at design review): it is the server's extrapolation of the victim capsule to `ContactServerTime` **only** when the victim was named by an honoured `PawnRef` (rule 1, which already required `CanViewerKnow(Attacker, V, Position)`); for every spot-rule strike, hit or whiff alike, it is derived from the submitted `WorldPoint` alone, clamped by F1's translation bound, and for a bush spot (rule 2) clamped to the footprint edge on the attacker's side. A concealed or culled victim's position is never read into it, so the lunge says nothing about whether a bush or spot was occupied, or where. Accepted tell (P2): after a blind open-spot hit the body may fall up to `r_c + OpenSpotToleranceCm` from the attacker's hands. There is **no target pawn reference** on the wire (a null or unmapped ref would itself distinguish "whiff or culled"). The struct has the same shape and size for a kill, a whiff and a reversal: a custom fixed-width serialiser (server time as a double, `WarpPoint` as a fixed-bit offset from the attacker), because a magnitude-packed vector is a size side channel (→ ADR; Iris serialiser to verify in 5.8).
- **At `ContactServerTime − StrikeNetLeadSec` (the death write)** the server writes the outcome-revealing state in one server step: the victim's (or reversed attacker's) `FDeathState {DeathServerTime, bDiedConcealed}`, `Disabled` on that pawn (R16), the re-open of a culled victim, including one killed while concealed, which carries `bDiedConcealed = true` (R10), and the defender's `FActionState {Pose = Counter, Seq, ContactServerTime}` (the Counter clip starts at `ContactServerTime`, never on receipt). This is a server-side presentation write with no gameplay effect (the outcome was settled in the slot), applied identically to every victim kind, and it is written even after a latch. Pending writes are held in one server-side queue (also used by AC-ASN-43); `Reset` drops them. Clients schedule from their synchronised server clock, `EstimatedServerNow()` (double precision; ADR-0002 §9, read for "GameState server clock") and seek into a clip when late, cross-fading at most 0.1 s; they never snap.
- **Uniform network treatment.** NPC and player pawns share net update frequency, minimum frequency, adaptive-frequency settings and priority. A forced net update is issued on the attacker at the slot and on every pawn written at the death write, identically for every outcome and victim kind (so arrival jitter never encodes kind). No dormancy or priority difference is used for strikes (SCL R13, IIF R12). No replicated effect actor is spawned (IVP L15).
- **Idempotent client presentation.** `FActionState` and `FDeathState` live on different actors, so their arrival order is unspecified: both handlers call one state-derived `RefreshPresentation(ServerNow)`, never edge-triggered logic. Replicated defaults are sentinels (`Seq` starts at 1, `DeathServerTime = −1`), so a late-opened actor that receives both in one bunch presents correctly. The listen-server host does not receive RepNotify for its own writes: it calls the same handler directly.
- **Residuals.** Bystanders (no Owner or Team audience on the strike) may learn kill vs reversal at most `StrikeNetLeadSec` before contact on a modified client (I-ASN-4). The attacker, the target and the team of whichever pawn is eliminated (the victim's team on a kill, the attacker's team on a reversal) receive Owner/Team-audience outcome data at the slot (Roster state, replies, `FStrikeResult`, `FDefenceResult`), so a modified client among them may learn the outcome up to `T_v` early (0.40 s; 0.75 s for `BushStrike`). Both are bounded, carry no kind beyond what those audiences are entitled to, and are accepted (change request: add both to IVP L10). Honest clients present nothing early (ASN-R23).

**ASN-R22. Possession stays put through the fall.** The victim's controller keeps the dying pawn until `DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec`; informant respawn takeover (Penalty, rule C1) and any ownership change happen after it, so nothing about ownership lands inside the visible moment. Possession pointers are owner-only (IVP R5), so the later takeover is invisible to others.

**ASN-R23. Presentation gating: nothing caused by an outcome is shown before contact** (added at design review; closes the pre-contact paths found against ASN-R6). Every client-side consumer of outcome-caused data presents it at the local contact time, never on receipt:
- **Elimination of the local player** (victim or reversed attacker): HUD, Camera (`Dying`, CS-R12), input-context changes and owner audio subscribe to one client-local dispatch, `OnRoleChangePresented`, fired by `RefreshPresentation` at the pawn's own `DeathServerTime` (from `FDeathState`), never to the arrival of the replicated Roster state (change request to Roster and HUD). If `FDeathState` arrives after `DeathServerTime`, the dispatch fires on arrival. Replies to the target's own later commands (`NotNow`) are rate-limited plates and are held to the same time, and so is every other outcome-caused owner datum of the struck pawn, including an `FHoldStatus` cancel of a hold it was in (a tend or hide cancelled by its death): all are presented at `OnRoleChangePresented`, never on receipt (HUD CR-HUD-14, reconciliation 2026-10-24; HUD-R6a implements it).
- **Defender on a reversal:** the server's `FHoldStatus` end for the consumed brace carries `ServerEndTime = ContactServerTime` (existing field); the brace indicator ends at that server time, not on receipt (change request to IIF/HUD). The defence plate (UI-ASN-4) is scheduled from the defender's own Counter write (R19).
- **Attacker:** the plate is shown at the attacker's own `ContactServerTime` (UI-ASN-1). **Matching a result to its strike write** (HUD CR-HUD-14, reconciliation 2026-10-24): `FActionState.Seq` is an All-audience, per-pawn counter that the server advances by one on every `FActionState` write of that pawn, for every pawn kind; it never carries or encodes the owner's `ClientCommandSeq` (a command sequence on an All-audience struct would expose a client's command cadence, which NPC pawns cannot match, a P1 tell). The defined correspondence is positional, not a field: the strike write of an executed `Attack` is the first `FActionState` write with `Pose = Strike` on the pawn that issued the command, observed after the press, whose `Seq` differs from the last consumed write and whose `ContactServerTime` is later than the last consumed contact time. Because `AttackAttemptLockoutSec` exceeds the longest contact delay (I-ASN-3), at most one strike write per pawn is in flight, so the match is unique; the defender's `Counter` write matches its `FDefenceResult` the same way. Since the user decision 2026-10-25 `FStrikeResult` carries `ContactServerTime` (ASN-R19), so HUD F2 shows the attacker's plate from that field; the positional match stays as HUD F2's fallback and is still the rule for the defender's `FDefenceResult`.
- **Allies of the victim** (revised at reconciliation 2026-10-24 to the Teammate Markers ruling, decision log 2026-10-24T09:15:06Z and CD P2): Teammate Markers reads nothing from Assassination at run time. The victim's marker retires with the Roster's C-TeamView retire update at the slot, holds `TeammateMarkerRetireHoldSec` and fades (TM-R6, F6), and the retire update carries the team-only `RetireAtServerTime` (the kill's `ContactServerTime`; user decision 2026-10-25, Roster R6), so the marker is half faded exactly as the blow lands, open or from a bush; the ally removal line (HUD-R10) enters with that retirement. This is an **accepted ally-only exception** to the honest-client rule above (allies are entitled to ally state, IVP R3; not a Pillar-1 leak). No other ally UI changes before contact.
- **Everyone, when the outcome ends the round:** Round's server latch stays in the slot (later items are `Rejected_Latched`), but the replicated `EliminationWin` broadcast and the round-end presentation are staged to the deciding strike's death write (`ContactServerTime − StrikeNetLeadSec`) through the same pending-write queue (change request to Round; `Reset` cannot run inside that window). A bystander client therefore may present the round end up to `StrikeNetLeadSec` (0.15 s) before the strike's `DeathServerTime`: the documented I-ASN-4 residual, matching HUD F10 (cross-review ruling D21, 2026-10-25); the owner-side floors (attacker's plate, victim's fall) are unchanged and never early.
- **Listen-server host:** routes its own HUD and Camera through the same gating (it reads server state directly, so the gate is its only protection against an early cue).

### States and Transitions

Per-pawn **strike role** (server-authoritative; layered on the IIF action slot, which it never contradicts):

| State | Entry | Exit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Ready` | default; cooldown and lockout clear | `Prepare` executes → `Bracing`; `Attack` executes → `Striking` | |
| `Bracing` | `Prepare(Begin)` executes (stamp `LastPrepareActivation`) | End/CapEnd → `Cooling`; reversal → `Countering`; own `Attack` → `Striking` (brace superseded); killed → `Dead`; legality change → `Cooling` | protected (R5) |
| `Countering` | a reversal against this pawn | `CounterDurationSec` elapses → `Cooling` (or `Ready` if the cooldown has expired); killed → `Dead` | pose-locked, **not** protected |
| `Striking` | `Attack` executes (any outcome) | `AttackAttemptLockoutSec` elapses → `Ready`/`Cooling`; killed → `Dead`; reversed → `Dead` | outcome already fixed |
| `Cooling` | brace ended | F3 true → `Ready` | `Prepare` → `Cooldown`; `Attack` legal |
| `Dead` | kill or reversal resolves on this pawn (slot) | body removal (R17) | not a candidate (R8); own motion until the death write, then `Disabled`; fall at `DeathServerTime` (R16, R21) |

`Cooling` and the lockout are independent timers on the pawn component (R18): a pawn can be `Striking` while its brace cooldown runs. Per-Attack lifecycle: `Submitted` → `Resolving` (victim resolution, outcome) → `Executed(Kill | Reversal | Whiff)` or `Voided(NotNow)` or `Rejected_Latched` (Round). Round Event Log rows: `KillResolved{VictimKind, Cause}` and `Reversal{Attacker, Defender}` with the Attack's key.

## Formulas

*systems-designer consulted.* Defaults: `AssassinationRangeCm` R = 200, capsule radius r_c = 35 (SCL, **provisional**), walk 140 cm/s with braking 187 cm/s², run 450 cm/s with braking 600 cm/s² (SCL F2), `TeamSize` 5, `NpcCount` 50, `NpcKillStrikeLimit` 3.

### F1. Reach and warp (reach-versus-animation check)

The strike_reach formula is defined as:

`Reach(A, V) = [ d0 − r_c ≤ R ] ∧ [ |Δz| ≤ Z ] ∧ [ X_eff ≤ X_max ] ∧ [ |θ_T| ≤ Θ ]`, where `d0 = |P_V − P_A|_xy` at the slot; the victim keeps its own velocity until the death write (R16) and brakes after it, so with `t_d = T_v − λ`, `D(v) = v·t_d + B(v)`, `B(v) = v·λ − ½·a_b·λ²` if `λ < v/a_b`, else `v²/(2·a_b)`; `P_V(T_v) = P_V + v̂·D(|v|)`; `X = |P_V(T_v) − P_A|_xy − 2·r_c − g` and `X_eff = max(X, 0)` (a victim at or inside contact distance, including one walking toward the attacker, is struck with zero warp translation: an arm strike in place); `θ_T` is the bearing of `P_V(T_v)` relative to the attacker's body facing at the slot. For a spot-rule strike the same test is applied to each candidate (R8 rules 2-3); `WarpPoint` is still derived from the `WorldPoint` only (R21).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R | float (cm) | 100-250 (hard) | `AssassinationRangeCm` (200) | reach from the attacker's capsule axis to the victim's capsule surface (one radius, matches Concealment F4) |
| r_c | float (cm) | ~35 | SCL (provisional) | capsule radius, identical for every pawn |
| Z | float (cm) | 30-120 | `StrikeVerticalReachCm` (75) | capsule-centre height difference limit |
| T_v | float (s) | 0.25-1.2 | `StrikeContactSec` (0.40), plus `BushStrikeEmergeSec` (0.35) for the `BushStrike` variant | execution-to-contact time of the chosen variant |
| v, a_b | float | v ≤ 450 cm/s | SCL | victim's server velocity at the slot (held until the death write) and its gait's baseline braking (applied after it) |
| λ | float (s) | 0.05-0.30 | `StrikeNetLeadSec` (0.15) | death-write lead before contact; `t_d = T_v − λ` is the victim's free-motion time |
| X_max | float (cm) | 150-240 | `StrikeWarpLimitCm` (240) | maximum warp translation of the lunge (authored root motion about 150 cm × warp scale ≤ 1.6) |
| g | float (cm) | 5-40 | `StrikeContactGapCm` (15) | authored capsule gap at contact |
| Θ | float (°) | 60-105 | `StrikeBearingLimitDeg` (90) | widest bearing a strike can reach: the 60° side variant plus `StrikeWarpYawLimitDeg` (I-ASN-8) |

**Output Range:** boolean. A true result guarantees the visible lunge covers ≤ X_max in T_v (≤ 600 cm/s average at defaults; I-ASN-7 warns above that) and turns at most Θ, so reach never exceeds what the animation shows. `X_eff` is never negative. **Invariant I-ASN-1** (to TDC): `AssassinationRangeCm − r_c − StrikeContactGapCm ≤ StrikeWarpLimitCm` (200 − 35 − 15 = 150 ≤ 240: a standing victim at maximum reach needs a 150 cm lunge). With the default gap and warp limit it caps reach at `StrikeWarpLimitCm + r_c + g` = 240 + 35 + 15 = 290 cm (250 cm at the 200 cm warp-limit safe minimum, 200 cm at the 150 cm hard minimum), so TDC's hard maximum of 300 is not credible: **change request, hard range 100-250**.

**Example** (defaults: T_v 0.40, λ 0.15, t_d 0.25). d0 = 235 cm (R − r_c test: 200 ≤ 200, inclusive with 0.5 cm epsilon). Victim stands: X = 235 − 70 − 15 = 150 → hit. Victim walks straight away (140 cm/s): D = 35 + 18.9 = 53.9, X = 203.9 → hit. Victim runs straight away (450 cm/s): D = 112.5 + 60.75 = 173.25, X = 323.25 > 240 → whiff. Effective reach against a runner fleeing straight away is therefore d0 ≤ 151.75 cm (was 193 when the victim was `Disabled` at execution; accepted at design review, R12). A runner crossing sideways at d0 = 235: |P_V(T)| ≈ 292.0, X ≈ 207.0 → hit. A victim walking toward the attacker at d0 = 100: X = 46.1 − 85 < 0, X_eff = 0 → hit with no warp translation. A victim at θ_T = 100° → whiff (beyond Θ; an honest camera ray reaches at most about 85°: CS-R3 yaw limit 70° + 5° push + the capsule's angular half-width at 2 m).

### F2. Braced at the Attack

The braced_at_attack formula is defined as:

`Braced(V, k_A) = [Key(Begin_V) < k_A] ∧ ¬[Key(End_V) < k_A] ∧ (Slot(V) = Bracing)`, with `Key(End_V) = min(Key(DebounceExpiry_V), Key(CapEnd_V))`; the protected interval is `t_start ≤ t < min(t_endRes + ReleaseDebounceSec, t_start + DefenceDurationSec)` (IIF F2).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| k_A | Round key | — | Round F1 | the Attack's `(ServerTickIndex, Seq)` |
| Begin_V, End_V | queue items | — | IIF R8 | the defender's `Prepare(Begin)` and the item that ends the window |
| `DefenceDurationSec` | float (s) | 3.0-5.0 | TDC (4.0) | window cap |
| `ReleaseDebounceSec` | float (s) | 0.05-0.2 | IIF (0.10) | pending-End grace |
| Slot(V) | enum | IIF R9 | live | `Countering`, `Striking` or `Dead` are never braced |

**Output Range:** boolean, from key order only (no wall-clock comparison inside a drain). **Example:** V begins at key (1000, 4), releases so that `DebounceExpiry` is at (1124, 2). Attack at (1124, 1) → braced (reversal); at (1124, 3) → not braced (kill). Equal-tick order is Round's stamping (F1), never an Assassination tie rule.

### F3. Cooldown, lockout and duty cycle

The defence_cooldown formula is defined as:

`CanPrepare(t) = (T_act = none) ∨ (t − T_act ≥ C)`; `CanAttack(t) = Slot ≠ Striking`, with `Slot = Striking` for `t − t_exec < Λ`; `Duty = (D_cap + dt) / C`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T_act | double (s) | — | R18 component | server time of the last `Prepare(Begin)` execution (`none` at round start) |
| C | float (s) | 30-120 | `DefenceCooldownSec` (60), latch `Action` | cooldown from activation, used or not |
| Λ | float (s) | 1.2-3.0 | `AttackAttemptLockoutSec` (1.75) | attacker lockout after any executed Attack |
| D_cap, dt | float (s) | 3-5; 1/60-1/30 | TDC; server frame | window cap; frame quantisation |

**Output Range:** `Duty` ∈ [0.025, 0.126] (the cap is TDC I-1's, which bounds the window-to-cooldown ratio; the corner C = 30, D_cap = 5 would give 0.168 and is rejected by I-1, not by this formula; dt adds at most 0.001); default (4.0 + 0.033) / 60 = **6.7 %** of time protected at most. Maximum strike rate 1/Λ = 0.57 /s; the IIF Attack bucket (burst 2, 1 /s) never binds because Λ > 1 s; the fastest possible strike-out takes 2Λ = 3.5 s. **Example:** brace tapped at t = 100.0 (window 0.10 s); a re-press at 159.9 is `Cooldown`; at 160.0 it executes (tests allow ± 1 server tick at the boundary).

### F4. Resolution order (in-encounter ordering)

The encounter_order formula is defined as:

`Outcome(Attack_k) = Whiff if V = ∅; Reversal if Braced(V, k); Kill otherwise`, with V = `ResolveVictim(A, WorldPoint, PawnRef, state after every item with key < k)`; every effect executes in slot k (Round R14), and candidates exclude pawns whose strike role is `Dead`, including those whose death write is still pending (R8; a dying NPC is not re-killable, closing the gap where `ResolveVictimKind` still answers `Npc` for an unbound body).

**Variables:** `k` (Round key), `A` (attacker, `Active` at slot k else `Voided(NotNow)`), `V` (victim from R8), `Braced` (F2). **Output Range:** exactly one of {Whiff, Reversal, Kill, Voided, Rejected_Latched} per Attack; at most one pawn eliminated per Attack. **Example:** A1 (key …,3) and A2 (key …,7) strike B in one drain: A1 kills B; A2's `PawnRef` names a `Dead` pawn → terminal Whiff (`NoContact`), even if an NPC stands beside B (R8 rule 1). A2 by `TargetSpot` at B: B is not a candidate, so only another live pawn within the open-spot radius could be struck (an ordinary spot strike).

### F5. Blind-guess economics (expected cost of a probe)

The probe_economics formula is defined as:

`q = p(1 − b_P)`, `ρ = p·b_P + (1 − p)·b_N`, `m = (1 − p)(1 − b_N)`, `π = m / (m + ρ)`; per active life: `E[N] = Σ_{i=0}^{L−1} π^i`, `E[RivalKills] = E[N] · q / (1 − q)`, `P(StrikeOut) = π^L`, `P(Reversed) = 1 − π^L`, `P(≥1 kill) = 1 − [ρ·Σ_{i<L} m^i + m^L]`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p | float | 0.01-0.5 | `TeamSize / (TeamSize + NpcCount)` (TDC) | share of non-ally pawns that are Active rivals |
| b_P | float | 0-0.125 | ≤ F3 `Duty` (0.067) | upper bound on a rival's braced share |
| b_N | float | 0-0.015 | Crowd F2 T4 (0.020 braces/min × ≤ 4.05 s ÷ 60 ≤ 0.00135 at defaults; ≤ 0.0135 at Crowd's shortest brace interval) | NPC braced share, unconditional (startle braces cluster after a visible strike, so a second strike near a first is reversed more often than this average) |
| L | int | 2-5 (hard, TDC; safe 2-4; 1 only in a test preset with `bTestAllowsStrikeLimitOne`) | `NpcKillStrikeLimit` (3) | strikes to removal |

**Output Range:** probabilities in [0, 1]. **Example (defaults, uniform blind strikes; `NpcCount` 50 since 2026-10-25):** q = 0.085, ρ = 0.0073, m = 0.908, π = 0.992; E[N] = 2.98 strikes, **E[RivalKills] = 0.276**, P(strike-out) = 0.976, P(reversed) = 0.024, **P(≥1 kill) = 0.232** (about 10.7 NPC kills per rival kill). TDC's `BlindGuessSuccess` (0.249) and `ExpectedBlindKills` (0.300) ignore reversals and are about 7-9 % optimistic: the safe direction for TDC I-4 (note requested). The brace deters deliberate hunters, not guessers (a blind probe is reversed < 1 % of the time); the probe's real price is the strike count plus the visible strike (IVP L28). No cooldown on misses is added.

## Edge Cases

*systems-designer consulted.* Everything below resolves in Round's single queue order (key, never wall-clock); Assassination adds no tie rule of its own. ASN-R13 already covers both-braced, mutual, chained and countering-defender strikes.

**Same-drain ordering**
- **If a strike and the victim's `Prepare(Begin)` share a tick:** key order decides. Begin first: the victim is `Bracing` at the slot and the strike is a reversal. Strike first: kill; the Begin is then voided `NotNow` (the victim is no longer `Active`) and writes no `LastPrepareActivation` stamp (F2, ASN-R5).
- **If a strike and the defender's `HoldTimer(CapEnd)` or `HoldTimer(DebounceExpiry)` share a tick:** the lower key wins (F2 example). The window-ending item first gives a kill; the Attack first gives a reversal. A pending End still inside its debounce protects (ASN-R5).
- **If a strike and an objective completion share a tick:** completion first latches the round; the strike is `Rejected_Latched` (owner reply `NotNow`): no outcome, no lockout, no `FStrikeResult`, no pose. Strike first: it executes fully and the completion then resolves under Round's rules. A strike that executed before the latch still gets its contact-time presentation write after it (ASN-R21).
- **If a kill eliminates a team's last `Active` player:** the `RequestTransition` triggers Round's elimination check inline in the slot (ASN-R10), the attacker's team wins by `EliminationWin`, and later items in the drain are `Rejected_Latched`. The fall and body sequence still play.
- **If a reversal eliminates the attacker team's last `Active` player:** `EliminationWin` goes to the other team (Roster F, Round F2), including when the reversing defender is an NPC: the trigger reads team counts, not who reversed.
- **If a kill is the attacker's strike-limit NPC kill and the attacker is its team's last `Active` player:** Penalty's `StrikeLimit` transition runs inline after `OnPawnKilled` and Round's check then eliminates the attacker's team. The NPC body still falls and the plate still reads `WrongTarget`.

**Targets**
- **If the target is a dead or dying body (NPC or player):** strike role `Dead` (including a pending death write) and `NotTargetable` pawns are excluded from candidates (R8, F4, Roster R10). A `PawnRef` naming the body is a terminal whiff (it never falls through to a spot rule and so never kills a bystander beside the body); a `TargetSpot` at the body strikes only another live candidate inside the open-spot radius, as any spot strike would. Struck at the body itself, the strike is a whiff with `NoContact`, byte-identical for an NPC body and a player body, and no strike is charged. This closes the gap where `ResolveVictimKind` still answers `Npc` for an unbound body, and answers Roster OQ9.
- **If the victim is an informant:** a kill with `VictimKind = Informant`; no `RequestTransition` (already `Informant`), `OnPawnKilled(…, Cause = Strike)`, plate `WrongTarget` byte-identical to an NPC kill (ASN-R19), not a strike (ASN-R11), and the same fall and removal (ASN-R17). An informant can never brace, so it is never a reversal. Penalty respawns it (decided rule C1).
- **If the named or nearest pawn is an ally:** allies are not candidates (ASN-R9); resolution continues to the next rule with allies excluded, otherwise `NoContact`. An ally never shields a rival standing behind it, and no reply differs.
- **If the target is concealed:** its `PawnRef` fails `CanViewerKnow` and the strike resolves as a `TargetSpot` at the same point (IIF R10, IVP R7.4). A `WorldPoint` in a spot zone resolves by rule 2 only, which is terminal: an empty or ally-only bush is a whiff (ASN-R8).
- **If the target is `Entering` before its commit, or `Exiting` before its leaf crossing:** before the commit it is an ordinary visible pawn and its `PawnRef` is honoured; while `Exiting` it is still a zone candidate and is struck although already un-culled (CON-R12).
- **If the target is above `StrikeVerticalReachCm` (a climber):** F1's `|Δz| ≤ Z` fails. A named pawn out of reach is a whiff (ASN-R8.1); a spot strike may still find another candidate in reach.
- **If the victim acts between the slot and the death write:** it moves under its own input (R16), and its discrete commands resolve under its new Roster state (IIF R7: an informant's legality; a struck rival's `Attack` and `Prepare` are `NotNow`), with the replies held until its contact time (ASN-R23). Any action or traversal it is in at the death write is cancelled there, and the fall starts from its server position.
- **If the victim is `Countering` or `Striking`:** never braced (F2), so it is killed; a striking victim's own strike outcome stands (ASN-R13).
- **If two pawns are exactly equidistant from an open-spot `WorldPoint`:** the lower pawn spawn serial wins (ASN-R8.3), a value every pawn has; never a kind or team.

**Attacker legality**
- **If the attacker is an informant at submission:** IIF R7 rejects `NotNow`. If it became an informant earlier in the same drain (killed, reversed elsewhere or struck out), the Attack is `Voided(NotNow)` at its slot (ASN-R2). Either way: no lockout, no pose, no `FStrikeResult`.
- **If the attacker strikes from inside a bush:** the `BushStrike` variant plays and concealment breaks at execution (CON-R9); F1 uses `T_v = StrikeContactSec + BushStrikeEmergeSec`. A whiff from a bush still exposes the attacker (P3).
- **If the attacker disconnects or is handed to AI hold mid-lockout:** `Striking` and `LockoutEnd` live on the pawn component (ASN-R18), so the lockout runs out normally; the result goes to the null sink. `ReleaseToCrowd` keeps the timer.
- **If the attacker is braced:** the brace is superseded at execution (ASN-R13); bracing never protects a striker.
- **If the attacker spams Attack during the lockout:** each press is rejected `Busy` (IIF R9); the bucket (burst 2, 1 /s) never binds before the lockout (F3).

**Defender**
- **If an NPC defender reverses:** same path (ASN-R7); nothing is charged to anyone (ASN-R11); no `FDefenceResult` (null sink); Crowd resumes the NPC after `Countering`.
- **If a braced pawn disconnects:** AI hold sends no End, so `HoldTimer(CapEnd)` closes the window at `DefenceDurationSec`; it stays protected until then. `ReleaseToCrowd` mid-brace behaves the same way.
- **If the pawn is re-pawned, rejoins or is released to the crowd:** ASN-R18 governs; the cooldown is never reset by a possession change.
- **If the round latches mid-brace:** later Attacks are `Rejected_Latched` and the hold timers resolve without effect.
- **If `Reset` runs:** every defence component is cleared (`LastPrepareActivation = none`, no lockout), strike roles return to `Ready`, pending contact-time writes (including a staged round-end broadcast, which Round guarantees cannot be pending at `Reset`) are dropped, and bodies are removed with their pawns.

**Bodies**
- **If a crowd pawn walks into a body:** the body blocks no pawn channel after the fall (ASN-R17), so no pawn can be trapped; Crowd may still steer around it as a static avoidance obstacle and use it as a glance attractor (CNB-R8), identically for every victim kind.
- **If a body is killed-concealed:** it re-opens at the death write with `bDiedConcealed = true`, and every non-owner client keeps it presentation-hidden through fall, linger and removal (ASN-R10, R17, CON-R6b, CON-R9; user decision 2026-10-25).

**Presentation and network**
- **If the victim is culled for some viewers at the kill for a reason other than concealment:** it re-opens at the death write, never in the slot (ASN-R10, R21; change request to IVP R7.5), and opens already falling at `DeathServerTime`, identically for every victim kind. A concealed victim is never re-opened (previous bullet).
- **If a viewer receives a fresh actor or joins mid-fall:** the client seeks into the fall from its synchronised server clock (`EstimatedServerNow()`) (cross-fade ≤ 0.1 s, never a snap), using the sentinel defaults of ASN-R21 to tell "no death yet" from a death; past `DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec + BodyLingerSec` the actor is never shown.
- **If the strike is outside a viewer's relevancy:** that viewer sees nothing; `FActionState` has the same shape for every outcome, so its arrival leaks nothing (ASN-R21).
- **On the listen-server host:** the host's presentation schedules from `ContactServerTime` through the same gating as a client (ASN-R23), so in server time its screen shows the outcome no earlier than any client's (in wall-clock it shows contact first, by the clients' one-way latency). The host receives no RepNotify for its own writes and calls the same handler directly. (The host process holds the server state; host trust is IVP's accepted listen-server risk, not Assassination's.)
- **If a modified client reads the wire:** as a bystander it can learn kill versus reversal at most `StrikeNetLeadSec` before contact (I-ASN-4); as the attacker, the target or the target's ally it can learn the outcome up to `T_v` early from Owner/Team data. Both are bounded and accepted (ASN-R21; IVP L10 change request); honest clients present nothing early (ASN-R23).

**Degenerate strategies (and the designed answer)**
- **Strike spam / blind probing:** capped at 1/Λ = 0.57 strikes/s (F3). F5: E[rival kills] = 0.28 per active life against about 10.7 visible NPC kills per rival kill (`NpcCount` 50); the price is the strike count plus exposure (IVP L28). No miss cooldown is added.
- **Brace baiting:** at most 6.7 % of time protected (F3, TDC I-1), planted and visible (P2), and every use burns 60 s. It deters deliberate hunters, not guessers (F5).
- **Pair trading a braced defender:** A1 is reversed, A2 then kills the unprotected defender (ASN-R13). Accepted: the pair pays one life, and the defender is unprotected for the rest of its cooldown, not just the counter.
- **Bracing at an objective:** the action slot is exclusive (IIF R9), so a braced pawn makes no progress; a planted 4 s stance at a site is itself a tell.
- **Killing informants to deny information:** strike-free, but the plate is `WrongTarget`, identical to an NPC kill, so the hunter cannot confirm it and pays the same exposure. The informant respawns (Penalty). The count side is resolved by Penalty PEN-R14: the attacker sees only the "Wrong targets" tally, which ticks identically for both, and the true strike count is server-only (OQ-ASN-2, closed; confirmed by the user 2026-10-25).

**Tuning extremes**
- **If a preset violates I-ASN-1 (for example range 250, `StrikeWarpLimitCm` 150, gap 5):** TDC rejects the whole preset (CR-6). Otherwise the server would kill from farther than the lunge visibly travels, a P2 defect.
- **If any of I-ASN-2 to I-ASN-5 or I-ASN-8 fails:** the preset is rejected (Tuning Knobs); I-ASN-5w, I-ASN-6, I-ASN-7, W-ASN-10 and W-ASN-11 only warn.

**VA-ASN-2 The one removal method: cut at occlusion.** From `DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec + BodyLingerSec`, each client hides the body on the first frame its bounds are outside that viewer's frustum or fully hidden by world geometry (pawns are not occluders). The hide covers mesh, hair, garments, props, shadow and reflections with Concealment VA-4's flags (art bible 5.2.1 rule 1 applied to bodies). If a viewer is still looking when `BodyRemovalGraceSec` runs out, the body is cut in a single frame: no fade, dissolve, sink, flash or effect. The rule reads only geometry and time, so even a seen cut reveals nothing about identity; watching a body for 8 s is itself loitering (5.5). A client that first receives the actor after the linger never shows it. The server destroys the actor when the grace runs out.

**VA-ASN-3 Reversal readability.** A bystander tells a kill from a reversal only by who falls and who stays standing; the attacker's clip up to contact, the (absent) bystander grade, the sound and the timing are identical. Tests:
- **(a) Who fell** (AC-ASN-30): 20 m, greyscale, baseline crowd, 3 s clips around contact, filmed from in front of, beside and behind the striker. Pass: ≥ 85 % of trained testers name the faller ("the striker" or "the one struck") within 2 s, for kills and reversals alike.
- **(b) Brace:** the 5.5 tell test; passes at default and fails at 2× compression.
- **(c) Pre-contact parity** (automated, AC-ASN-21): same variant and `WarpPoint`, attacker bones up to contact differ by ≤ 0.5 cm between a kill, a reversal and a whiff.

| Brace press | Plain and immediate button feel; the stance is the weight. "Pending" emphasis next frame; Protected only on server confirmation; planted from the first frame | indicator ≤ 17 ms; protected at confirmation (~100 ms); `Brace_In` 0.25 s |
| Reversal (defender) | Block on the contact frame, blow at +0.15 s, back to baseline stance when `Countering` ends; ends planted rather than posing | counter visible 0.80 s; `Countering` 1.2 s from execution |
| Reversal (bystander) | Loud by readability, not by effects: the striker falls, the braced one stands | 20 m greyscale ≥ 85 % (VA-ASN-3) |

**What it must never feel like:** a parry (no window to hit, no reward for brace timing, no flash on a "perfect" brace), a QTE, an aim test (no lock-on, reticle, snap or magnetism; a blind strike is a choice of bush, not a point), a combat exchange (no health, no second hit, no stagger), or a cinematic (no kill cam, slow motion or cut).

**Mastery:** the skill expressed is reading an approach and choosing the moment to commit, both as attacker (close to arm's length without reading as a hunter) and as defender (brace before a committed striker is in reach). Execution difficulty is deliberately near zero.
