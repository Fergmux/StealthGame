## Summary

Round & Match Framework runs each round of Smoking Mirror on the server: it forms the two teams of up to five, walks the round through `Lobby`, `Preparing`, `Active`, `Ended` and `Reset`, and decides the one winner, by the shared final objective or by eliminating every active rival. It owns the single resolution queue that orders every simultaneous event, which is why a tie or draw cannot happen, and the consequences of a disconnect, the server-only Round Event Log, the round counter and score, and the host's start and reset commands. It exists so that every round starts and ends for everyone at the same instant with exactly one result, with no clock and no enemy head-count, and without leaking which bodies in the crowd are people (Pillar 1, Pillar 4).

> **Quick reference** -- Layer: `Core` · Priority: `MVP` · Key deps: `Participant State & Roster, Session & Travel, Tuning Data & Config (upstream); Level Socket Contract, Character Pool & Appearance, Input & Interaction Framework (called); dependents: Information Visibility Policy, Assassination, NPC-Kill Penalty & Informants, Objectives Chain, HUD, Lobby UI, Post-Round Reveal, Telemetry, Dedicated Server`

## Overview

Round & Match Framework is the server-side system that turns ten connected people into a round and a round into exactly one result. It owns five things. (1) **Teams of up to five**: the size, balance and minimum-to-start rules; the Roster holds the data and Session takes the join requests. (2) **Five phases**, `Lobby`, `Preparing`, `Active`, `Ended` and `Reset`, with a stated precondition for every transition: `Preparing` waits for the frozen tuning (`OnTuningReady`), the layout (`OnLayoutReady(LayoutId, Seed)` from the Level Socket Contract; Round never depends on the Generator), the appearance deal, and every connected client's readiness, then places and spawns players mixed into the NPC crowd in one frame. (3) **The single resolution queue**: every command, objective completion and timer that can interact with another is stamped with one total-order key (server tick, then a monotonically increasing sequence id) and resolved serially, so two simultaneous events always have a first and a second. (4) **The win conditions**: `ObjectiveWin(team)` when the shared final objective completes and `EliminationWin(team)` when a team's Active count reaches zero, latched once, so a tie or a draw cannot exist; there is no match time limit and Round adds no timer or reveal that could end or steer a round. (5) **The consequences of disconnecting**: an absent player's pawn keeps walking as part of the crowd for a grace period, then the player becomes an informant, and the pawn joins the crowd for good. Round also keeps the **server-only Round Event Log** (the record of why every round ended, released to clients only in `Ended` and, in the MVP, only as a bare result), the round counter and match score, the host's start and reset commands, and the rule for what `Reset` clears.

Players never see Round; they feel what it guarantees: a round that begins for all ten at the same instant, ends for all ten at the same instant with one winning team and a stated reason, never shows a clock or a head-count of rivals, and never penalises a dropped connection more than the loss of an active role. Without Round, each feature would order its own simultaneous events (two knives, a final objective and a last kill) and disagree, and the distinction between a player and a crowd member could leak through round-start placement, spawn order or the timing of a disconnect.

*Framing (autonomous, logged)*: both the data/infrastructure layer and the player effect (Core category, player-facing consequences); no ADR exists yet, so implementation choices are marked "-> ADR"; the fantasy is indirect.

## Player Fantasy

*Framing: indirect. Players feel what the framework guarantees, not the framework. Shaped by creative-director: framing A "One Breath, One Verdict" with the line "no clock, only the other team" from framing B; the seat-keeping fantasy already belongs to Participant State & Roster and Session & Travel and is only referred to here.*

**One breath, one verdict.** The screen lifts for all ten of you on the same breath, onto a city already crowded, where nobody has taken a step before you. From then on the only clock is the other team. There is no timer to watch and no count of how many rivals are left, so safety is never something you can read off a number: the round ends because someone *did* something, either finished the race or finished the last rival. When it ends, it ends for everyone in the same instant. If a knife and a final completion arrive together, one of them was first, cleanly, and the city gives exactly one answer, never "nearly" and never a draw. The result tells you who won and how: "completed the final objective" or "every rival eliminated for the round". If you drop out mid-round the crowd does not flinch: your pawn keeps walking like everyone else's, and when your active place is eventually lost you are still at the table.

Anchor moments, each mapped to an acceptance criterion below: (1) the loading screen lifts for all participants at one scheduled server time (`ActiveAtServerTime`, R7), within the clock-sync error, and never before the client holds every spawned pawn or `RevealPawnWaitMaxSec` has passed (R7); (2) a final completion and a last elimination in the same tick produce exactly one result; (3) no clock, countdown or enemy count is ever shown before `Ended`; (4) the round ends for all clients at once, with the reason; (5) a player who disconnects is, to every non-teammate, indistinguishable from a crowd member throughout grace and after.

*Pillars served*: P4 A Race, Not a Hunt ("never a timer, never a reveal"; the log's `EndReason` measures it); P1 Hide in Plain Sight (round start, spawn order and the disconnect takeover show no player/NPC difference); P3 One Strike, Lasting Consequence (a single decisive ordering of simultaneous events; a disconnect costs the active role, not the seat). *Language rule for player-facing text derived from this system*: never "draw", "tie", "stalemate", "timer", "time left", "overtime", "sudden death", "match point", "N enemies left", "x/5 alive", "last one standing", "bot", "AI took over", "AFK", "abandoned", "game over" (the session continues); plus the Roster and Session lists. Approved phrasings: "completed the final objective"; "every rival eliminated for the round"; "The round was called off". Say "round", "city" and "race"; not "match timer" or "map". *(creative-director consulted: framing A with B's line.)*

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

*Specialists consulted (full review mode): game-designer (phases, preconditions, disconnect fairness, host commands), systems-designer (queue, latch, tie impossibility), ue-replication-specialist (public state, intake, drain placement, log exposure), creative-director (fantasy). Findings adopted are noted inline as "(specialist)". Engine statements are marked [V] verified in installed 5.8.4 source by the specialist, [S] needs a spike, [NS] not sourceable from `docs/engine-reference/unreal/`.*

**A. Scope and authority**

**R1. What Round owns, and what it does not.** Round owns: the five round phases and every transition's preconditions; the team rules (size, balance, minimum to start); the **single server-side resolution queue** and its total-order key; the win conditions and the win latch; the disconnect policy (what a connection event means for the round); the server-only **Round Event Log**; the round counter and match-scoped data; the start/reset commands; and what resets in `Reset`. Round does **not** own: participant data (Roster), connections and travel (Session & Travel), tuning values (TDC), layout and sockets (Level Socket Contract), appearance deals (Character Pool & Appearance), the meaning of a command (Input & Interaction Framework and the owning feature), strikes (NPC-Kill Penalty & Informants), objective stages and **stall pressure** (Objectives Chain). Round never references the Procedural City Generator: it waits for `OnLayoutReady(LayoutId, Seed)` from the Level Socket Contract.

**R2. Server-authoritative; no client surface.** All Round logic runs on the server game thread. Round adds **no client-to-server RPC**: commands reach the queue through the IIF's RPCs (derived from the sending connection's controller), readiness comes through Session's and TDC's acknowledgements, and the host commands (R22) are local console/UI executions on the authority. The only client-visible Round data is `FRoundPublicState` (R20). Clients never supply a phase, a team, a result or a participant id.

**B. Teams**

**R3. Teams of up to five.** There are exactly two teams (`0`, `1`), each holding at most `TeamSize` participants (default 5, so 10 per match; capacity `2 x TeamSize`). Round owns the rules; the Roster owns the data (`AssignTeams`, `SetTeamsLocked`); Session owns join requests.
- **Assignment in Lobby only.** When `OnParticipantRegistered` fires for a **new** key in Lobby, Round assigns the participant to the team with fewer rostered members via one atomic `AssignTeams`; on a tie the favoured team alternates by round (`RoundsPlayedCount mod 2`), so an odd head-count does not always enlarge team 0 (specialist). A returning known key (`OnParticipantReclaimed`, a Dormant record) keeps the team it had: the Roster record is match-scoped, so teams do not drift when someone crashes and returns (specialist). At `RoundsPlayedCount = 0` the tie favours team 0 (so the first registrant, normally the host, is on team 0). The host may move one participant with `SwapTeam(Id)` (a single move, refused only if the target team already holds `TeamSize`) or exchange two (a batch validated against the final state, so a 5/5 swap succeeds). Any team layout is legal while in Lobby; F3 is checked only at `StartRound`. Teams are match-scoped: they persist across rounds and change only in Lobby. **Teams are never rebalanced after the lock**: a disconnect mid-round does not move anyone, and a `Dormant` record does not free a slot until the host removes it.
- **Start rules.** A round may start only when F3 holds: every participant has a team, each team has at least `MinPlayersPerTeamCount` (1) participants, `abs(n0 - n1) <= MaxTeamImbalanceCount` (1), and no team exceeds `TeamSize`. Fewer than 10 participants is legal (2v2, 4v3, 5v4). With `bAllowEmptyTeamDev` a 1v0 dev round may start; it can end only by `ObjectiveWin` because an empty team is never eliminated (Roster F4).
- **`TeamSize`: a TDC key, Round's rule (review 2026-10-24).** The value lives in TDC's canonical table (owner column: Round, latch `Round`); the *rule* (cap, balance, minimum, F3) is Round's. Round reads the lobby-time value through `GetServerTuning()` and **injects** it into the Roster: `BeginMatch(TeamSize)` once when the match is created, and `SetTeamSize(TeamSize)` in Lobby whenever `stealth.round.preset` changes it. A `Rejected_TeamSizeBelowRoster` refusal keeps the previous preset and shows the host the refusal; teams must be reassigned first. Round never changes `TeamSize` while teams are locked. Cross-field invariant (TDC): `MinPlayersPerTeamCount <= TeamSize`.
- **No auto-scaling.** Round does not change `NpcCount` with the participant count (TDC CR-5: one value set per round for everyone). A smaller test match uses a preset (`Solo`, `Playtest`) with its own `NpcCount`; TDC invariant I-2 (`NpcCount >= 2 x TeamSize`) still holds.
- **Teams are not secret from your own side, and Round never replicates one team's membership to the other** (Roster R6). The lobby shows a viewer their own team only.

**C. Phases and their transitions** (the table is under States and Transitions)

**R4. Five phases, one machine.** `Lobby`, `Preparing`, `Active`, `Ended`, `Reset`. Round's phase machine and its match-scoped data live in a server object scoped to the game instance (-> ADR, the same container pattern as the Roster store) so they survive the seamless travel that recreates GameMode and GameState; `FRoundPublicState` is rebuilt from it on every map. `Lobby` and `Reset` run on the lobby map (which may equal the match map, Session rule 12); `Preparing`, `Active` and `Ended` run on the match map. Round pushes the admission mode on every phase change (Session rule 20): Lobby `Open`; Preparing, Active, Ended `RejoinOnly`; Reset `Closed`.

**R5. `StartRound` (Lobby to Preparing).** Triggered only by the host command (R22). The call returns `FStartRefusal {Code, ParticipantIds}` on refusal, shown to the host only (console line or host panel; clients never see it), and changes nothing. Checked in this order; the first failure decides:
1. `NotHost`: the caller is not the host's local controller.
2. `WrongPhase` / `TravelInProgress`: the phase is not `Lobby`, or a travel or an earlier start is in progress.
3. `ParticipantNotConnected` (retryable; lists the ids): any rostered participant is `Unresponsive`, `Connecting` or `Dormant`. A `Dormant` participant is **never pruned automatically** (specialist): the host removes them explicitly with `stealth.round.remove <participant>` (`RemoveParticipant`, legal while teams are unlocked), or waits for them to return; an accidental alt-tab must not silently cost a friend their seat.
4. `TeamsInvalid(TooSmall | Imbalance | Oversize)`: F3 fails. A participant without a team is auto-assigned first by R3, so an unassigned participant can never reach this check (asserted; there is no `Unassigned` sub-code).
5. `PresetInvalid` / `DeckInfeasible`: **best-effort pre-checks** against a TDC dry-run of the requested preset (provisional TDC interface `PreviewPreset(PresetName)`; if the dry-run is unavailable the checks are skipped with a log warning and the Preparing ladder catches the same faults later): the preset resolves to a valid set, and `D = participants + NpcCount` satisfies Character Pool's deck floor (`D >= DeckCopiesFloorCount x DeckActiveAppearancesMinCount`, 24 at defaults; TDC I-3). This spares ten people a wasted travel.

Effects, in order, on acceptance (**decided cross-GDD rule C2: admission lock -> `ResetAll(RoundIndex)` -> travel -> `Preparing`**; review 2026-10-24): (5b) `CanRequestRoundTravel()` (provisional Session query with no side effects; a `false` refuses with `SessionRefused` before anything changes); (6) **admission lock**: `SetAdmissionMode(RejoinOnly)` and `SetTeamsLocked(true)`; (7) `ResetAll(RoundIndex)` with `RoundIndex = RoundsPlayedCount + 1` (this is **the** once-per-round roster reset; it runs here and not on `Preparing` entry because Session rule 11.2 requires the Roster round scope to be reset **before** `RequestRoundTravel`, and because a participant registered in Lobby starts as `RoundEnded` and must be `Active` before the round); (8) `RequestRoundTravel(MapId, PresetId, RoundIndex)`; Session draws a **fresh seed for every start** (an aborted start never reuses a seed); (9) **only when Session accepts the travel**, set phase `Preparing`. **Rollback** if Session still refuses at step 8: admission `Open`, `SetTeamsLocked(false)`, phase stays `Lobby`, log `StartRolledBack`, report `FStartRefusal(SessionRefused)`. The reset round scope is left as it is: `Active` in Lobby means only "will compete" (Roster R7), I4 and F4 apply only in phase `Active`, and the next start calls `ResetAll` again. **Repeat index:** after an aborted start or a rollback, `RoundsPlayedCount` has not advanced, so the next start calls `ResetAll(N+1)` again with the same index; `RoundEpoch` advances again (only the epoch is used for staleness) and the Roster re-records the pair (change request: Roster states that `ResetAll` is repeatable per index). **The lobby has no gameplay pawn**: participants see a lobby camera or UI only (Lobby UI is Vertical Slice; the MVP lobby is a blank view driven by host commands).

**R6. `Preparing`: the readiness sequence.** Preparing begins when travel is accepted and spans the load. It ends in `Active` or in an **abort to Lobby** (R8). The ordered steps, each with its owner of the wait:

| Step | Round waits for | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| P1 params | the new map's game mode has parsed `FRoundParams` (Session rule 11.4) | `OnTravelFailed(BadParams)`: abort `BadParams` |
| P2 tuning | Round signals "Preparing entered"; TDC freezes and raises `OnTuningReady(TuningHash)` | TDC `Failed`: abort `TuningLoadFailed` |
| P3 start work | on `OnTuningReady`: Round writes the Event Log header, then in parallel calls `RequestLayout(Seed, TuningHash)` and `AllocateAppearances(Players, NpcCount)` (once; `Players` = every rostered `ParticipantId`, connected or not) | `DeckInvalid` or `Error_DeckTooSmall`: abort `DeckInvalid`. A second call returns `Error_AlreadyDealt`: a bug, abort `AllocatorMisuse` |
| P4 layout | `OnLayoutReady(LayoutId, Seed)` with `Seed` equal to `FRoundParams.Seed` | `OnLayoutAborted`: abort `LayoutAborted`. Seed mismatch: abort `LayoutSeedMismatch` |
| P5 barrier | every participant that is `Connected` has delivered **Session's single round acknowledgement** (`OnParticipantTravelComplete`), which carries the client-recomputed `ClientHash` (tuning, decided rule C3) and `PoolHash` (Character Pool CP-18). There is no separate TDC or pool acknowledgement and no `TuningAckTimeoutSec`/`PoolResidentTimeoutSec` wait (both retired, review 2026-10-24). The only per-client bound is Session's F7 `Deadline`; a participant that misses it becomes `Disconnected(TravelTimeout)`, leaves the wait set and is a normal disconnect (R17). There is no quorum abort: a client whose `ClientHash` or `PoolHash` still mismatches after the resend is disconnected `ParamsMismatch` by Session and becomes a normal disconnect, however many do (TDC CR-9: TDC never aborts on client reports); `TuningSnapshotFault` comes only from TDC's server self-check, at P2. Travel-complete comes from the seamless-travel arrival or loaded-world notification, never from a PlayerState existing (PlayerStates are carried through travel) [V]; the acknowledgement is keyed by `ParticipantId` and this Preparing's nonce, and stale acks from a previous round or a replaced controller are dropped. A participant who **rejoins before P6** re-enters the wait set and must acknowledge; one who rejoins after P6 takes the R17.3 path | abort as listed; no wait of Round's own other than P5's end |
| P5b gate | `>= MinPlayersPerTeamCount` participants **Connected** on each team after the barrier | abort `TeamAbsent` |
| P6 placement and spawn | Round calls `RequestStartPlacement(PlayersByTeam, NpcCount)` once, builds one spawn list of `D = players + NpcCount` entries, shuffles it with the server sub-seed `GetServerSubSeed(SpawnOrder, LayoutAttempt)` (F5), then spawns all `D` pawns **through one factory in one frame**: Shared Character & Locomotion's `SpawnSharedPawn` (the one "named spawn site" of Roster R9, used for every pawn creation, NPC included); NPC entries via Crowd, player entries by deferred spawn, `BindPawn`, finish spawning, possess. The `AssignedAppearanceId` of each participant is read from the allocator's `ParticipantToAppearance` map and copied to the Roster field; `AppearanceId` is on the pawn before it first replicates (Character Pool CP-10). The shuffle protects the server's spawn and iteration order only; **arrival order at clients is decided by net priority, not spawn order**, which is why P7 exists | If `BindPawn` is rejected the pawn is destroyed and the round aborts `BindFailed` (Roster I4 would otherwise break). If a placement invariant fails (F5 zone share) abort `PlacementInvariant`; if the Level Socket Contract returns `PlacementInfeasible` or `NpcCountTooLow`, abort with that code and return to `Lobby` |
| P7 world-ready and scheduled start | pawns replicate during Preparing (relevant to every `Connected` client; the spawn gate below still applies). `FRoundPublicState.ExpectedPawnCount = D` is published. Each `Connected` client reports **world-ready** (it holds `D` pawns with a resolved `AppearanceId`) through the second stage of Session's round acknowledgement (`ServerWorldReady`, Session rule 11; Round CR-13 (d), adopted by user decision 2026-10-25), which Session forwards as `OnParticipantWorldReady`. Round waits until every `Connected` client is world-ready or `WorldReadyTimeoutSec` (10) passes, then sets `ActiveAtServerTime = ServerTime + StartLeadSec` (0.25 s) in `FRoundPublicState` | a client not world-ready at the timeout is **not** disconnected: the start proceeds and that client's loading screen stays up until R7's reveal rule releases it (`D` pawns held, or `RevealPawnWaitMaxSec` after `ActiveAtServerTime`; the cost of a slow machine falls on that machine only) |

Rules for the whole phase:
- **Every Active participant gets a bound pawn, connected or not.** A participant that is `Disconnected` at P6 (a load timeout, a crash during travel) still gets a pawn, placed and bound like everyone else and **AI-driven** by the Crowd controller exactly as in a mid-round disconnect (R17). This keeps the spawn indistinguishable (Pillar 1: no pawn is missing or late) and lets a quick rejoin repossess it. (This replaces the Roster/Character Pool edge-case text "no pawn is spawned"; change request.) Its grace clock starts at `ActivePhaseStart` (F4).
- **Spawn gate (Level Socket Contract R4.8).** No pawn is spawned for, possessed by or made relevant to a client before Session has moved that participant to `Connected` for this round. A participant not `Connected` at P6 has its pawn spawned AI-driven and unpossessed. The per-connection relevancy mechanism (relevancy override, dormancy, or an Iris filter) -> ADR [S]; which replication system (legacy or Iris) is in use is part of the same ADR, because `ForceNetUpdate`, net frequency and relevancy differ between them.
- **All commands are rejected until `Active`** (human and AI, `NotNow`), and the Crowd AI does not start; pawns exist in place but nothing moves. Clients show only the briefing screen, so nobody observes the pawns (Level Socket Contract R6.3).
- **`PreparingWatchdogSec`** (150) aborts a `Preparing` that is still not ready, reason `PreparingWatchdog`. It exists only to bound a hang that the owners' timeouts failed to catch; it is not a game timer and it does not run in any other phase.
- A `Disconnect` of a participant during Preparing never aborts the round by itself; only P5b (`TeamAbsent`) does. P5b counts **Connected** participants, so a barrier straggler that has not yet timed out still counts; with `bAllowEmptyTeamDev` the minimum for an empty team is 0.
- **A slow loader is bounded, not punished** (specialist): the barrier holds for at most Session's F7 `Deadline` (60 s at defaults, up to 70.2 s at the layout budget); a participant that exceeds it becomes `Disconnected(TravelTimeout)`, which opens the normal rejoin window, and gets an AI-driven pawn that they repossess when they return. The cost of one slow machine to nine others is therefore at most the F7 `Deadline` plus `WorldReadyTimeoutSec` (a tuning item for the first 10-player test, Open Questions).

**R7. `Active` entry (Preparing to Active).** At the first drain whose server time is `>= ActiveAtServerTime` (P7), Round performs, in one server step: sets `ActiveAtServerTime = 0` in `FRoundPublicState` (so no Active start time stays public, AC 27), stamps `ActivePhaseStart` (server time), increments `RoundsPlayedCount` (so `RoundIndex` is now this round), opens the resolution queue (`Seq` starts at 1; `ServerTickIndex` continues), raises `OnRoundActive(RoundEpoch, ActivePhaseStart)`, accepts commands, tells Crowd to start every NPC and every AI-driven pawn, and releases the loading screen (Session rule 11.8) **in the same step**. Each client drops its loading screen locally when its synchronised server clock reaches `ActiveAtServerTime` **and** either it holds `ExpectedPawnCount` pawns or `RevealPawnWaitMaxSec` (2 s) has passed since `ActiveAtServerTime`, provided it holds its own pawn or its own Roster state is not `Active` (an own pawn killed while loading must not hold the screen); it always drops it when `Phase = Ended` arrives; no countdown or "starting in" text is ever shown. If its clock is not yet synchronised when `Phase = Active` arrives, the bound is timed from that arrival. **The bound** (cross-review S-B4 2026-10-24; ruling, decision log): once `Active`, `ExpectedPawnCount` may never be reached (concealed pawns are culled for every viewer, kills leave vacancies), so an unbounded wait could keep a loading screen forever. Such a client has already passed its single acknowledgement (P5 barrier), exactly like a rejoiner, who drops the screen when its acknowledgement completes (Session rule 11.8, test 48); the time bound only covers pawn arrival. Pawns that arrive after the reveal appear on that client only; NPC and player pawns replicate identically (R23, Shared Character R13), so a late arrival is kind-blind and accepted, not an identity tell. The world-ready stage (Session rule 11, Round CR-13 (d), adopted 2026-10-25) shortens the start wait but is not needed for this bound. The residual spread between clients is the client clock-sync error (about 30 ms, [S] spike), not the one-way delay, so the start no longer carries the host's 0 ms edge. Round invokes `GetUnboundActiveParticipants()` immediately before: a non-empty result is Roster invariant I4 broken and aborts `BindFailed`.

**R8. Aborts, and the leaving of `Active`.**
- **Abort in `Preparing`** (any reason in R6): Round logs the reason, runs the `Reset` cleanup (R10) with reason `PreparingFailed(code)`, and returns everyone to the lobby by `RequestLobbyTravel`. No result, no score change, `RoundsPlayedCount` unchanged. The Event Log slice is archived server-side and **never released** (there was no `Ended`). The host may also issue `AbortRound` in Preparing (the escape from a stuck load).
- **Abort in `Active`** is the host command `AbortRound`, accepted only if `bHostMayAbortActive` (otherwise refused with `WrongPhase`); it goes `Active -> Reset` directly (no `Ended`), `EndReason = Aborted`, no winner, no score, log archived, never released. Without the flag the only ways out of `Active` are a win or a void.
- **Void in `Active`** (review 2026-10-24) is the system's no-award outcome, decided by `TryTerminate(Void(code))` (R15) with `code` in {`Abandoned`, `KickedLastActive`, `PawnLost`}: `Active -> Reset` directly, `EndReason = Void(code)`, no winner, no score, every client sees "The round was called off" (`bCalledOff`), log archived, never released. After an Active abort or void `RoundsPlayedCount` keeps the increment it received at Active entry (the round was played; each Active round has a unique `RoundIndex`).
- **Session end** (`OnSessionEnding`) is a terminal event, not a phase: Round stops processing, flushes the log with `EndReason = SessionEnded`, and nothing is released.
- **Win** is the normal exit: R15.

**R9. `Ended`: one round-end ordering contract, two server steps** (cross-review fixes 2026-10-24, findings S-B1 and S-B2; creative-director + systems-designer ruling, decision log). The win is decided in the **latch step**; everything observable about it happens together in one later server step, the **round-end step**. When the deciding item is a strike, the round-end step is that strike's death write (Assassination ASN-R21: `ContactServerTime − StrikeNetLeadSec`, 0.25 s after the slot at defaults, 0.60 s for a `BushStrike`), run in the same server step as the death write through Assassination's pending-write queue (ASN-R23; Assassination change request 14). For every other terminal item (objective win, forfeit, debug win) the round-end step **is** the latch step and nothing is staged. *Latch step* (R15, inside the winning queue slot; server-only, nothing is replicated or presented): (i) `Terminated` is set and the outcome `{Winner, EndReason, RoundIndex}` is held in the server's latch record; from here Round's server gate treats the round as over (`TryTerminate` refuses, F4 is not evaluated, connection events change only the connection); (ii) the queue closes: every item still pending, and every command, AI item or timer item submitted before the round-end step, returns `Rejected_Latched` (logged), and the `NotNow` replies the IIF owes for them are held and sent at the round-end step, never before; (iii) every active hold, of every pawn kind, is **frozen**: its completion item can only return `Rejected_Latched`, so nothing completes, but it is not cancelled and its owner's ring keeps running; (iv) locomotion is untouched: human-driven pawns keep moving under their players' input and the crowd keeps walking (only discrete commands are refused, for every pawn kind alike), so nothing in the city changes before the blow; (v) a host `ResetRound` or `AbortRound` arriving now is held exactly like an early `ResetRound`. *Round-end step* (one server step; the observable end): (a) sets the replicated phase `Ended` and publishes `FRoundResult {Winner, EndReason, RoundIndex, TeamSummary[2]}` in `FRoundPublicState` with a forced net update (the duration is kept in the server log only: a duration shown after the round would teach players to think in clock time) (specialist). **`TeamSummary[t]`** (added by user decision 2026-10-25 for the MVP text-only round summary, HUD-E21) is `FRoundTeamSummary { uint8 StepsCompleted (0-3: the team's completed stages, the final counted only for the objective winner, from Objectives' server store), uint8 EliminatedCount (members of team t whose `State` is `Informant` in the latch record, every cause alike, forfeits included, counted before `EndRoundAll`), uint8 WrongTargetCount (team t's total of `WrongTarget` results this round, NPC and informant kills alike, saturating at 255, from the Event Log) }`. It is computed in the latch step and published only in the round-end step, is zero in every earlier `FRoundPublicState`, and holds team aggregates only (no participant, no time; IVP R3 `RoundTeamSummary` row); the same step also clears every Informant Pings team part (PNG-R10); (b) calls `EndRoundAll()` (Roster batch; every `Active` or `Informant` record becomes `RoundEnded`; the batch event is **not** evaluated as an elimination) and forces a net update on every record it wrote, so the Roster records leave in the same server frame as the GameState; (c) increments `MatchScore[Winner]` in the same `FRoundPublicState` write (the score is staged with the publication: nothing reads it between the two steps); (d) sends the held `NotNow` replies; (e) fires `OnRoundEnded(Result)`, after which the Visibility Policy treats every viewer as class `PostRound` (the same step for all; the Event Log's `PostRoundEligible` class may be released through the Policy, and in MVP only `FRoundResult` is); (f) **the whole crowd holds**: Round tells Crowd to raise Halt and bring every NPC and AI-driven pawn to a stop by the normal stop-and-blend path, so frozen player pawns are not an accidental "who was who" reveal among still-walking NPCs (specialist; the city holds its breath as the blow lands); **human-driven pawns stop through the same server-driven stop-and-blend path** in the same step (not by input loss and predicted-velocity decay, which would differ from the AI pawns' stop); (g) every frozen hold cancels through the normal release blend (IIF phase-change row; its server event reason `Illegal` is never sent, because `FHoldStatus` carries no reason, and the HUD shows no failure cue for a cancelled ring); (h) `EndedMinDwellSec` and `EndedAutoResetSec` count from this step, so `Reset` can never run between the two steps (Penalty's `OnRosterRoundEnded` handling also runs here, with `EndRoundAll`). From the round-end step all commands are rejected by class `RoundEnded` (IIF R7, Look allowed). **Arrival order:** the GameState and the Roster records are different actors, so a client may receive `RoundEnded` records a net frame before `Phase = Ended`; Teammate Markers (TM-R5) and the HUD (States and Transitions) treat a `RoundEnded` received while their phase is not yet `Ended` as no change, so no ally marker or viewer state moves before the phase does. A death write of an earlier strike that falls due after the round-end step still plays (ASN-R21: written even after a latch) on a pawn that is already stopping. Any deliberate reveal is the Vertical Slice Post-Round Reveal's and goes through the Visibility Policy. A disconnect or rejoin during `Ended` changes only the connection (no transitions are requested). `Ended` leaves on the host command `ResetRound`, or after `EndedAutoResetSec` if non-zero, but **never before `EndedMinDwellSec` (3 s)** after the round-end step: an earlier `ResetRound` is held and executed at the dwell's end, so no client can miss `Ended` between two net updates. `FRoundResult` is also kept in `FRoundPublicState.LastResult` through `Reset` and re-seeded on the lobby GameState.

**R10. `Reset` (Ended or abort to Lobby).** In order: (1) admission `Closed`; (2) cancel every Round timer and invalidate `RoundEpoch`-stamped work (the `ResetAll` of the next start increments the epoch; stale timer items return `Rejected_StaleEpoch`); (3) archive the Event Log slice server-side (match-scoped, `RoundLogRetainCount` = all rounds of the match) and clear the round log buffer; (4) **destroy or release every player pawn of the round** (Roster R9 precondition for the next `ResetAll`): destroy by default, `ReleaseToCrowd` is not used here because the world is about to be torn down; (5) clear round-scoped data (R21); (6) `RequestLobbyTravel()`; (7) when Session reports every participant terminal (`TravelComplete` or `Disconnected`, bounded by `TravelLoadTimeoutSec`), set phase `Lobby`, `SetTeamsLocked(false)`, admission `Open`. `Reset` therefore never waits on anything unbounded. Round does **not** call `ResetAll` here; it runs at the next `StartRound` (R5 step 7).

**D. The resolution queue**

**R11. One queue, one total order.** There is exactly one resolution queue per round, on the server, open from `Active` entry to the latch (or abort). Every state-changing event that can interact with another is an **item** in it. The total-order key of an item is `Key = (ServerTickIndex, Seq)`: `ServerTickIndex` is a monotonically increasing integer incremented once per server game-thread frame at the queue's drain; `Seq` is a monotonically increasing 64-bit counter, assigned on the game thread when the item is **stamped** (R12: client items at drain start by the mailbox permutation, server items by the due-time rule), restarting at 1 for each round. Keys are compared lexicographically; no two items share a key; the queue is drained in ascending `Seq`. **This is the tie-break key that Assassination and every other system must use.** Nobody may keep a feature-local clock or timestamp to order events against another system's events.
- **Follow-ons resolve inline** (systems-designer). Work caused by an item (a Roster transition, `OnPawnKilled` and its subscribers, a strike-limit transition, an elimination check) is executed synchronously, depth-first, **inside the causing item's slot**, so nothing can interleave between a cause and its consequence. Only genuinely separate events (a timer, a deferred outcome) become new items; such an item takes the current `ServerTickIndex` and the next `Seq` and is processed later in the same drain. Inline depth is bounded by `QueueCascadeDepthMax` (8, asserted); a breach is a defect, logged `CascadeOverflow`, and the remaining work is submitted as items **without changing the order** in which it was raised.
- **Roster events flush first.** The Roster queues its events during a mutating call and dispatches them FIFO after the outermost call returns (Roster R12). Round requires: all events raised by an item's resolver are fully dispatched (including Round's own elimination evaluation) **before** the next item is dequeued.
- **Drain position** (-> ADR; ue-replication-specialist): server RPCs run during network dispatch at the top of the world tick, before any tick group [V], so command RPC bodies only push into mailboxes. One Round tick function drains the queue once per frame in `TG_PostPhysics`, after human (dispatch-time) and host/AI (PrePhysics to EndPhysics) movement has settled, so validation sees this frame's positions. Round's own timers do not use the engine timer manager: they live in a Round-owned due-time heap evaluated at the start of the drain [V tick order; S spawn/destroy inside the drain]. If the spike shows that destroying a CMC pawn inside `TG_PostPhysics` is unsafe, pawn destruction requested by a resolver is deferred to a post-drain step **in the same frame** (state is already changed in the slot; only the actor teardown moves).

**R12. Intake: who gets which `Seq`.** Client commands arrive during the network receive step of the frame. They are not stamped on arrival but placed in a **per-source mailbox** (one per participant, including the host's local controller, plus one for server-local sources such as AI pawns), each FIFO. At the start of the drain Round stamps `Seq` by walking the **non-empty** mailboxes, first sorted into a canonical base order (ascending `ParticipantId`, the server-local mailbox last) and then permuted by a **per-tick Fisher-Yates permutation** drawn from the server sub-seed stream `GetServerSubSeed(SeqOrder, ServerTickIndex)`, taking each mailbox's items in FIFO order. Fairness is per source per frame, not per command (a source's burst stays contiguous). Each item carries the sender's Session `ConnectionGeneration`; an item whose generation is older than the participant's current one (sent by a superseded connection) is `Dropped(StaleConnection)` and logged, so FIFO holds per generation. At most `MailboxMaxItemsPerDrain` (constant 32) discrete items are stamped per mailbox per drain (AI `Move` and `Look` are continuous inputs, not queue items, so they never count against the cap; Crowd change request 16); the excess is dropped and counted (`MailboxOverflow`); the rate limit and any kick belong to the IIF. Mailboxes are keyed by `ParticipantId` (plus one server-local mailbox), not by connection or controller, because controllers are replaced at travel. Effects: (a) two commands from different sources that land in the same frame are ordered with probability exactly 1/2 each way (a rotating start index was rejected: it favours adjacent pairs by (M-1)/M to 1/M; ue-replication-specialist and systems-designer), the order is reproducible from the log, and no client can predict it; (b) within one connection order is exactly arrival order; (c) no client-supplied timestamp is ever used. **No lag compensation**: Round never reorders by estimated one-way delay. Answer to Session's change request 2: a command that reaches the server earlier is earlier, including the host's 0 ms edge (about one client one-way delay, 50 ms at 100 ms RTT); the host rotates every session and the log records `SourceMailbox` per item so the bias can be measured. **Server-generated items** (review 2026-10-24, systems-designer): every timed item, **including every hold completion** (`ObjectiveCompletion` and defence-window end), goes through Round's due-time heap. Items with `DueTime <= DrainStartTime` are stamped **before** that frame's intake items (they were already due, so a brace that expired before the frame is not extended by a frame); items that become due during the drain's own processing are stamped after intake. Within each group the order is `(DueTime, TieRank)`, except that IIF `HoldTimer` items with equal `DueTime` are ordered by the key of their `Begin` command (which already embeds the per-tick `SeqOrder` permutation; IIF change request 4, reconciliation 2026-10-24), and `TieRank` breaks any remaining tie, where `TieRank` is a server-secret shuffle of `ParticipantId`s re-drawn **per tick** from `GetServerSubSeed(TieRank, ServerTickIndex)` (not the id itself, which would always favour the earliest joiner, and not fixed per round, which would favour the same participant in every exact tie).

**R13. Resolvers and item types.** Round does not know what a command means. Each owning system registers a **resolver** for its item type; at an item's slot Round calls `Resolve(Item, Key)`; the resolver re-validates against live state, executes synchronously (including follow-on calls such as Roster transitions and the emission of `OnPawnKilled`, `OnActionStarted`), and returns `Executed` or `Voided(reason)`. Voided items change nothing and emit nothing. Item types:

| Type | Submitted by | Resolver | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PawnCommand` | IIF (after its stage-2 checks) | IIF | re-validated at resolution (IIF R5) |
| `ObjectiveCompletion` (stage or final) | Objectives, **inline as a follow-on inside the IIF `HoldTimer(Complete)` item's slot** (not re-submitted as a separate item; systems-designer fix, matches Objectives change request 4) | Objectives | the resolver re-validates the tender (`Active`, Connected, human-controlled, on the team, site live and not completed) at that slot; the final one may call `DeclareObjectiveWin` |
| `HoldTimer(Complete \| CapEnd \| DebounceExpiry \| ConditionCancel)` | IIF, through Round's due-time heap (`ScheduleAt`); `ConditionCancel` is scheduled at the current server time by the IIF's read-only condition sweep (ADR-0011) | IIF | carries `ActionEpoch`; equal `DueTime` ordered by the `Begin` key (R12); `Complete` hosts Objectives' completion follow-on. **Human-controlled** (R15, F2 `Valid(i)`) is re-validated in the slot as "the pawn's control epoch is unchanged since the hold's `Begin` and no hand-over is pending" (ADR-0020 §5.4 (c), ADR-0010 §4.4) |

**R14. Contract with Assassination (and every in-encounter resolver).** (1) Order inside an encounter is decided by comparing the **Round keys** of the commands (`Compare(Key(Prepare), Key(Attack))`): the lower key acts first; Prepare first means the target is prepared (reversal), Attack first means the strike lands (this matches IIF edge case "Prepare and Attack in the same tick"). (2) The outcome of an encounter executes **inside the slot of the command that resolves it**, so the outcome inherits that command's key and nothing else can interleave. It is not re-submitted and not re-stamped. (3) If a feature needs a deferred outcome (a wind-up), it submits a new item with its own later key and records `CauseKey` for the log; it must not reorder items by `CauseKey`. (4) Round exposes `GetKey(Item)`, `Compare(KeyA, KeyB)`, `CurrentKey()`; the attacker-state check at resolution is the resolver's (Roster OQ9).

**E. Win conditions**

**R15. Two win outcomes, one latch.**
- **`ObjectiveWin(team)`**: the shared final objective completes. Objectives' resolver, after re-validating (the completer is `Active`, **Connected and human-controlled** (never an AI-driven bound pawn, R17), on `team`, the site is live and not yet completed), calls `Round.DeclareObjectiveWin(team, Key)`. Preliminary stage completions do not end the round.
- **`EliminationWin(team)`**: a team's **Active** count reaches 0. Round evaluates **every applied single-participant transition event** `e`, in `EventSeq` order, with the zeroing condition `Old = Active`, `New = Informant`, `ActiveCountAfter[e.Team] = 0`, `RosterCount(e.Team) >= 1`, phase `Active`, not terminated; `W` is the other team. Informants never count; batch events (`OnRosterRoundEnded`, `OnRosterReset`) are never evaluated. The outcome is decided by the **Reason partition** (review 2026-10-24; resolves the conflict with Roster F4, which never fires its trigger for Reason `Disconnect` and leaves the forfeit/void adjudication to Round):
  - **(a) Decision reasons** (`Assassinated`, `ReversedAgainst`, `StrikeLimit`): Roster F4 verbatim, including `RosterCount(W) >= 1` and `ActiveCountAfter[W] >= 1`. Result `EliminationWin(W)`, `EndReason = Elimination`.
  - **(b) `Disconnect` from a grace or window expiry** (not a kick): if `ConnectedActiveCount(W) >= 1`, result `EliminationWin(W)` with the **log-only** `EndReason = ForfeitElimination`; the score counts. Otherwise `Void(Abandoned)` (no team has anyone playing). This replaces the earlier listen-server-only `Abandoned` edge and is reachable and testable in MVP.
  - **(c) `Disconnect` caused by a host kick** (Session flags `Disconnected(Kicked)`, change request): `Void(KickedLastActive)`, so the host cannot win by kicking the last rival.
  - There are still **exactly two public win types**. `FRoundResult` shows `EliminationWin` for (a) and (b) alike and the banner keeps "every rival eliminated for the round" (literally true: a converted player is removed from active play); a separate forfeit phrase would tell opponents that someone disconnected (R20; creative-director ruling). The forfeit/elimination split lives only in the log, so F7 can exclude forfeits.
- **The terminal latch.** `TryTerminate(outcome, key)` with `outcome` in {`Win(result)`, `Void(code)`} is single-assignment: it succeeds only if the phase is `Active` and `Terminated` is false; it then stores the outcome and `TerminalKey`, sets `Terminated`, and inside the same call performs R9's latch step (and its round-end step too unless the deciding item is a strike, R9) for a win, or the R8 void path to `Reset` (for a void). A later attempt returns false and is logged `LateWinSuppressed(outcome, key)`. Because the queue is processed serially on one thread, at most one terminal outcome exists per round. (`EndedLatched` in earlier text means `Terminated`.)

**R16. No ties, no draws, no clock.** (1) There is no draw state in the phase machine, the result struct or the score. (2) Simultaneous events are ordered by the key, so exactly one is first (F2 proves uniqueness). (3) The shared final objective means both teams cannot "both complete": the first valid completion latches and the other is voided at re-validation or dropped after the latch. (4) **There is no match time limit and no timer anywhere that can end or change the outcome of an `Active` round**: Round adds no dwell timer, no sudden-death rule and no countdown, and shows no clock (R20). The only Round timers are the disconnect grace, the Preparing watchdog, the world-ready wait and start lead (P7), the pawn-loss emergence retry (R17.9), `EndedMinDwellSec` and the optional `EndedAutoResetSec`. None of them can run during a contest between teams except the grace and the pawn-loss retry, whose effects are a normal `Disconnect` transition or a rebind, both ordered by the queue. (5) Stall pressure (camping, both teams stalling) is Objectives' responsibility and is never a timer and never a reveal; Round contributes only the **Event Log's `EndReason`**, so a playtest can measure whether rounds end by objective (Pillar 4, F7).

**F. Disconnects**

**R17. Disconnect policy (Round decides the consequences of Session's facts).** The facts are Session's events; the transitions are the Roster's; Round is the policy in between.
1. **`OnParticipantUnresponsive` (silence >= `SessionSoftTimeoutSec`, 3 s).** If `bHandOverOnUnresponsive`, and the participant is `Active` with a bound pawn: Round asks Crowd to drive that pawn through the pawn command interface, entering by the **normal blend path**: the pawn does **not** stop dead; a running pawn decelerates through the baseline gait and keeps walking along its heading at crowd pace toward the nearest crowd route node, an interaction is cancelled with its ordinary cancel animation, a held `Prepare` is cancelled (its 60 s cooldown is already spent), and there is no single-frame snap. A dead stop mid-stride would be a human tell (creative-director); a pawn that keeps sprinting into a wall would be another (game-designer). `OnParticipantResponsive` or `OnParticipantRejoined(Superseded)` hands control back by the reverse blend. A handover (either direction) is committed in the drain, bumps the pawn's control epoch (commands stamped under the old epoch void at re-validation) and takes effect at the next frame start; it is never committed during a traversal (it waits for the traversal to end; Shared Character change request 4, Crowd change request 16). No state changes. **Provisional pending the net spike and the Crowd GDD** (Session open question 5, answered: Round adopts it, default on).
   - The same AI driving applies to an `Unresponsive` **informant** with a bound pawn (ADR-0019, ADR-0016): Crowd drives it by the same blend path and constraint, and hands it back on `OnParticipantResponsive`.
   - **Participant-bound AI constraint (binding on Crowd).** A pawn that is bound to a participant and AI-driven (hand-over or grace) issues only `Move` and `Look`. It never issues `Interact`, `Prepare`, `Attack` or `Hide`, never completes an objective and never starts or ends concealment; an absent human must not be struck out, reversed or exposed by a script. If the pawn is concealed when control passes, it stays hidden until it is killed or released to the crowd (specialist).
2. **`OnParticipantDisconnected` (Hard timeout, or a socket close/quit/kick, which skips Unresponsive).** For an `Active` participant: if the pawn is not already AI-driven, take over at once (same blend); start the **grace timer**, a record `{P, GraceExpiry, ExpectedEpoch = (MatchId, RoundEpoch), ExpectedConnectionGeneration}` with `GraceExpiry = max(DisconnectedAt, ActivePhaseStart) + G_eff` (F4). The generation is the participant's Roster `ConnectionGeneration` captured when the timer starts; for a participant disconnected before `Active` the timer is created at `ActivePhaseStart` with the generation current then (a rejoin and re-drop in Preparing therefore leaves no stale timer). The pawn stays `BoundLive`; a kill on it is a `Player` kill: the participant becomes `Informant`, Penalty puts it in Absent with no respawn timer (PEN-R8, cross-review S-B3), and a return, inside or after the grace, takes R18's informant path; the grace timer then fires as a logged no-op (step 4).
3. **Rejoin inside grace** (`OnParticipantRejoined`, participant still `Active`): cancel the grace timer, hand control back by blend; the participant keeps their pawn and their state. A rejoiner passes Session's acknowledgement first and sees only the loading screen.
4. **Grace expiry** (a `TimerFired` item): `RequestTransition(P, Informant, Disconnect, ExpectedEpoch, ExpectedConnectionGeneration)` (Roster R8 requires the generation; without it every request returns `Rejected_StaleConnection`). If `Applied`: `ReleaseToCrowd(pawn)` in the same call stack (the pawn becomes an NPC with the same look; killing it now costs a strike; observers see the same pawn keep walking), and Round evaluates R15 on the resulting event (partition (b)). `Rejected_StaleEpoch`, `Rejected_StaleConnection`, `Rejected_NotDisconnected` and `NoOp_AlreadyInState` change nothing and are logged as `Voided(TimerFired, code)`, never as `GraceExpired`. Constraint (F4): the effective grace is `G_eff = min(DisconnectGraceSec, RejoinWindowSec - GraceMarginSec)` with the constant `GraceMarginSec = 30`; TDC warns when the clamp applies. If Session's `OnRejoinWindowExpired` arrives in phase `Active` for a participant who is **still `Active`** (only possible through a defect, since `G_eff` precedes the window), Round treats it as an immediate grace expiry and logs `WindowBeforeGrace` as a defect; in `Preparing` the event is ignored (the grace has not started, and R18 still admits the participant).
5. **An `Informant` disconnects** (`Disconnected`): Round calls `ReleaseToCrowd` on the informant's pawn at once (it has no grace value). The state stays `Informant`. No elimination effect.
6. **A team whose every player disconnects**: each `Active` member converts at its own grace expiry (R17.4); when the last converts, R15 partition (b) applies: `EliminationWin(W)` with `EndReason = ForfeitElimination` if `W` has a Connected Active member, otherwise `Void(Abandoned)`. If the last conversion was a host kick, `Void(KickedLastActive)`.
7. **Disconnect before `Active`** (during Preparing): the participant gets an AI-driven pawn (R6); the grace clock starts at `ActivePhaseStart`; the round is not aborted unless P5b fails.
8. **Host leaves** (listen server): `OnSessionEnding`; the session and the match end; Round does nothing further (R8).
9. **An `Informant` whose pawn is lost without a kill** is handed to NPC-Kill Penalty & Informants for an immediate re-pawn (its F2 with `D = 0`); Round's own-card respawn below applies to `Active` participants only (Penalty change request 8). **An `Active` participant left without a pawn** (Roster I4 recovery, review 2026-10-24; creative-director ruling). Detection: the Roster raises `OnBindingLost(P, PawnDestroyed)` when a `BoundLive` binding of an `Active` participant clears without a resolved kill (KillZ, streaming, a pool recycle or a bug; change request to the Roster), and Round also asserts at the end of every drain that `GetUnboundActiveParticipants()` is empty. Round submits a `PawnLost(P)` item; in its slot Round logs `PawnLost(P, cause)`, cancels P's in-flight hold or encounter (queued items void at re-validation), keeps P `Active` (no transition, no new Reason) and requests an **own-card respawn**: Crowd's replacement path spawns one pawn of the shared class with P's current `AppearanceId` through `RequestEmergence(Kind = PawnRecovery)` at an emergence socket out of every non-team viewer's line of sight (Level Socket Contract emergence rules), then `BindPawn`, possess if `Connected` or AI-drive if not. This is not a takeover (decided rule C1 is untouched), the card count is unchanged (Character Pool CP-11) and the total pawn count is restored. If no socket is eligible, Round retries once per second for up to `LateJoinTakeoverTimeoutSec` (Round's only use of that key); the participant is pawnless meanwhile and is **not** put in the engine spectator state (`bIsSpectator` replicates to every client). Cap: `PawnLossRecoveryMaxCount` (2) per participant per round; a third loss, or a retry timeout, ends the round `Void(PawnLost)` (a systemic fault, never punished as a decision). Prevention is Shared Character's: KillZ far below the kit and a pawn that survives `FellOutOfWorld`.

**R18. Re-admission after expiry.** Round implements Session's `ShouldAdmitExpired(P)` (review 2026-10-24, matching the Roster rule "a returner is never refused for having been gone"): **`ShouldAdmitExpired(P) = P.InRound AND Phase in {Preparing, Active, Ended}`**. It is false only for a record that was not `InRound` at `ResetAll` (Dormant at the start; it returns through normal admission in `Lobby`) and in `Lobby`/`Reset` (where Session's own admission modes apply). The answer never depends on state; **the role on return follows the Roster state**:
- `Active` (in `Preparing`, or within grace in `Active`): R17.3 (or the P5 wait set before P6); the grace timer is cancelled and the participant repossesses their pawn.
- `Informant` in `Active` with no live pawn ("awaiting pawn", Roster R11): re-pawned by NPC-Kill Penalty & Informants through the **informant respawn path of decided rule C1**, in Penalty's PEN-R9 order (as fixed by ADR-0019 §4): the Level Socket Contract takeover `PickRespawnPawn` (R5b) picks an unseen eligible crowd NPC in the team's respawn region (out of line of sight of all non-team viewers; NPCs mid-interaction, concealed or braced are not eligible); Crowd releases it; Shared Character's `TakeOverCrowdPawn` (R2a) checks eligibility and calls Roster `BindPawn` **on Penalty's behalf** (Penalty never calls `BindPawn` itself; ADR-0010 §3.2); only if it returned `Applied`, the taken pawn's existing card is moved by `TakeNpcCard(ParticipantId, Pawn)`, a card move that leaves `AppearanceId` untouched (Penalty CR-6, Character Pool CP-12b; this replaces the earlier "no `TakeNpcCard` card move is needed", Crowd change request 9), then `RecordAppearance`; never a spawn. Round starts this path by calling Penalty's `IInformantBodyProvider::OnInformantReadmitted(P)` (an interface Round declares and the composition root binds to Penalty, ADR-0019 §1) inside its slot, **only after the returner's Session acknowledgement**, when its PlayerController is ready. The returner's old pawn was released to the crowd when they left, so taking one back leaves the total pawn count and the NPC count unchanged from before their departure, and no extra I-2 check is needed. Penalty owns the respawn and its retries (PEN-R10, LSC R5b.5 widening): `LateJoinTakeoverTimeoutSec` and its view-only fallback do **not** apply to informants. While waiting, the returner is admitted and sees the world from a neutral view (not the engine spectator state).
- In `Ended`: admitted, view only, no pawn, no transition, until `Reset` (neutral camera, not the engine spectator state).
Reconnecting never changes state: an informant stays an informant, an Active-within-grace participant stays Active.

**Informant awaiting a body (ADR-0019 sync 2026-10-24).** Round's own handler of an applied `Active → Informant` transition with Reason `Assassinated`, `ReversedAgainst` or `StrikeLimit` calls `IInformantBodyProvider::OnInformantAwaitingBody(P, Cause, slot time)` **after** its R15 elimination check and **only if the round did not latch**, so no respawn is ever scheduled (and no countdown published) inside a deciding slot; Penalty schedules the respawn from that call (PEN-R9). After a latch Round makes no call.

**G. The Round Event Log**

**R19. What is recorded, and who may see it.** The log is **ServerOnly** during `Preparing` and `Active` and is held in memory (a ring of `RoundLogMaxEntriesCount` entries plus an uncapped header). It is the only record of why a round ended and the raw material for playtest analysis (Pillar 4).
- **Header** (written at P3): `RoundIndex`, `RoundEpoch`, `Seed`, `LayoutId`, `LayoutAttempt`, `LayoutHash`, `ProviderKind`, `LayoutFallbackLevel`, `PresetId`, `TuningHash`, `SchemaVersion`, `FallbackLevel`, `HotReloaded`, tuning warnings, `DealDigest`, the active appearance set and counts, `PoolBelowMvpFloor`, the team rosters (`ParticipantId` to team), the host's `ParticipantId`, per-zone player shares (F5), build version.
- **Entries**, each `{Key, ServerTime, RoundEpoch, Type, ParticipantIds, SourceMailbox, data}`: `PhaseChanged`; `StateTransition` (Old, New, Reason, `EventSeq`, `ActiveCountAfter[2]`, `InformantCountAfter[2]`); `KillResolved` (`VictimKind`); `Reversal`; `StrikeLimit`; `ObjectiveStage(team, stage)`; `FinalCompleted(team)`; `Unresponsive`, `Disconnected`, `Rejoined`, `GraceExpired`, `ReleasedToCrowd`, `HandOver`, `HandBack`; `InformantRespawn`; `StrikeCounted`, `RespawnScheduled`, `RespawnCancelled` (Penalty); `StageSignalReleased(viewingTeam, count)`, `TendCompleted(ParticipantId, court, Counted | NoEffect)` for bound pawns, `ClueTooBroad(LayoutHash)` once per layout (OC edge case, AC-OC-28), and the per-team Objectives F5 pacing fields at `Ended` (Objectives); `Voided(type, reason)` for items that failed re-validation; `Rejected_Latched`; `LateWinSuppressed`; `PawnLost`, `PawnRecovered`; `StartRolledBack`; `Dropped(StaleConnection)`, `MailboxOverflow`; `WinLatched(outcome, EndReason, key)` (also written for a void). High-rate commands (`Move`, `Look`) are not logged. `Voided` entries are rate-limited to one per source per second (the rest are counted). Because a 10-source flood over a whole round can still exceed the ring (ADR-0023 §5, AQ-16), the **kill timeline is protected from eviction**: `KillResolved`, `Reversal`, `StateTransition`, `StrikeLimit`, `ObjectiveStage`, `FinalCompleted` and `WinLatched` entries are never dropped by truncation (their count is bounded by the round's kills, transitions and stages), and `TruncatedCount` is kept per entry type.
- **`EndReason`** is one of `ObjectiveWin`, `Elimination`, `ForfeitElimination` (both public as `EliminationWin`), or, for a non-result, `Aborted`, `Void(Abandoned | KickedLastActive | PawnLost)`, `PreparingFailed(code)`, `SessionEnded`, `DebugForced`.
- **Truncation.** The header sits outside the ring; the protected entries above (including `WinLatched`) occupy ring slots but are never evicted. When the ring is full the oldest non-protected entry is dropped and `TruncatedCount` (per type) increments.
- **Telemetry section (ADR-0023).** The Event Log store also holds a separate, fixed-capacity **telemetry section** (playtest probes and aggregates; it never shares or evicts ring slots). Its release class is `NeverReleased`: it is never replicated, shown or included in `FRoundResult`, and is exported to the server's disk on entering `Ended`, or on entering `Reset` for a round that never reached `Ended` (abort, void, `PreparingFailed`), or at the `SessionEnded` latch; then it is freed.
- **Secrets are not stored.** The log holds `Seed` (public by Session rule 14) and hashes only; server sub-seeds and entropy are derived on demand and never written. Log entries never pass through `UE_LOG`, the output log, `ClientMessage` or trace channels, because `Saved/Logs`, crash dumps and Insights captures are readable by the host (ue-replication-specialist).
- **Release classes** (under the Information Visibility Policy; **provisional**): `NeverReleased` (`DealDigest`, `TuningHash`, mailbox order, connection and ping data, `Debug` items); `PostRoundEligible` (kill timeline, state transitions, objective milestones, the team table, the look-to-participant link: released only by the Vertical Slice Post-Round Reveal, only in phase `Ended`, only to class `PostRound`); `PublicResult` (`FRoundResult`, including its per-team `TeamSummary` aggregates: the **only** content released in MVP). Nothing is released in `Preparing`, `Active`, `Reset` or after an abort. The text-only round summary is MVP since the user decision of 2026-10-25 and reads only `FRoundResult` (HUD-E21); the look-to-participant link and per-participant content stay with the VS Post-Round Reveal (OQ4 declined).
- **Host.** The log never leaves the server process before `Ended`; the host's own HUD reads `FRoundPublicState` and C-TeamView only (host parity, Roster R6). **Stated honestly:** on a listen server this is a code discipline enforced by review and a compile-time check (host UI code may include only the public struct), not a security boundary; the host process holds the log in memory until the Dedicated Server (Alpha). The development dump command `stealth.round.dumplog` is compiled out of shipping and refuses to run unless the phase is `Ended` or `Reset`. The log is written to disk server-side on entering `Ended` or `Reset`, never mid-round.

**H. Replication**

**R20. What is public, and what never leaves the server.** `FRoundPublicState` lives on the GameState (always relevant, one struct with RepNotify, identical bytes for every viewer): `Phase` (enum value 0 is an explicit `Unknown`, so a late joiner whose first value equals a default still runs its handler; clients also read the struct at begin-play, and the host calls the same handler explicitly because the server receives no RepNotify), `RoundIndex`, `PhaseChangeCount` (a counter for stale-message logic), `PhaseEnteredServerTime` (**stamped for `Lobby`, `Preparing`, `Ended` and `Reset` only; zero during `Active`**), `MatchScore[2]`, `bCalledOff` (true in `Reset` after a host abort or a void, so every client can show "The round was called off"), `ExpectedPawnCount` (`D`, published in `Preparing`; constant for the round, it reveals nothing the lobby's `n / capacity` does not), `ActiveAtServerTime` (non-zero **only in `Preparing`** after P7; zeroed in the Active-entry step), and in `Ended` the `FRoundResult`, kept as `LastResult` through `Reset`. The client handler diffs the struct against its previous copy (one RepNotify can carry a phase and a score change together) and uses `PhaseChangeCount` to detect a skipped phase. **Never replicated, computed on or presented by any client before `Ended`:** opponent `ActiveCount`, `InformantCount`, `AwaitingPawn` or anything derived from them ("1 enemy left", a remaining-rival icon, an elimination-progress bar); grace deadlines or any disconnect fact about a non-teammate; queue keys; the Event Log; the elapsed time of the `Active` phase or any countdown. Omitting the Active start time keeps the public struct free of a clock source, but it cannot stop a client from timestamping the phase change locally (stated honestly, review 2026-10-24); the binding, testable rule is presentation: **no client presentation may show elapsed Active time** (AC 31). The phase change is delivered to every client by the same property and carries no per-participant data, so it is Pillar-1 neutral. A late or reconnecting client receives the current struct on connect and sees exactly what every client sees. Every Round mutation forces a net update of the GameState (it otherwise sends at 10 Hz, up to 100 ms late) [V]. `RoundEpoch` is **not** public (no client use). The GameState is recreated at every seamless travel [V], so the mirror is re-seeded from the game-instance Round object before its first net update; a new match world starts at `Preparing`, never at a default. **The simultaneous reveal is a scheduled start** (R7, P7): every client lifts its loading screen at `ActiveAtServerTime` on its synchronised server clock (`EstimatedServerNow()`, ADR-0002 §9; never the engine's replicated GameState world time), so the spread is the clock-sync error [S], not the one-way delay; the queue opens at the same server time. Later phase changes (`Ended`) still reach clients up to one one-way delay plus one send interval after the host (Session rule 3) [V]. The seed travels in the travel URL, which the engine sends to every client [V]; this is acceptable only because the seed is public by design (Session rule 14) and every hidden draw comes from `ServerSecret` sub-seeds, never from the seed.

**J. Host commands and testing**

**R22. Start and reset.** The MVP's "basic way to start and reset a round" is a small set of host-only commands executed on the authority (console or the optional host panel). ## Formulas

*systems-designer consulted (win-resolution attack: findings A1-A10 and F1-F6; the latch was judged deterministic, and its fixes are folded into R11-R17). Round defines ordering, validity and timing rules, not balance curves.*

### F1. Resolution-order key and intake permutation

The resolution_order formula is defined as:

`Key(i) = (T_i, Seq_i)`; `i precedes j  <=>  T_i < T_j  OR  (T_i = T_j AND Seq_i < Seq_j)`; at intake of tick T with non-empty mailboxes `B_1..B_m`: `pi_T = FisherYates(m, SubSeed(SeqOrder, T))`, and `Seq` is assigned to the items of `B_pi_T(1)` in FIFO order, then `B_pi_T(2)`, and so on, then to server-generated items in submission order.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| T_i | int64 | >= 0 | calculated (Round tick counter, +1 per drain) | Server tick in which item i was stamped |
| Seq_i | int64 | 1 to 2^63 | calculated (per-round counter) | Arrival sequence id; strictly increasing, unique per round |
| m | int | 0 to 11 | calculated | Non-empty mailboxes this tick (up to 10 participants + 1 server-local) |
| pi_T | permutation | -- | calculated (Session `GetServerSubSeed(SeqOrder, T)`) | Per-tick intake order, server-secret, logged |
| dt | float | 0.011-0.033 s | measured (server frame time) | One frame; the width of a "same tick" |
| W | float | 0.5-5 s | design estimate | Window within which two contested actions are both plausible |

**Output Range:** a strict total order over every item of a round (no two keys equal). For two sources landing in the same frame, `P(a before b) = 1/2` exactly. Probability that two independent contested actions land in the same frame: `P_same ~= dt / W`. **Example:** at 60 Hz (`dt` = 0.0167 s) and a 2 s contest window, `P_same` = 0.83 %; at 30 Hz it is 1.7 %. A final-site race that is that close is decided by the permutation, with no source favoured. Orders outside one frame are decided by arrival time (no lag compensation, R12).

### F2. Win predicates and outcome uniqueness

The win_resolution formulas are defined as:

`ObjectiveWin(t, i) = Phase = Active AND NOT L AND i.Type = FinalCompletion AND Valid(i) AND Team(i.Completer) = t`, where `Valid(i)` = completer `Active`, `Connected`, human-controlled, site live and not yet completed;

`Zero(e) = e.Applied AND e.Old = Active AND e.New = Informant AND Phase = Active AND NOT L AND e.ActiveCountAfter[e.Team] = 0 AND RosterCount(e.Team) >= 1`, with `W = 1 - e.Team`;

`Decision(e) = e.Reason in {Assassinated, ReversedAgainst, StrikeLimit}`; `Expiry(e) = e.Reason = Disconnect AND NOT e.Kicked`; `Kick(e) = e.Reason = Disconnect AND e.Kicked` (the three are disjoint and cover every Reason that can zero a team);

`EliminationWin(W, e) = Zero(e) AND RosterCount(W) >= 1 AND ( (Decision(e) AND e.ActiveCountAfter[W] >= 1) OR (Expiry(e) AND ConnectedActiveCount(W) >= 1) )` (Roster F4 for (a), forfeit for (b));

`Void(e) = Zero(e) AND RosterCount(W) >= 1 AND ( (Expiry(e) AND ConnectedActiveCount(W) = 0) -> Abandoned ; Kick(e) -> KickedLastActive )`; plus `Void(PawnLost)` from R17.9;

`Outcome = the terminal outcome (win or void) of the first item, in key order, for which a predicate is true`; `L` (`Terminated`) becomes true in that item's slot.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| L | bool | -- | calculated (`Terminated`) | Set once by `TryTerminate` |
| e.Kicked | bool | -- | calculated (Session `Disconnected(Kicked)` flag, provisional) | The disconnect was a host kick |
| ConnectedActiveCount(T) | int | 0 to TeamSize | calculated (Roster state and `Connection`) | Members of T that are `Active` and `Connected` |
| i | queue item | -- | calculated | A resolving item |
| e | event | -- | calculated (`OnParticipantStateChanged` payload) | One applied single-participant transition |
| e.ActiveCountAfter[T] | int | 0 to TeamSize | calculated (Roster F1 snapshot) | Active count right after e |
| RosterCount(T) | int | 0 to TeamSize | calculated (Roster F3) | Records on team T |


**Output Range:** exactly one terminal outcome (win or void) per round that leaves `Active` other than by host abort or session end, and never two. Proof: (1) items are resolved one at a time in a strict total order (F1); (2) follow-ons run inside the causing slot (R11), so no other item interleaves between a cause and its check; (3) one item causes at most one single-participant transition that can zero a team (an assassination removes the victim **or**, if reversed, the attacker; a strike-limit or grace expiry removes one participant), and an objective completion removes nobody, so one item produces at most one candidate; the Reason partition is disjoint, so at most one of `EliminationWin` and `Void` holds for that event; (4) `TryTerminate` is single-assignment over wins and voids; every later candidate returns false and every later item returns `Rejected_Latched`; (5) batch events are never evaluated, so `EndRoundAll` cannot create a second candidate; (6) a team with `RosterCount = 0` is never eliminated and never wins by elimination, so a 1v0 dev round has only `ObjectiveWin`; if its solo player becomes an informant the round cannot be won and the host aborts it. There is no result value for "both", so a draw cannot be represented (a void is "no award", not a shared result). **Forfeit example:** team 1's last Active member's grace expires at tick 5000 while team 0 has 2 Active, 1 of them Connected: `EliminationWin(0)`, `EndReason = ForfeitElimination`, `MatchScore[0] += 1`. Had team 0's only Active members both been disconnected, the result would be `Void(Abandoned)` and no score. **Example:** tick 900: Seq 41 is team 0's final completion and Seq 42 is team 1's strike on team 0's last Active player. Result `ObjectiveWin(0)`; Seq 42 returns `Rejected_Latched`. With the Seqs swapped, Seq 41's kill gives `e{team 0, ActiveCountAfter = [0, 3]}`, so `EliminationWin(1)`, and the completion returns `Rejected_Latched` (the completion fails both re-validation, because the completer is no longer `Active`, and the latch; either alone is sufficient).

### F3. Start validity

The start_validity formula is defined as:

`StartValid = for all p: Team(p) != None AND Conn(p) = Connected; for t in {0,1}: m_t <= n_t <= TeamSize; abs(n0 - n1) <= MaxTeamImbalanceCount; n0 + n1 >= 1`, where `m_t = MinPlayersPerTeamCount`, except that with `bAllowEmptyTeamDev` one team may have `m_t = 0`. Cross-field invariant: `MinPlayersPerTeamCount <= TeamSize` (otherwise no lobby can start). With `MaxTeamImbalanceCount = 0` an odd head-count can never start; the host sees `TeamsInvalid(Imbalance)`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| n_t | int | 0 to 5 | calculated (Roster `RosterCount`) | Rostered participants on team t |
| TeamSize | int | 1 to 5 | data file (`TeamSize`, Round) | Team cap |
| MinPlayersPerTeamCount | int | 1 to 5 | data file (Round) | Minimum per team |
| MaxTeamImbalanceCount | int | 0 to 4 | data file (Round) | Allowed difference |
| bAllowEmptyTeamDev | bool | -- | data file (Round, dev) | Allows 1v0 |

**Output Range:** boolean. **Example:** 5v4: valid. 5v3: invalid (`Imbalance`, 2 > 1). 2v2 with `TeamSize` 2: valid. 1v0: invalid unless `bAllowEmptyTeamDev` (then imbalance 1 <= 1, valid).

### F4. Disconnect timing

The disconnect_timing formulas are defined as:

`G_eff = min(G, RejoinWindowSec - GraceMarginSec)` with `GraceMarginSec = 30` (constant); `GraceExpiry(p) = max(DisconnectedAt(p), ActivePhaseStart) + G_eff`; `T_takeover = L_soft` (silent drop, hand-over on) or `0` (socket close, quit, kick); `T_informant = L_detect + G_eff`, with `L_detect = L_hard` (silent drop) or `~0` (explicit); guaranteed `RejoinWindowSec - G_eff >= 30`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| G | float | 3 to 120 s | data file (`DisconnectGraceSec`, Round, default 30; below 10 only in dev presets) | Grace as configured |
| G_eff | float | 3 to 120 s | calculated | Grace actually applied |
| DisconnectedAt | float | >= 0 | calculated (Session event time) | When Session reported `Disconnected` |
| ActivePhaseStart | float | >= 0 | calculated (R7) | Start of `Active` |
| L_soft, L_hard | float | 2.75-3.3 s, 11.75-12.3 s | calculated (Session F2 at defaults) | Detection latency of Soft and Hard |
| RejoinWindowSec | float | 60 to 600 s (Round requires a hard minimum of 60; Session's table says 30, change request) | data file (Session, default 120) | Session's slot reservation |

**Output Range:** `T_takeover` 0 to 3.3 s; `T_informant` from `G_eff` (explicit quit) to `G_eff + 12.3` s (silent drop): 30 to 42.3 s at defaults, 3 to 15.3 s at the dev floor, 120 to 132.3 s at the maximum. `RejoinWindowSec - G_eff >= 30` always (90 s at defaults) for an informant's return. At `RejoinWindowSec = 60` the clamp limits `G_eff` to 30. **Preparing case:** a participant who timed out loading at t = 60 s of Preparing still converts at `ActivePhaseStart + G_eff`, even if their Session window has meanwhile expired (R17.4 ignores the expiry in Preparing; R18 still admits them). **Example:** a client's router dies at t = 200.0 s of an Active round. The pawn is AI-driven at about 203.3 s; Session reports `Disconnected` at about 212.3 s; the grace expires at 242.3 s and the participant becomes an Informant (`Disconnect`) and the pawn joins the crowd. A player who instead quits the game at 200.0 s becomes an Informant at 230.0 s. A participant who failed to load and is `Disconnected` at Active start (t = 0) converts at 30 s.

### F5. Round-start placement share and spawn order

The placement_share and spawn_order formulas are defined as:

`ZoneShare_z = P_z / (P_z + N_z)`, defined only for zones with `P_z > 0` (an empty zone has no share), required `ZoneShare_z <= 0.5` (asserted by Round; guaranteed by Level Socket Contract R6.2/D6); `GlobalShare = P / (P + NpcCount)`; spawn order `sigma = FisherYates(D, SubSeed(SpawnOrder, LayoutAttempt))`, `E[rank of a player pawn] = (D + 1) / 2`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| P_z | int | 0 to 5 | calculated (placement result) | Players placed in zone z (within `TeamZoneRadiusCm` of the anchor) |
| N_z | int | 0 to NpcCount | calculated (placement result) | NPCs placed in zone z (the share rule requires `N_z >= P_z`) |
| P | int | 1 to 10 | calculated | Participants this round |
| NpcCount | int | `max(2 x TeamSize, 24 - P)` to 100 | data file (`NpcCount`, Crowd/TDC, default 50) | NPC count; the floor combines TDC I-2 and the Character Pool deck floor (`D >= 24`); below it R5 refuses `DeckInfeasible` |
| D | int | P + NpcCount | calculated | Pawns spawned in the start frame |

**Output Range:** `ZoneShare_z` 0 to 0.5 (a failure aborts `PlacementInvariant`); `GlobalShare` 0.167 at 5v5 with 50 NPCs, 0.074 at 2v2. Spawn ranks of player pawns are uniform on 1..D. **Example:** 5v5, 50 NPCs: `D` = 60, `GlobalShare` = 0.167, and expected player spawn rank 30.5. If zone 0 receives 5 players and LSC D6 places 9 NPCs there, `ZoneShare_0` = 0.36 (valid); 5 players with 4 NPCs would be 0.56 and the round aborts.

### F6. Preparing watchdog bound

The watchdog_bound formula is defined as:

(Revised in review 2026-10-24: the client load runs in parallel with the server load and layout, so the bound is Session's single deadline, not a sum of waits; the separate tuning and pool acknowledgements are retired.)

`T_prep_max = max(TravelLoadTimeoutSec, T_srvload + LayoutTotalBudgetSec + T_tuning + TravelAckGraceSec)` (Session F7); `T_needed = T_prep_max + T_spawn + WorldReadyTimeoutSec + StartLeadSec + 30`; **`PreparingWatchdogEff = max(PreparingWatchdogSec, T_needed)`**, computed once when tuning freezes, with a logged warning whenever `T_needed` wins. No knob combination can therefore make the watchdog fire before the owners' own deadlines.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| TravelLoadTimeoutSec | float | 20 to 180 s | data file (Session, 60) | Load-and-acknowledge deadline from travel start |
| TravelAckGraceSec | float | 5 to 60 s | data file (Session, 15) | Minimum ack time after the server's readiness data is published |
| T_srvload | float | 1 to 20 s | measured (spike); 20 used as the bound | Server map load |
| LayoutTotalBudgetSec | float | 0 to 45 s | data file (Level Socket Contract, 45; ~0 for the graybox) | Worst-case layout time including reseeds |
| T_tuning | float | 0 to 1 s | measured (TDC); 1 used as the bound | Tuning freeze |
| T_spawn | float | 0 to 1 s | measured (spike S: 60 pawns in one frame); 1 used as the bound | P6 |
| WorldReadyTimeoutSec, StartLeadSec | float | 2 to 30 s; 0.1 to 1 s | data file (Round, 10; 0.25) | P7 |

**Output Range:** `T_needed` 52.1 s (all minima: `max(20, 1 + 0 + 0 + 5) + 0 + 2 + 0.1 + 30`) to 242 s (all maxima: `max(180, 20 + 45 + 1 + 60) + 1 + 30 + 1 + 30`; the hard maximum of `PreparingWatchdogSec`, 300, stays above it). **Example:** defaults with a generated layout at its budget: `T_prep_max = max(60, 20 + 45 + 1 + 15) = 81`; `T_needed = 81 + 1 + 10 + 0.25 + 30 = 122.25 <= 150`, so `PreparingWatchdogEff = 150`. With `TravelLoadTimeoutSec = 120`: `T_needed = 120 + 1 + 10 + 0.25 + 30 = 161.25`, so the effective watchdog becomes 161.25 s and a warning is logged.

### F7. Pacing metrics (logged only, never a rule)

The pacing formulas are defined as:

`PacingRatio = DurationSec / (60 x TargetRoundMinutes)`; `ObjectiveEndShare = N_obj / (N_obj + N_elim)` over playtest rounds that ended by a win and are not `Debug`, where `N_elim` counts `EndReason = Elimination` only (forfeits are counted separately in `N_forfeit` and excluded, so network drops cannot distort the Pillar 4 measure); `AbortedActiveShare = (N_abort + N_void) / (N_obj + N_elim + N_forfeit + N_abort + N_void)`; any share whose denominator is 0 is reported as "n/a", never as 0; `StallFlag = DurationSec > 2 x 60 x TargetRoundMinutes`, and also any Active-phase abort with `DurationSec > 60 x TargetRoundMinutes` (a called-off long round is a stall candidate, so host aborts cannot hide stalls).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| DurationSec | float | > 0 | calculated (latch time - `ActivePhaseStart`) | Length of the Active phase |
| TargetRoundMinutes | float | 5 to 30 | data file (Round, 12) | Non-binding target |
| N_obj, N_elim | int | >= 0 | calculated (log `EndReason`) | Rounds ended by each win type |
| N_abort | int | >= 0 | calculated (log `EndReason = Aborted` from `Active`) | Rounds the host called off while Active |
| N_forfeit, N_void | int | >= 0 | calculated (log `EndReason = ForfeitElimination`, `Void(code)`) | Forfeited and voided rounds |

**Output Range:** `PacingRatio` > 0 (1.0 = on target); `ObjectiveEndShare` 0 to 1, with the Pillar 4 validation target "most rounds end by objective" (> 0.5). A `StallFlag` round is reported as an **Objectives stall-pressure defect**, never answered with a Round timer. A rising `AbortedActiveShare` is a Pillar 3/4 defect signal (losing rounds are being erased, so "every mistake sticks" does not hold) and is reviewed after every playtest (creative-director). **Example:** a round of 15 min 36 s: `PacingRatio` = 936 / 720 = 1.3. Over 10 playtest win rounds with 7 objective wins and 3 eliminations: `ObjectiveEndShare` = 0.7; with 2 more rounds called off: `AbortedActiveShare` = 2 / 12 = 0.17. Boundaries at the 12-minute target: `DurationSec` 1440.0 is not a stall, 1440.001 is; an Active abort at 720.0 is not flagged, at 720.001 it is.

### F8. Event Log size (sanity check)

The log_size formula is defined as:

`LogBytes ~= HeaderBytes + E x EntryBytes`, with `E <= RoundLogMaxEntriesCount`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| HeaderBytes | int | ~1 KB | constant (estimate) | Header |
| EntryBytes | int | 64 to 96 B | constant (estimate; to be measured, AC 34b) | One entry (key 16 B, time 8, epoch 4, type, mailbox, participant ids, data) |
| E | int | 0 to 16384 | calculated | Entries this round (the ring holds `RoundLogMaxEntriesCount`; the header is outside it) |

**Output Range:** about 20-30 KB for a typical round (about 300 entries), at most about 400 KB at the 4096 default and about 1.6 MB at the 16384 hard maximum. Server memory only; no bandwidth before `Ended`. **Example:** 300 entries x 80 B + 1 KB = 25 KB.

## Edge Cases

*systems-designer consulted (same-tick and cascade interleavings). Ordering of anything in one tick is by F1's key.*

**Simultaneous events and the latch**
- **If team 0's final completion and the strike that eliminates team 0's last Active player resolve in the same tick**: the lower key wins (F2 example). The other item returns `Rejected_Latched`. No draw.
- **If the last Active players of both teams eliminate each other in the same tick**: the first strike in key order zeroes one team and latches `EliminationWin` for the other; the second strike returns `Rejected_Latched` (its attacker was also already `Informant`, so it would fail re-validation anyway).
- **If both teams' final holds would complete in the same tick**: impossible to share: the final site is one objective. The first completion in key order latches `ObjectiveWin`; the other returns `Rejected_Latched` and its hold releases through the normal blend.
- **If a defender's brace expires (`TimerFired`) and a strike on them arrives in the same tick**: key order decides; the brace-expiry item and the strike are both items, so the outcome never depends on which tick function ran first.
- **If a player's third NPC kill (strike limit) makes them their team's last Active to leave**: the strike-limit transition runs inline in the kill's slot; F4 on that event gives `EliminationWin` for the other team in that slot.
- **If a reversal eliminates the attacker who was their team's last Active**: `EliminationWin` for the defender's team, inline (Roster F4 example).
- **If a grace expiry and the same participant's rejoin are due in the same tick**: the rejoin is a Session event processed before the drain (receives first, Session's same-tick order), so the timer item finds `Connection = Connected`, the transition returns `Rejected_NotDisconnected`, and the participant stays `Active`.
- **If two participants' grace timers expire in the same tick**: they are submitted by `(DueTime, TieRank)`. If they are the last Active of opposite teams, the first conversion is evaluated by R15 (b): the other team's last Active member is still Active but **disconnected**, so `ConnectedActiveCount(W) = 0` and the round is `Void(Abandoned)`; the shuffle never picks a winner from a team with nobody playing.
- **If a participant rejoins and drops again just before the first grace timer fires** (drop at 100 s, rejoin at 129.9 s, drop at 129.95 s, first timer at 130 s): the first timer carries the old `ConnectionGeneration` and returns `Rejected_StaleConnection` (logged `Voided`); the second timer converts at `129.95 + G_eff`.
- **If an item arrives after the latch in the same drain**: `Rejected_Latched`; it is logged and never executed, and the issuing owner gets the ordinary IIF reply for a phase change (`NotNow`).
- **If a resolver tries to call `DeclareObjectiveWin` or causes an elimination outside `Active`**: `TryTerminate` returns false and logs `LateWinSuppressed`.

**Phases and starts**
- **If `StartRound` is issued twice**: the second returns `WrongPhase` or `TravelInProgress`; nothing changes.
- **If the host issues `AbortRound` during travel**: Round marks the round aborted; when the new world reports P1 it immediately runs `Reset` and `RequestLobbyTravel`; no pawn is spawned.
- **If TDC reports `Failed` or `TuningSnapshotFault`**: abort to Lobby with that reason; no result; `RoundsPlayedCount` unchanged.
- **If `OnLayoutReady` reports a `Seed` different from `FRoundParams.Seed`**: abort `LayoutSeedMismatch` (a provider bug).
- **If all members of one team are `Disconnected` at the end of the barrier**: P5b fails, abort `TeamAbsent`, back to Lobby.
- **If `GetUnboundActiveParticipants()` is non-empty at Active entry**: abort `BindFailed`; this is Roster I4 broken and is a defect.
- **If a rostered participant is `Dormant` when the host presses Start**: `ParticipantNotConnected` lists them; the host waits or removes them; no automatic pruning.
- **If the participant count changes in Lobby after a refused start**: the next `StartRound` re-evaluates F3 from scratch.

**Disconnects**
- **If an Active participant's socket closes (quit) without a Soft phase**: takeover at once, grace from `DisconnectedAt` (F4: Informant after 30 s).
- **If an informant disconnects**: `ReleaseToCrowd` at once; state stays `Informant`; on return during `Active` they are re-admitted as an informant awaiting a pawn (R18).
- **If Session's rejoin window expires in `Active` while the participant is still `Active`**: only possible through a defect (`G_eff` always ends first); treated as an immediate grace expiry and logged `WindowBeforeGrace`. **In `Preparing`** the expiry is ignored and the participant is still admitted on return (R18).
- **If a participant rejoins during `Ended`**: admitted, view-only, no pawn, no transition, not in the engine spectator state.
- **If the host kicks a team's last Active member**: the kick is a `Disconnect` with the `Kicked` flag; at grace expiry R15 (c) voids the round (`Void(KickedLastActive)`), so a kick can never win a round. A kick of a non-last member converts normally at grace expiry.
- **If the last Active member of team 1 converts at grace expiry while team 0 has Active members but all of them are disconnected**: `Void(Abandoned)`, no score.
- **If an Active participant's pawn is destroyed without a kill** (KillZ, a bug): R17.9: own-card respawn through an emergence socket out of non-team sight, the participant stays Active; the third loss in a round, or no eligible socket within `LateJoinTakeoverTimeoutSec`, voids the round `Void(PawnLost)`.
- **If a returning informant finds no eligible NPC in its team's respawn set**: admitted with a neutral view; Penalty keeps retrying with the Level Socket Contract's widening (PEN-R10, LSC R5b.5) until a pawn is found, the round ends or the participant leaves. `LateJoinTakeoverTimeoutSec` does not apply (reconciliation 2026-10-24).
- **If a participant disconnects during `Ended` or `Reset`**: no grace timer, no transition; at Lobby arrival they become `Dormant` (Session rule 12).
- **If every member of a team disconnects mid-round**: each converts at its grace expiry; the last conversion gives `EliminationWin` to the other team with `EndReason = ForfeitElimination` (F2, R15 (b)) if that team has a Connected Active member, otherwise `Void(Abandoned)`.
- **If the listen-server host leaves or crashes**: session end; Round processes nothing more; log flushed `SessionEnded`; no result for anyone (Session F6 bounds the client message).
- **If an AI-driven bound pawn stands at an objective or in a bush**: it never interacts or toggles concealment (R17 constraint); a held Interact was already released at takeover; if concealed it stays concealed until killed or released to the crowd, and then Concealment's natural exit applies.
- **If a hold completes for a participant who disconnected in the same tick**: `Valid(i)` requires `Connected` and human control, so the completion is voided; the IIF has already turned the disconnect into a release.

**Latency and replication**
- **If a client receives `Ended` later than others**: it may have sent commands after the latch; they return `Rejected_Latched` (`NotNow` to the owner). Nothing executed after the latch is ever shown, because nothing executes.
- **If a late joiner's first replicated `Phase` equals the class default**: value 0 is `Unknown`, so the handler runs; clients also read the struct at begin-play.
- **If a client builds a clock from `PhaseEnteredServerTime`**: the field is zero during `Active`, so the public struct is no clock source; a client can still timestamp the phase change locally, which is why the binding rule is that no presentation may show elapsed Active time (AC 31).
- **If the host resets immediately after a win**: the reset is held until `EndedMinDwellSec` (3 s), so every client receives `Ended`; `LastResult` survives into the lobby.
- **If a client's pawns have not all arrived at `ActiveAtServerTime`**: that client keeps its loading screen until it holds `ExpectedPawnCount` pawns or `RevealPawnWaitMaxSec` has passed, whichever is first (and holds its own pawn unless its own state is no longer `Active`; R7); the round is already Active for everyone else. The rest of its pawns appear as they arrive, kind-blind (cross-review S-B4).
- **If a strike decides the round while another strike's death write is still pending**: the earlier write still plays after the round-end step (ASN-R21); the pawn falls while the city is stopping (R9).
- **If a player presses a command between the latch and the round-end step**: `Rejected_Latched`; its `NotNow` reply is sent at the round-end step, so no reply announces the end before the blow (R9).
- **If a superseded connection's commands are still in the mailbox when the rejoiner's controller takes over**: they carry the old `ConnectionGeneration` and are `Dropped(StaleConnection)`.

**Pillar 1 leak cases**
- **If a disconnected player's ping, `bIsInactive` flag or PlayerState presence would change on other clients**: forbidden; Session rule 25 (a)-(b) owns the fix (ping not replicated, PlayerState kept until travel) [V that `CompressedPing` replicates by default: must be disabled]. Round's grace and hand-over add nothing visible.
- **If player pawns spawn in a block before or after NPCs**: forbidden; F5's single shuffled frame.
- **If the takeover makes a pawn stop dead or sprint into a wall**: forbidden; R17.1 blend rule and Acceptance Criteria 28a/28b (kinematics and blind observer).
- **If Round code reads `IsPlayer` to choose a socket, start cell or spawn slot**: forbidden; server assert (R23).
- **If a takeover, release, informant re-pawn or pawn-loss respawn changes the pawn's replicated `PlayerState` or `Controller` on a non-team client**: forbidden (R23 (f)); caught by AC 27b's property-delta test.
- **If the host's HUD shows any value not in `FRoundPublicState` or C-TeamView**: a host-parity defect, caught by the compile-time include check.

**Testing and abuse**
- **If a debug command is issued in a shipping build**: it does not exist (compiled out).
- **If a non-host client runs `stealth.round.start` through a development `ServerExecRPC`**: unreachable, because the command lives only on the game instance; and the entry point would return `NotHost`.
- **If a host aborts a round they are losing**: allowed by default (`bHostMayAbortActive`); no winner, no score, everyone sees "The round was called off", the log records who; revisited for the Dedicated Server.

## Visual/Audio Requirements

*`art-director` not consulted: Round is a `Core` framework (Visual/Audio is optional for the category and the visual surface is two screens). The art bible (`design/art/art-bible.md`, sections 2.3 briefing state and 7 UI/HUD) owns the look; this section only states the constraints Round places on it. Provisional until the HUD and Lobby UI GDDs exist.*

- **Briefing / loading state (Preparing).** One neutral screen for every participant, the host included, from travel start until `Active` (Session rule 11.8). It shows no world view, no player count, no team roster other than the viewer's own team, no progress bar that varies per participant, and no loading text that differs between participants (a differing screen would be a timing tell). The art bible 2.3 briefing treatment applies.
- **Active reveal.** The first frame of `Active` is the world already populated: players and NPCs are in place, emerging from the same sockets (Level Socket Contract R6.3). There is no fade-in per pawn, no spawn effect, and no sound that marks a player pawn.
- **End banner (Ended).** A neutral text banner per viewer: "Victory" or "Defeat" in the viewer's own terms, plus a one-line reason using exactly the approved phrasings: "completed the final objective" or "every rival eliminated for the round" (also for a forfeit, `EndReason = ForfeitElimination`, which is never named to players); after a host abort or a void, "The round was called off". No team colours for opponents, no per-participant list in MVP. The polished reveal is Vertical Slice (Post-Round Reveal & Results).
- **Audio.** One short round-start stinger and one round-end stinger, both non-diegetic, identical for every viewer, played at the phase change. No audio cue for any individual elimination, disconnect, or count change (that would be a remote detector, Pillar 1). Cue content is owned by Event Feedback & Cues and Diegetic Audio (VS).
- **Forbidden presentation (testable):** any element that shows elapsed Active time, a countdown, an enemy count, a remaining-rival icon, or an elimination-progress bar (Roster R6; Pillar 4).

## Game Feel

Round is infrastructure, so its feel is about **synchrony and finality**, not responsiveness.

- **The start is one instant.** Every client leaves the briefing state at the same scheduled server time (`ActiveAtServerTime`, R7). The target is that the spread between the first and last client revealing is bounded by the clock-sync error (AC 30a), not by the network one-way delay; a client still loading pawns lifts late on its own, never delaying others beyond `WorldReadyTimeoutSec`; measured in the 3-client test.
- **The end is one instant.** At the latch the server stops accepting discrete commands for every pawn; commands that arrive after the latch are dropped, not "almost counted". Nothing visible changes until the round-end step (for a strike win, the deciding strike's death write, so the city goes still as the blow lands, never just before it; R9): then the banner appears, every pawn, NPCs included, comes to a stop through the normal stop-and-blend path, and in-progress holds release through the IIF release blend, never a snap or a freeze. The player must never see an action animate that the server then discards as post-latch.
- **A returning player is quiet.** Rejoin shows only the neutral loading screen and then the world. No banner, no sound and no notice goes to anyone else (Session rule 24).
- **No tuning-by-feel numbers** exist in Round beyond the timing keys above; the 12-minute pacing target is measured (F7), not felt.

## UI Requirements

Round has **no player-facing UI of its own** beyond two minimal surfaces. Everything else belongs to the Lobby UI (VS) and HUD GDDs.

1. **Host controls (MVP, console and a minimal host-only panel).** `stealth.round.start`, `stealth.round.reset`, `stealth.round.abort`, `stealth.round.swapteam <name>`, `stealth.round.remove <name>`, `stealth.round.preset <name>`; each executes on the authority only, shows a one-line result (`Started`, or the `FStartRefusal` code and any listed participant names) to the host, and is not available to non-host clients. A host panel with Start and Reset buttons is optional for the first playable.
2. **End banner (HUD, MVP).** Reads `FRoundResult` and `bCalledOff` from `FRoundPublicState`. Text only. Non-host clients in Lobby see one plain line, "The host will begin the next round"; no text containing "waiting for players" or a player count beyond the lobby's `n / capacity` (Session rule 24) is shown.

> **UX Flag -- Round & Match Framework**: the end banner, the briefing screen and the host panel are UI surfaces. In Phase 4 (Pre-Production) run `/ux-design` for the briefing/loading screen and the end banner before writing epics; stories should cite `design/ux/[screen].md`.

Presentation rules (binding on HUD, Lobby UI and Post-Round Reveal): read `FRoundPublicState` and the viewer's own Roster C-TeamView only; never show elapsed Active time, a countdown or opponent counts before `Ended`; never show who disconnected or when, except to teammates through C-TeamView; "eliminated" appears only as "eliminated for the round" and the words "dead", "lives", "revive", "spectator", "ghost" never appear (Roster language rule).
