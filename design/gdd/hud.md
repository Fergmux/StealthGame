## Summary

The HUD is the quiet, client-side layer that shows a player only what is theirs to know: their own wrong-target tally and brace readiness, the result of their own strike at the moment the blade lands, their house's stage card and race steps, one plain line when an ally falls or is reconnecting, the team's pings drawn on the ground, and a round-end banner with a two-line text summary of team totals. It owns no data and no rule; it reads only the player's owner data, their team view and the public round state, and its central job is to give the core loop feedback clarity (Pillar 3) and race pressure (Pillar 4) without ever identifying, counting, locating or reacting to a non-ally person (Pillar 1), which it proves with an automated leak suite.

## Overview

The HUD is the client-side presentation layer that turns the viewer's own private state, their team's shared state and the round's public state into a small set of screen-space plates, rings, cards and lines. It owns **no gameplay rule and no data**: every value it shows is already held by the viewing client through exactly three carriers (the Information Visibility Policy's R4/R10 host-parity rule): the viewer's **owner data** on their PlayerController (Assassination's `FStrikeResult` / `FDefenceResult` and cooldown end, Penalty's wrong-target tally, exile flag and respawn due time, IIF hold status and reply codes, the owner's own concealment flag), the viewer's **C-TeamView** (own state and team, teammates' states, Objectives' team view: stage card, own steps, `RivalPrelimCount`), and the GameState's **`FRoundPublicState`** (phase, round index, match score, round result). What the HUD owns is the *when, where and how* of showing them: the element inventory per viewer state, the anchors and layering (including the teammate-marker layer of system 17), the timelines that keep feedback in step with the world (result plates at the attacker's local contact frame, the exile card a beat later, a removal line on allies' screens only once the ally's marker retires), and the player-facing wording.

**What the player gets.** In a game with no radar, the HUD is deliberately quiet: in the bottom-left corner, the player's wrong-target tally and brace indicator; at the top centre, the team's stage card and race steps; at the top right, at most three team lines; at the lower centre, one result plate after each strike; at round end, a one-line banner. The centre of the screen is always empty. Everything else the player needs to read lives in the world (posture, pace, a tend, a brace), and the HUD never restates or amplifies it.

**Why it exists.** Without it the core loop has no feedback clarity (game concept, Flow State: "kill confirmation after an attack; strike counter; team objective progress"), a wrong read would not visibly *stick* (Pillar 3), and the race would have no felt pressure (Pillar 4). Without its constraints it would be the easiest place to break Pillar 1: art bible 7.1's rule, *"nothing on the screen is ever about a person who is not an ally"*, is enforced here as testable leak rules (HUD-R2, HUD-R3, and the AC-HUD leak suite). The implementation mechanism (UMG layers versus an `AHUD` canvas pass, view-model shape) is ADR material and is not decided here (-> UI ADR, shared with Teammate Markers OQ6).

## Player Fantasy

**"Only what is yours to know."** The HUD should feel like the private notebook of an assassin standing in a sunlit square: a few calm marks in the corner of the eye, never a radar. It knows your mistakes, your readiness, your house's progress and your allies' fate, and nothing else. When you strike, it waits until your blade lands to tell you what you struck, in one quiet plate that reads the same whoever you meant to kill. When a citizen you misread falls, a square fills in red and stays filled. When your house moves a stage closer, a marigold step settles in. It never tells you where to look, who to fear or how long the round has run; that is the crowd's job and your eyes'.

The player moments to anchor to:
1. **The verdict.** Twenty seconds of watching, one strike, and at the instant of contact the plate: "Target eliminated". Or "Wrong target", and a square fills. The HUD makes the read *matter* (P3) without ever having helped make it (P1).
2. **The quiet corner.** Bracing, the ring fills in four still steps; afterwards a slow sweep counts back to readiness. Nothing ever warns you that someone is coming (ASN-R6): the HUD protects anticipation by staying silent.
3. **The race felt, not seen.** "Rival house" fills a step a minute or two after the rival moved: pressure without a reveal (P4).
4. **The fall, and the role.** Cut down, you see the city hold its breath, then a card that tells you what you are now, and the corner brackets of an informant. Your allies read one plain line. Nobody else learns anything.

*Tone and language.* Level, civic, unhurried (art bible 7.6 "plaster setting, not a game show"); short declarative words; never "dead", "lives", "revive", "spectator", "ghost", "detected", "spotted" or a stealth-meter vocabulary (Roster language rule, IIF language rule). Aesthetics served: Challenge (feedback clarity of the read), Fellowship (team steps and lines), Fantasy (the diegetic, civic language keeps the player in the assassin's role), Sensation by restraint (the city stays the picture).

*Framing confirmed by the creative-director at CD-GDD-ALIGN (alternatives offered and not adopted: "A ledger, not a lens"; "The city keeps its secrets; you keep your accounts").*

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

*Specialists consulted (full review mode; single-agent fan-out rule of this run): one combined panel brief to `ux-designer` (primary) carrying the `ue-umg-specialist`, `art-director`, `systems-designer` and `qa-lead` lenses. Where the panel changed an author proposal, the rule says so.* Element ids (HUD-E01 to HUD-E22; the teammate markers, system 17, are listed there as `(17)`) are defined in UI Requirements; formulas F1-F10 in Formulas.

**A. What the HUD may read and show**

**HUD-R1. Three carriers, nothing else** (IVP R4, R10; Roster R6 host parity). Every HUD value comes from (a) the viewer's own owner data on their PlayerController, (b) the viewer's C-TeamView (including Objectives' `FObjectiveTeamView`), (c) `FRoundPublicState` and the public tuning snapshot (`GetClientTuning()`), or (d) purely local client facts: the owner's own camera, input bindings, own RTT, own command history (`ClientCommandSeq`) and settings. The HUD module never calls `CanViewerKnow`, never includes a server store's query header (Policy, Roster, Round, LSC, Penalty, Objectives; AC-IVP-17), never iterates world pawns, and never reads a pawn's `PlayerState`, `Controller`, team or kind. The one pawn-derived input it reads is the **owner's own pawn** (`FActionState {Pose, Seq, ContactServerTime}` of the pawn handle captured when the owner's command was sent, F2; the own role change through Assassination's client-local `OnRoleChangePresented` dispatch at the pawn's `DeathServerTime`, ASN-R23) and, for teammates only, the pawn resolved from a C-TeamView `PawnRef` (through the Teammate Markers retire event `OnAllyMarkerRetired`, TM-R14, HUD-R10). It also consumes Camera's `GetViewState()` (`Live` / `Neutral`, CS-R15). The listen-server host's HUD obeys the same rule and reads the same carriers (host parity; AC-HUD-20).

**HUD-R2. Nothing on screen identifies, counts or locates a non-ally person** (art bible 7.1, reworded at CD-GDD-ALIGN: *anything shown in response to one, the strike hint or the result of your own strike, is identical for every kind of person until your own strike resolves*; HUD-R3). There is no element anchored to, sized by, coloured by or timed by any non-ally pawn, and nothing triggered by one except the strike hint, whose trigger is identical for every rendered non-ally pawn. The only HUD elements anchored in the world are the teammate markers (system 17), the team ping rings (HUD-E22, Informant Pings), the interact ring and the context prompt. The markers anchor to **allies** (the viewer's own team, never a non-ally); the ping rings anchor to a **ground point** the sender chose, never to a pawn (PNG-R1: the server never receives a pawn identity with a ping; cross-review C-W33 2026-10-25); the interact ring and the context prompt anchor to **objects** (a court fixture, a bush). None anchors to a non-ally person. A result plate states the outcome of the viewer's own action ("Target eliminated" describes the viewer's strike, not a person on screen) and identifies nobody.

**HUD-R3. Subject-kind parity (the leak rule).** For any two worlds that differ only in the kind (NPC, enemy player, enemy informant) or team of a non-ally subject the viewer interacts with or looks at, the HUD's view-model and widget tree are identical on every frame **before** the owner-only result is due (F2 show time), and afterwards differ only in the result plate's text and stamp (`RivalEliminated` vs `WrongTarget`; never between an NPC and an informant victim, which are byte-identical, Roster R10). This covers: the strike hint (shown for every rendered non-ally pawn in reach, IIF UI-4), the lockout hint suppression (HUD-R8), the brace indicator, plate entrance timing and size, cue events, the tally (HUD-R7) and the feed. Verified by the view-model diff suite (AC-HUD-01 to AC-HUD-04).

**HUD-R4. Layers and draw order** (art bible 7.1; Teammate Markers UI Requirements; panel). From bottom to top, all drawn after post-process (no mood grade, concealment grade, vignette or snap ever darkens the HUD):
1. **L0 world-anchored layer** (ZOrder 10): team ping rings (HUD-E22, beneath markers; Informant Pings owns content, MVP since 2026-10-25), teammate markers (system 17 owns content), the interact ring (HUD-E12) and the context prompt (HUD-E13); constant pixel size, edge-clamped; collapsed while `GetViewState() = Neutral`.
2. **Fade layer** (ZOrder 20): a full-screen black widget **owned by the HUD and driven by Camera** (Camera decides when and how fast, CS-R12/CS-R15, and publishes the per-frame alpha through `GetScreenFade()`; the HUD only renders it; ADR-0021 §3). Camera's engine fade (`StartCameraFade`) and post-process do not darken UMG, so the black that must cover L0 but not L1 has to be a UMG layer (review, ue-umg-specialist; CR-HUD-10).
3. **L1 persistent plates** (ZOrder 30): bottom-left cluster (brace indicator, tally, rejection plate), top-centre cluster (stage card, own and rival steps), top-right team feed, informant brackets, latency icon.
4. **L2 transient lower-centre column** (ZOrder 40): Result slot at 70 % height (result plates, defence plate, informant plate) and Notice slot below it (strike hint, one-time third-person hint, returning countdown).
5. **L3 cards and banners** (ZOrder 50): removal card, end banner, called-off line, lobby line.
6. **L4 status overlays** (ZOrder 60): Session & Travel's local connection and loading overlays, **display-only** (message, spinner, progress, countdown; never a button), `HitTestInvisible`. D15 (ADR-0021 L4 split): every layer below ZOrder 100 is display-only; an overlay that needs buttons (Cancel, OK) pushes a Session-owned modal at ZOrder 100 (below).
7. Menus and Session's status action modal (ZOrder 100, above everything).
So in the neutral view (black screen while awaiting a pawn) L1-L4 remain readable (CS-R12.2 "black + HUD plates") and world-anchored elements vanish with the world. The ZOrder integers are the contract; gaps leave room. Every HUD layer is a plain overlay, `HitTestInvisible`, never a `UCommonActivatableWidget` (an activatable widget's input config could change the mapping context mid-strike); CommonUI is used only for the menu root (ZOrder 100, a Common UI activatable stack). **L4 and D15 (ADR-0021 L4 split):** L4 holds only the display part of Session's status overlays, as a plain `HitTestInvisible` overlay; "no input below layer 100" is absolute, so no HUD layer and no L4 overlay ever hosts a button, a hit-testable widget or an activatable (a lint fails the build otherwise, ADR-0021 §1). A status overlay that needs buttons (the Cancel of a connection-lost or loading state, the OK of an ended session) is split in two: the display part stays on L4, and its action part is a Session-owned modal (`UStealthStatusActionModal`, a `UCommonActivatableWidget`) that Session pushes onto the menu root stack at ZOrder 100 through `PushMenu`, so it gets Common UI focus and `IMC_Menu` routing exactly like any menu. The HUD owns neither the modal's content nor when it is pushed (Session does; never during a live strike); it is the one Session element above L4 (ADR-0021 §1; review, ue-umg-specialist).

**HUD-R5. Anchors and slots** (art bible 7.1; F1). Inside a safe rectangle inset by `HudSafeMarginPct` (5 %): **top-centre** = objectives cluster; **bottom-left** = brace indicator, tally, rejection plate (stacked upward); **top-right** = team feed (max `HudFeedLineCount` lines); **lower-centre column** = Result slot (centre at 70 % height) and Notice slot (below it); **frame** = informant brackets at the four corners; the **screen centre is always empty of persistent elements** at every text scale (cards are transient and may sit in the central band, art bible 7.3 row 11). Persistent corner area ≤ `HudPersistentAreaLimitPct` (8 %) at 100 % scales (F1). One layout for every viewer, team, role and state (art bible 7.7 rule 1): an element that does not apply is collapsed, and a persistent element's slot is never reused by a different element (no reflow). The two shared transient slots (Result, Notice) are the only exceptions, and their occupancy follows the HUD-R13 priority list. The area budget holds for every supported aspect from 16:10 to 32:9 (F1); narrower aspects (4:3, 5:4) keep every rule except the area cap, like 200 % text.

**B. When things appear**

**HUD-R6. Result plates at the local contact frame, one timing for every outcome** (ASN-R19, UI-ASN-1, UI-ASN-4; resolves the timing half of OQ-NKP-6). When an `FStrikeResult` arrives for the owner's command `ClientCommandSeq`, the plate (HUD-E07) is shown at the F2 show time: the later of the owner's own strike contact frame mapped to local time and the RPC's arrival, never earlier, capped against clock error by `HudResultPlateMaxHoldBackSec`. Every outcome (`RivalEliminated` "Target eliminated" (renamed from "Target exiled" by ruling D29: "exile" is reserved for the strike-limit removal) with the stepped-square stamp; `WrongTarget` "Wrong target" with the upward Cochineal triangle; `NoContact` "No contact", Bone only; `Reversed` "Reversed" with the triangle) uses the same plate size, slot, entrance, hold and exit: **250 ms stepped in, `HudResultPlateHoldSec` 3.0 s hold, 600 ms out** (art bible 7.3 row 8 and 7.6; F3). The IIF UI-5 timing (400 ms / 1.5 s / 600 ms) applies only to rejection plates. The reversing defender's "Strike reversed" plate (HUD-E08, `FDefenceResult`, stepped-square stamp per UI-ASN-4) uses the same timeline and **never shows before the defender's view of the contact** (ASN-R6). *(Revised at design review: the interim arrival-plus-hold rule is replaced by Assassination's settled rule ASN-R19/R23 and kept only as the fallback; CR-HUD-11 withdrawn, OQ3 closed.)* `FDefenceResult` arrives with the resolving drain, up to `StrikeContactSec` (+ `BushStrikeEmergeSec` from a bush) before contact, and carries no time. The defender plate is held `Pending` until the defender's **own** `FActionState` Counter write (`Pose = Counter`, written at the death write, `ContactServerTime − StrikeNetLeadSec`) is observed, and is shown at F2's show time computed from that write's `ContactServerTime`; if no Counter write is observed by `t_arrival + HudResultPlateMaxHoldBackSec`, it shows then (lost-data fallback, always at or after contact by I-HUD-1). The brace indicator's End on a reversal is presented when `ServerNowEst` reaches the consumed brace's `FHoldStatus.ServerEndTime` (= `ContactServerTime`, ASN-R23), never on receipt; any reply to a defender command during `Countering` (`Busy`) is held to the defender plate's show time. So the defender's HUD changes nothing before contact (AC-HUD-05). Duplicate or out-of-order results (same or lower `ClientCommandSeq`, compared as the IIF pair `(PossessionEpoch, Seq)`) are dropped; a late, never-shown result is **never** dropped in live play (F2; PEN-R16).

**HUD-R6a. One presented own state** (added at design review; ASN-R6, ASN-R23; closes the slot-time paths). Every HUD predicate about the owner's own role reads one client-local value, `PresentedOwnState` (`Active` / `Informant`), never the raw C-TeamView own `State`. It changes from `Active` to `Informant` only at the own removal presentation: `OnRoleChangePresented` (Assassinated, ReversedAgainst; the pawn's `DeathServerTime`, or the arrival of a late `FDeathState`), the exile card time (StrikeLimit, F4), or, for a rejoin after the grace, at once. Its readers: the strike hint, the context prompt and interact ring, the brace indicator, the tally freeze, the informant brackets, the informant plate, the state machine and HUD-R8. Outcome-caused owner data that reaches the client before that moment (the own `State`, an `FHoldStatus` cancel of a hold the victim was in, `NotNow` replies to its commands, the tally re-push, `bExiled`) is held and presented at that moment, never on receipt. Until then the HUD keeps running exactly as in `ActiveAlive` (the cooldown sweep and numeral keep moving, a tend ring keeps its prediction), so a frozen or cancelled element can never announce the outcome early. Exception (ruling D1): when `OnRoleChangePresented` fires for the owner's own fall, the strike hint, the prompts and the interact ring are hidden at once (the player already sees the fall; a live prompt on a falling pawn is a false affordance); the brace indicator stays until the card.

**HUD-R7. The tally moves with the plate, never before** (UI-NKP-1, PEN-R14, PEN-R15). The displayed wrong-target tally (HUD-E02) changes only at the show time of the plate whose `FStrikeResult.TallyAfter` carries the increment, with the 300 ms stepped square fill and a one-step numeral change. A C-TeamView owner re-push of the tally that arrives before its plate is held (the HUD keeps the last shown value) and is used directly only when no result plate is pending (a rebuild after reconnect, travel or a late join), only if its `LastResultSeq` is at least the sequence of the last plate shown, and never lowers the displayed tally within a round (Penalty CR-12 b). **The re-push fields `{WrongTargetTally, LastResultSeq}` on the C-TeamView owner part were approved by the user 2026-10-25** (Penalty CR-19, Roster R6). The HUD also keeps a client-local copy of the last shown tally keyed by `RoundEpoch` (a client-local store that outlives the PlayerController) as a display cache: it bridges a rebuild until the re-push lands and never overrides a re-push that passes the gate above; a new `RoundEpoch` shows 0. Line text **"Wrong targets: {n}"**; `L` squares filled up to `min(n, L)`; the static upward danger triangle from `n ≥ L − 1` (F6). The limit is never written as a number (the squares encode it; panel: a deliberate choice, recorded). While `PresentedOwnState = Informant`, the numeral and squares are frozen at their last shown value and stay visible as a record, and the danger triangle is removed (ruling D4: an informant has no strike left to lose) (IVP R2, PEN-R16). The tally replaces the concept's "strike counter for NPC kills" (confirmed by the user 2026-10-25; Penalty OQ-NKP-1 closed, CR-15 applied to the concept).

**HUD-R8. Strike hint and lockout suppression.** The strike hint (HUD-E14, "[bound glyph] Strike", IIF UI-4) is computed locally from the camera aim ray, rendering, reach and the owner's own state only. It is hidden while the owner's own action slot is `Striking` (the 1.75 s lockout), while any card is up, and from the owner's own fall (HUD-R6a); all three conditions are owner-local, and the first two are identical for every outcome. "Own state legal" reads `PresentedOwnState`, never the raw C-TeamView state (HUD-R6a). It is never shown for a spot, a bush or a marked ally, and its predicate reads no team, controller, kind or concealment field (panel).

**HUD-R9. Own elimination: nothing before the fall, then one card** (ASN UI-ASN-5, PEN-R16, Camera CS-R12.1). The owner's own `State → Informant` arrives in C-TeamView at the resolving slot, up to `StrikeContactSec` before anything is visible. The HUD gates all own-elimination presentation (removal card, informant brackets, hiding the brace indicator) to the F4 card time, through `PresentedOwnState` (HUD-R6a):
- **Assassinated or ReversedAgainst** (the owner's pawn falls): the fall start is Assassination's client-local `OnRoleChangePresented` dispatch (the pawn's `DeathServerTime`, or the late `FDeathState`'s arrival, ASN-R23); the strike hint, prompts and ring hide then (HUD-R6a), and the card enters `HudRemovalCardDelaySec` (0.8 s) later. If the dispatch has not fired within `HudOwnRemovalHoldCapSec` of the State arrival (lost death state, pawn-loss recovery), the HUD treats the cap as the fall start. A reversed attacker first sees its own "Reversed" plate at its contact frame (HUD-R6); the card then preempts the plate.
- **StrikeLimit (exile)**: the card enters at the WrongTarget plate's show time + `ExileBeatSec` (Penalty, 0.8 s), gated on `bExiled` and the causing `ClientCommandSeq` (PEN-R15). The plate is never dropped for lateness (F2), so a late `FStrikeResult` delays the card; Camera's `ExileCardTimeoutSec` fallback fades the view meanwhile, and the card shows when the data lands (PEN-R16 edge case). The HUD fires a client-local `OnRemovalCardEntered` event at every card entrance; Camera's exile `ViewRelease` is raised by it (Penalty CR-10, Camera CS-R12.1).
- **If the own removal ends the round** (`Phase = Ended` arrives while the own removal is pending or showing): no removal card is shown (or a showing card exits over `HudPreemptExitSec`); the banner follows F10, which never shows before the own fall start (ruling D12).
- When the card enters, a plate still in the Result slot leaves over `HudPreemptExitSec` (0.25 s, stepped), so after 0.25 s only one message is on screen (panel: no two text blocks at once; changes Penalty's "cross-fade" wording, CR-HUD-6).
- Card text (OQ5 closed 2026-10-25, D11; answers Penalty OQ-NKP-4): title **"Informant"** for every cause; subtitle exile **"Too many wrong targets"**, assassinated or reversed **"Eliminated for the round"** (Roster language rule); second line **"You watch for your team now."** Opaque Cochineal Deep title bar with Bone text, upward stepped triangle, ≤ 30 % of the screen, central band; 600 ms in, `HudRemovalCardHoldSec` 4 s hold, 800 ms collapse into the informant brackets (art bible 7.3 row 11, 7.6). Shown once, never repeated (a reconnect does not replay it).
- **Informant killed** (`InformantKilled`, Penalty PEN-R16 / CR-18, applied at reconciliation 2026-10-24; Camera CS-R12.1 implements the view): an embodied informant has no `State` change, so there is **no removal card** and no exile cue; the view stays on the own falling body and Camera fades it from `DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec`; the returning countdown (HUD-E11) appears only when that fade completes. The reason value is client-local; the contract change was approved by the user 2026-10-25 (Penalty OQ-NKP-12 `InformantKilled` half closed, CR-18).
- No element names the killer, its kind or its team; there is no kill cam (Camera CS-R12.1).

**HUD-R10. Team removal line on allies' screens** (UI-NKP-5, art bible 7.3 row 12; panel change). When a teammate's C-TeamView `State` changes `Active → Informant`, allies (Active and Informant viewers) get one feed line **"{Ally name} — eliminated for the round"**, identical text for every removal reason (strike, reversal, exile, forfeit). It is shown at the later of the State's arrival and that ally's `OnAllyMarkerRetired(PublicHandle)` (Teammate Markers TM-R14, which fires exactly once per retirement, also for an undrawn marker, and immediately if no marker existed), so the line enters together with the marker's exit fade. That moment is the marker's hold end (about `TeammateMarkerRetireHoldSec` after the State, i.e. close to the blow, not after it); allies are entitled to the fact (IVP R3); since the user adopted `RetireAtServerTime` (2026-10-25, TM OQ3) the marker's hold end, and so the line, follows the exact blow automatically (half-way through the marker's exit fade at contact). Marker retirement alone (for example a release to the crowd on disconnect) never creates a line. Allies may infer the reason from the world (the fall or a body that keeps walking); allies are entitled to ally state (IVP R3), so this is accepted. Opponents receive nothing (no carrier exists). A `RoundEnded` state written to every record by Round's `EndRoundAll` at `Ended` (Round R9) is never a removal: it creates no line and no card, and the viewer state follows the phase. The name comes from C-Public `DisplayName` by the ally's `PublicHandle` (streamer alias in VS); an unresolved name reads "Ally". **Round-end rule (cross-review S-W2 2026-10-25):** once `Phase = Ended` arrives (the round-end step, Round R9), the HUD creates no feed line of any kind and discards every line still waiting: a removal line pending on `OnAllyMarkerRetired`, an entry in F8's pending queue, and a line due to a State change or flag change that arrives after the phase. `FRoundResult.TeamSummary` already counts every ally eliminated at that step (Round R9 (a)), so the banner's summary is the record and no late line is needed; lines already showing fade with the feed cluster over 600 ms when the banner enters (States, `Ended`). The ally's marker completes any pending exit at once under the banner (Teammate Markers TM-R6 step 6), so no marker outlives the decision to drop its line.

**HUD-R10a. Teammate reconnecting lines** (added by user decision 2026-10-25; Session OQ7 and Teammate Markers OQ1 closed; reworked by ruling D23, cross-review S-B1 2026-10-25). The HUD reads two Team-audience fields of a teammate's C-TeamView entry: `bReconnecting` (Roster R6) and `ReconnectEndCause` (a `uint8` enum: `None`, `Rejoined`, `GraceExpired`, `Cleared`; Roster R6 writes it in the same net update as every clear of the flag, and it is `None` while the flag is true or was never set). The Roster clears `bReconnecting` **only on an event, never by a timer** (D23): `Rejoined` = the rejoin completed (the rejoiner possessed), `GraceExpired` = grace expiry, `Cleared` = a silent clear when the participant record is removed (leave, kick) or at round end (`EndRoundAll`). An `Active` ally killed while disconnected becomes an Informant but stays flagged (no clear); an informant ally that stays disconnected stays flagged until one of those events; a superseding rejoin keeps the flag until the rejoin completes. The HUD acts only on an **observed edge** of `bReconnecting` in an entry update it receives, and reads the cause from that same update (never the cause field alone, so a rebuild replays nothing):
- `false → true`: one feed line **"{Ally name} — reconnecting"**.
- `true → false` with `Rejoined`: one line **"{Ally name} — back"**. This is the only path to a "back" line.
- `true → false` with `GraceExpired`: one line **"{Ally name} — disconnected"** (never "back"), **unless** the same entry update also carries the ally's `Active → Informant` State change (the grace expiry converting an `Active` ally, Round R17.2, "forfeit"): then HUD-R10's **"{Ally name} — eliminated for the round"** is that ally's only line and the "— disconnected" line is suppressed. Design choice (D23 interaction, logged): one fact gets one line, and "disconnected" next to "eliminated for the round" would be a second, weaker statement of the same event (HUD-R10 already words every removal, forfeit included, identically); so "— disconnected" appears for an **informant ally** whose grace has expired and who is still away (no State change at that instant), which tells the team that this informant is not about to return.
- `true → false` with `Cleared` (record removed by leave or kick, or round end) or with `None` (defensive): **no line**.
The lines are identical for every cause (crash, network loss, quit), carry no time, ping or reason, use the F8 queue and timings, and are never shown on the disconnected player's own screen (its own status is Session's L4 overlay) or on any opponent's screen (no carrier exists). Rebuilds replay none (HUD-R17); a viewer that rebuilds while an ally's flag is true shows no line, and the ally's marker label carries the "reconnecting" suffix (Teammate Markers TM-R11; the markers read the flag alone, never `ReconnectEndCause`). Roster writes the flag false outside `Active` (cause `Cleared`), so no line appears in `Preparing`, `Ended` or `Reset`; at `Ended` the HUD-R10 round-end rule drops pending lines anyway.

**C. Accessibility and settings hooks (MVP behaviour, VS controls)**

**HUD-R16. Scaling and comfort.** UI scale (75-150 %), text scale (100/125/150/200 %), Reduce motion, Reduce screen effects, high-contrast mode and the streamer alias mask are **client-local settings** (TDC CR-14), not tuning keys; in MVP they are fixed at 100 % / 100 % / off / off / off / off and the HUD must already honour them (F1, F3) so that Settings & Accessibility (VS) only exposes them. Text never falls below 18 px (22 px for changing numerals; Cochineal Bright text never below 28 px) at 1080p for the PC-first MVP (ruling D7; raising floors for a TV/console target is OQ14); text below 28 px has ≥ 4.5:1 contrast against its plate and larger text and non-text graphics ≥ 3:1 (Bone Dim included); plates grow with text and never truncate a message (names are ellipsised at the plate width), and no meaning is carried by colour alone (art bible 4.7). Read holds (result, defence, rejection and informant plates, the card hold, feed line life and dwell) are multiplied by `HudReadHoldMult` (1.0 in MVP), which Settings & Accessibility (VS) ties to text scale (ruling D11). No accessibility setting may add directional or player-only information (IVP R15).

**HUD-R17. Reset and rebuild.** On a `RoundEpoch` change (C-TeamView), `OnRosterReset` effects, travel or reconnect, the HUD discards every pending or showing transient element (plates, cards, feed lines, pending tally holds) and rebuilds persistent elements from the carriers' current values with no animation and no replayed cue; `PresentedOwnState` is rebuilt from the current own `State` at once (no card). The duplicate-seq ring and `seq_lastShown` are cleared on reconnect, travel and `RoundEpoch` change (a new PlayerController may restart `ClientCommandSeq`). RPC-only feedback whose show time passed during a disconnect is not replayed after the rebuild (F2 replay window applies to rebuilds only). Team ping entries present at a rebuild are marked shown and never drawn; entries accepted after it show normally (Informant Pings PNG-R10).

## Formulas

*systems-designer lens folded into the combined panel brief (single-agent rule). All formulas are client-local presentation; none feeds a gameplay outcome. Times are seconds unless stated; `ServerNowEst` is the client's synchronised server-clock estimate, `EstimatedServerNow()` (ADR-0002 §9: owner-only ping/pong clock exchange, min-RTT offset, slewed and monotonic; read wherever older text says "GameState server time"; shared with Assassination, Camera and Markers).*

### F1. Layout scale, safe rectangle and corner-area budget

The hud_layout formula is defined as:

`u = s_ui · H / 1080`; `m = HudSafeMarginPct / 100`; `SafeRect = [m·W, (1 − m)·W] × [m·H, (1 − m)·H]`; `y_result = 0.70 · H` (Result-slot centre); `y_notice,top = y_result + 0.5 · h_result,actual · u + g · u` (`h_result,actual` = 64 for one line, more when the plate wraps); `size_text = max(floor_px · H / 1080, base_px · u · s_text)`; `A_persist = Σ_i (w_i · h_i · u²) / (W · H) ≤ HudPersistentAreaLimitPct / 100` at `s_ui = s_text = 1`, checked at the **narrowest supported aspect** (16:10; wider aspects only lower it); build-time lint: `HudPersistentAreaLimitPct ≥ A_persist(16:10)`. Note: at `s_ui` 0.75 text stays at its floor while plates shrink 25 %, so plates must size to their text (they grow, never clip).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| W, H | int (px) | 1280-7680, 720-4320 | calculated (viewport) | Viewport size |
| s_ui | float | 0.75-1.5 | client setting (CR-14; fixed 1.0 in MVP) | UI scale |
| s_text | float | 1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 2.0 | client setting (CR-14; fixed 1.0 in MVP) | Text scale |
| u | float | 0.5-6.0 | calculated | Pixels per 1080p reference pixel |
| HudSafeMarginPct | float | 3-10 | data file | Safe margin, percent of each dimension |
| floor_px | int (px @1080p) | 18 / 22 / 28 | constant (art bible 7.4) | Text floor: any text / changing numerals / Cochineal Bright text |
| base_px | int (px @1080p) | 18-48 | constant (art bible 7.4 hierarchy) | Designed size of the text level |
| w_i, h_i | int (px @1080p) | per element | constant (UX spec) | Reference footprint of persistent element i |
| h_result, g | int (px @1080p) | 64, 12 | constant (UX spec) | Result-plate height and slot gap |
| HudPersistentAreaLimitPct | float | 7-12 | data file | Persistent area cap at 100 % scales, narrowest supported aspect |

**Output Range:** `u` 0.5 (720p at 75 %) to 6.0 (4320p at 150 %); text never below its floor. `A_persist` is checked at 100 % scales (art bible 7.1) and 16:10; at `s_text ≥ 1.25`, or on an unsupported narrow aspect (4:3 ≈ 9.2 %, 5:4 ≈ 9.8 %), it may exceed the cap (art bible 7.4), never the centre-empty rule.
**Example:** 1920×1080, `s_ui` 1: `u` = 1, safe rect [96, 1824]×[54, 1026], Result slot centred at y = 756, Notice slot top at 756 + 32 + 12 = 800. Persistent footprints (the brace cluster now includes its "Brace" label and glyph): bottom-left cluster 360×120 (43,200 px²), top-centre cluster 520×110 (57,200), top-right feed 3 lines 420×100 (42,000): `A_persist` = 142,400 / 2,073,600 = 6.9 % ≤ 8 %. At 2560×1440, `u` = 1.333 and the same layout covers the same 6.9 %. At 1920×1200 (16:10), `u` = 1.111: 142,400 × 1.235 / 2,304,000 = 7.6 % ≤ 8 % (the binding case). **Open (second review, 2026-10-25):** the three footprints above are the reference clusters only. The lint must sum every persistent element in any one viewer state, including the informant brackets (HUD-E09) and the latency icon (HUD-E19), whose reference footprints the UX spec must add; the 16:10 margin is only 0.4 percentage points (about 9,000 px² at 1920×1200), so the UX spec re-runs this budget before the limit is frozen (an informant has no brace indicator, so its bottom-left cluster is smaller; the UX spec confirms the worst state rather than assuming it).

### F2. Result show time (local contact frame)

The result_show formula is defined as:

`offset = ServerNowEst − LocalNow` (one slewed offset shared with animation, Camera and Markers; it never jumps, OQ8); `t_c,local = ContactServerTime − offset`, where `ContactServerTime` is read **from the result itself** (`FStrikeResult.ContactServerTime`, Owner-only, added by user decision 2026-10-25, ASN-R19) and, only when absent (a defender's `FDefenceResult`, which carries no time, or a malformed result), from the matching `FActionState` below; `t_show = min( max(t_c,local, t_arrival), t_arrival + HudResultPlateMaxHoldBackSec )`.
**Matching fallback** (revised at design review; UE does not order an RPC against property replication; since 2026-10-25 used only for `FDefenceResult` and as a fallback): `ContactServerTime` is then read from the **matching** `FActionState`: on the pawn handle captured when the command was sent (not the currently possessed pawn, which an exile releases to the crowd at the slot), a strike pose (defender: `Counter`) whose `Seq` differs from the last consumed write and whose `ContactServerTime` is later than the last consumed contact time (one strike can be in flight: `AttackAttemptLockoutSec` 1.75 s exceeds the longest contact delay, I-ASN-3). Until it is observed, the result stays `Pending` and `t_c,local` is undefined; if it has not been observed by `t_arrival + HudResultPlateMaxHoldBackSec`, `t_show` is that cap (fallback, still at or after contact by I-HUD-1). This positional match is the defined fallback rule (Assassination ASN-R23, applied for CR-HUD-14 at reconciliation 2026-10-24: `Seq` never carries the command sequence, which would be a P1 tell on an All-audience struct); for the attacker's plate it is superseded as primary by `FStrikeResult.ContactServerTime` (OQ15 closed 2026-10-25).
**Drops:** a result is dropped only if `seq ≤ seq_lastShown` or `seq` ∈ the last-8 ring (pairs `(PossessionEpoch, Seq)` compared lexicographically). In live play an unshown, non-duplicate result is **never dropped for lateness**: if processed after its `t_show`, it shows on processing (PEN-R16; ruling D9). `HudReplayWindowSec` applies only to rebuilds (HUD-R17): a result whose `t_show` passed more than `HudReplayWindowSec` before a rebuild completes is not shown.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| ContactServerTime | double | ≥ 0 | `FStrikeResult.ContactServerTime` (Owner, ASN-R19; primary since 2026-10-25); else the matching `FActionState` of the pawn captured at the press (All, ASN-R21; defender's Counter and fallback) | Server time of the owner's strike (or Counter) contact |
| t_arrival | double | ≥ 0 | calculated (client clock) | Arrival of `FStrikeResult` |
| offset | double | any | calculated | Server-to-local clock offset |
| HudResultPlateMaxHoldBackSec | float | 1.25-2.0 (hard minimum 1.25, decision D8) | data file (1.25) | Cap on holding a received result for the contact frame. The RPC leaves at the resolving slot and contact is `StrikeContactSec` (0.4; bush strike + `BushStrikeEmergeSec` 0.35) later, so the cap must cover the longest contact delay: invariant I-HUD-1 `≥ StrikeContactSec + BushStrikeEmergeSec` (≤ 1.2 by Assassination I-ASN-3) |
| HudReplayWindowSec | float | 0.5-3.0 | data file (1.0) | Rebuilds only (HUD-R17): how long after its show time a result pending across a rebuild may still be shown |
| seq, seq_lastShown | `(PossessionEpoch, Seq)` | — | owner command history (IIF R5) | `ClientCommandSeq` of the result / of the last shown plate; ring cleared on rebuild |

**Output Range:** `t_show − t_arrival` ∈ [0, HoldBack] when the result is processed on arrival; a result processed late (hitch) shows on processing. Because `FStrikeResult` is enqueued at the end of the resolving drain for every outcome (ASN-R19, IIF R6), `t_arrival` and `t_c,local` do not depend on the outcome or the victim's kind, so `t_show` does not either (HUD-R3).
**Example:** the strike executes at server 5.000 and the RPC arrives at local 1.000 (offset 4.050, one-way 50 ms); `ContactServerTime` 5.400 → `t_c,local` = 1.350 → plate at 1.350 (held 350 ms for the lunge). A bush strike with contact at 5.750 → plate at 1.700. With a 600 ms one-way delay the RPC arrives after contact and the plate shows on arrival. With a corrupt offset giving `t_c,local` = 3.0, the cap shows it at 2.250. With `FStrikeResult.ContactServerTime` 5.400 in the payload the plate shows at 1.350 whatever the `FActionState` timing. Fallback path (defender, or no time in the result): if the RPC arrives at 1.000 but the matching `FActionState` only at 1.100 (contact 5.400), the plate stays `Pending` and shows at 1.350; if it never arrives, at 2.250. After a 1.5 s hitch the result first processed at 2.850 shows at 2.850 (never dropped).

### F3. Plate and card timelines (alpha)

The plate_timeline formula is defined as:

`α(t) = in(t − t0)` for `t − t0 < T_in`; `1` until `t0 + T_in + T_hold`; then `1 − clamp((t − t_out) / T_out, 0, 1)`; stepped entrance `in(x) = floor(k · clamp(x / T_in, 0, 1)) / k` with `k = 4`; eased entrance (non-urgent plates) `in(x) = easeOut(clamp(x / T_in))` plus an 8 px settle in whole 4 px steps. **Read hold:** every `T_hold` below is multiplied by `HudReadHoldMult` (1.0 in MVP; HUD-R16). **Preempt:** `t_out := t_preempt`, `T_out := HudPreemptExitSec`. **Reduce motion:** `T_in = T_out = 0`, no settle; `T_hold` unchanged. Timelines are evaluated by sampling `now − t0` each frame (deterministic, time-dilation-safe), not by widget animation assets (OQ8).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| t0 | double | — | calculated (F2, F4) | Show time |
| T_in, T_hold, T_out (result plate) | float | 0.1-0.5, 1.0-6.0, 0.2-1.0 | data file (`HudResultPlateInSec` 0.25, `HudResultPlateHoldSec` 3.0, `HudResultPlateOutSec` 0.6) | Result and defence plates (stepped) |
| T_in, T_hold, T_out (rejection plate) | float | 0.4 / 1.5 / 0.6 | constant (IIF UI-5) | Rejection plates (eased) |
| T_in, T_hold, T_out (informant plate) | float | 0.4 / 2.0 / 0.6 | constant (IIF UI-6) | Informant plate (eased) |
| T_in, T_hold, T_out (removal card) | float | 0.3-1.0, 2-6, 0.4-1.2 | data file (`HudRemovalCardInSec` 0.6, `HudRemovalCardHoldSec` 4.0, `HudRemovalCardOutSec` 0.8) | Card (eased; the out phase is the collapse into the brackets) |
| HudPreemptExitSec | float | 0.1-0.4 | data file | Exit when a higher-priority element takes the slot |
| k | int | 4 | constant (art bible 7.6 stepped) | Entrance steps |

**Output Range:** result plate on screen `0.25 + 3.0 + 0.6 = 3.85` s; card `0.6 + 4.0 + 0.8 = 5.4` s. Identical for every result outcome (HUD-R6).
**Example:** "Wrong target" shown at t0 = 10.00: α = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 at 10.0625/10.125/10.1875/10.25; holds to 13.25; gone at 13.85. On an exile (F4) the card enters at 10.80 and preempts the plate: plate α falls from 1 to 0 over 10.80-11.05; card fully in at 11.40.

### F4. Own removal time and card time

The own_removal formula is defined as:

- Exile: `t_card = t_show(WrongTarget plate of seq_exile) + ExileBeatSec`.
- Assassinated / ReversedAgainst: `t_fall` = the local time `OnRoleChangePresented` fires (`DeathServerTime − offset`, or the late `FDeathState`'s arrival, ASN-R23) if it fires by `t_state + HudOwnRemovalHoldCapSec`, else `t_fall = t_state + HudOwnRemovalHoldCapSec`; `t_card = t_fall + HudRemovalCardDelaySec`. Strike hint, prompts and ring hide at `t_fall` (HUD-R6a).
- `t_removal` (`PresentedOwnState := Informant`: brace indicator hidden, informant brackets and frozen tally presented) `= t_card`.
- If `Phase = Ended` arrives before `t_card`, no card is shown (HUD-R9, F10).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| t_state | double | — | calculated | Arrival of own `State = Informant` in C-TeamView |
| DeathServerTime | double | — | own pawn `FDeathState` (All) | Server time the owner's fall starts (= contact time) |
| ExileBeatSec | float | 0.75-0.95 (Penalty safe range, narrowed by the cross-review fix 2026-10-25, C-W17: I-HUD-6 and W-NKP-5) | Penalty key (default 0.8) | Beat between the exile plate and the card |
| HudRemovalCardDelaySec | float | 0.5-1.2 (hard) | data file (0.8) | Beat between own fall start and the card; I-HUD-3 **rejects** the preset if ≠ `ExileBeatSec` |
| HudOwnRemovalHoldCapSec | float | 1.25-2.0 (hard minimum 1.25, decision D8) | data file (1.25) | Longest wait for the own death state after the State arrives; invariant I-HUD-2 `≥ StrikeContactSec + BushStrikeEmergeSec` (the latest a fall can start after the slot) |

**Output Range:** `t_card − t_state` ∈ [≈ `StrikeContactSec` − one-way latency + 0.8, `HudOwnRemovalHoldCapSec` + 0.8] (≥ 0.8 s when the state arrives after the fall start). For an exile, `t_card − t_show` = `ExileBeatSec`; a late `FStrikeResult` delays the card with its plate (Camera's `ExileCardTimeoutSec` fallback fades the view meanwhile, PEN-R16). The camera fade to black starts when the binding is retired (`DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec`, 1.2 s) and lasts 0.5 s; the card (L3) sits above it.
**Example:** a victim's State arrives at 20.00; death state observed at 20.25 with fall start mapped to 20.40 → card enters 21.20, fully in 21.80; camera black from 22.10; card collapses into the brackets at 25.80-26.60. For an exile whose plate showed at 10.00, the card enters at 10.80.

### F5. Brace cooldown numeral and sweep

The brace_cooldown formula is defined as:

`T_end = LastPrepareActivation + DefenceCooldownSec`; `r = max(0, T_end − ServerNowEst)`; numeral `"Brace in " + floor(ceil(r) / 60) + ":" + pad2(ceil(r) mod 60)` while `r > 0`, held at "Brace in 0:01" while `r = 0` and `ServerNowEst < T_end + 0.5 · RTT_own`, then Ready (ruling D5; host `RTT_own` = 0); `sweep = clamp((ServerNowEst − LastPrepareActivation) / DefenceCooldownSec, 0, 1)`; Protected segments `seg = min(4, floor(4 · (ServerNowEst − t_confirm) / DefenceDurationSec) + 1)`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| LastPrepareActivation | double | — | owner data (IIF R12 / ASN-R18 cooldown end) | Server time the brace was accepted |
| DefenceCooldownSec | float | 30-120 (default 60) | Assassination key (All) | Cooldown from activation |
| DefenceDurationSec | float | 3-5 (default 4) | Assassination key (All) | Brace window |
| t_confirm | double | — | calculated (`FHoldStatus` Protected) | Server-confirmed start |

**Output Range:** numeral from `DefenceCooldownSec` (default "1:00"; up to "2:00" at the 120 s hard maximum) down to 0:01, then Ready; the sweep is drawn only in Cooling (from End), 0-1 linear (the only linear motion, art bible 7.6). The numeral text updates only when `ceil(r)` changes. Reduce motion: the sweep advances in 1 s steps.
**Example:** activation at server 100.0, now 117.2 → `r` = 42.8 → "Brace in 0:43", sweep 0.287. A brace released at 101.5 still counts from 100.0 (stamped at activation, ASN-R3).

### F6. Tally display

The tally_display formula is defined as:

`filled = min(n, L)`; `triangle = (n ≥ L − 1) ∧ PresentedOwnState = Active` (frozen informant tallies show no triangle, ruling D4); line `"Wrong targets: " + n`; `n` = `TallyAfter` of the latest shown plate (HUD-R7), else the owner re-push when no plate is pending.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| n | uint8 | 0-255 | Penalty (`TallyAfter`, owner) | Wrong-target tally |
| L | int | 2-5 (default 3; TDC hard range; 1 only in a test preset with `bTestAllowsStrikeLimitOne`) | Penalty key `NpcKillStrikeLimit` (public, latched) | Strike limit; number of squares |

**Output Range:** `filled` 0-L; triangle from `n = L − 1` (exile on the next kill is possible only from there) and kept for `n > L`; nothing escalates beyond it.
**Example (L = 3):** n = 0 → 0 squares; n = 1 → 1; n = 2 → 2 + triangle; n = 3 → 3 + triangle; n = 4 (still Active: an earlier victim was an informant or graced) → 3 + triangle, line "Wrong targets: 4". With the warning preset L = 1, the triangle shows from n = 0. L = 5: n = 3 → 3 squares, no triangle; n = 4 → 4 + triangle. n = 255 (saturated) → L squares + triangle, line "Wrong targets: 255". After an exile at n = 3, the informant sees "Wrong targets: 3", 3 squares, no triangle.

### F7. Returning countdown

The returning_countdown formula is defined as:

`s = ceil(T_due − ServerNowEst)`; text `"Returning in " + s` while `s ≥ 1`; `"Returning"` while `s ≤ 0` and no pawn is bound. `T_due` is the Owner-only field of the C-TeamView owner part (user decision 2026-10-25; Roster R6, ADR-0001 §6, ADR-0019 §6), so the countdown is **live**: it counts down in whole seconds from the carried value and resumes correctly after a reclaim or rejoin; it is never shown to any other connection. The `s ≤ 0` form stays a static "Returning" (no count-up, Penalty PEN-R14.3).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| T_due | double | — | Penalty owner data (`T_due`, PEN-R15) | Server time the takeover becomes due |

**Output Range:** whole seconds down to 1, then "Returning" until the takeover. The countdown is first visible when the removal card has collapsed (about 6.2 s after the fall at defaults: 0.8 + 0.6 + 4.0 + 0.8), so the first value shown is about `InformantRespawnDelaySec` − 6 (≈ 9 at the default 15); after a killed informant's fall (no card, Penalty CR-18) it is first visible when Camera's fade completes (`DeathFallSec` + `NoPawnFadeSec` ≈ 1.7 s after the fall start), so the first value is about `InformantRespawnDelaySec` − 2; the text at 0 is the static "Returning" (no ellipsis, animation, count-up or searching wording; Penalty UI-NKP-4, CR-12 c); at `InformantRespawnDelaySec` ≤ 6 only "Returning" is ever seen after a card (Penalty waits per LSC starvation rules; median total ≤ 30 s, Penalty F3). No pulse and no colour change near zero.
**Example:** `T_due` = 230.0, now 216.4 → "Returning in 14"; at 229.1 → "Returning in 1"; at 230.3 with no pawn yet → "Returning".

### F8. Team feed queue

The team_feed formula is defined as:

Each line has `t_in` (F3 eased entrance) and lives `HudFeedLineLifeSec`; at most `HudFeedLineCount` lines show; a new line, when full, evicts the oldest line only if that line has shown for ≥ `HudFeedMinDwellSec`, else it waits in a pending queue of at most 4 (the most a team of 5 can produce at once) until that holds. At `Phase = Ended` the pending queue is discarded and no new line is created (HUD-R10 round-end rule, cross-review S-W2).

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| HudFeedLineCount | int | 1-4 | data file (3; art bible cap 3) | Visible lines |
| HudFeedLineLifeSec | float | 3-12 | data file (6.0) | Line lifetime |
| HudFeedMinDwellSec | float | 0.5-4 | data file (2.0) | Shortest time before eviction |

**Output Range:** a line shows for `HudFeedMinDwellSec` to `HudFeedLineLifeSec` (× `HudReadHoldMult`); for a burst of `b` simultaneous lines with `c = HudFeedLineCount`, the last line waits `ceil((b − c) / c) · HudFeedMinDwellSec` when `b > c` (defaults `b` = 4, `c` = 3: 2 s; worst `c` = 1, dwell 4.0: 12 s).
**Example:** allies A, B, C, D removed in one update at 50.0: A, B, C show at 50.0; D waits until 52.0, evicts A; B and C expire at 56.0, D at 58.0. With `c` = 1: A at 50.0, B at 52.0, C at 54.0, D at 56.0 (each evicting the previous), D gone at 62.0.

### F9. Objectives cluster update

The objectives_update formula is defined as:

`rival_shown = clamp(max(RivalPrelimCount, rival_shown_prev), 0, 2)` within one `RoundEpoch` (reset to 0 on a new epoch); any change `Δ ≥ 1` animates all new steps in one `HudObjectiveStepSec` fill with one tick; own steps `filled_own = StageIndex` mapped Stage1→0, Stage2→1, Final→2 (the Final step fills only in `Ended` by the win, never earlier); stage card turn-over over `HudObjectiveStepSec`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| RivalPrelimCount | uint8 | 0-2 | Objectives team view (OC-12) | Released rival preliminary completions |
| StageIndex | enum | Stage1, Stage2, Final | Objectives team view | Own team progress |
| HudObjectiveStepSec | float | 0.25-1.0 | data file (0.5; art bible 7.6 "objective step added") | Step fill and card turn |

**Output Range:** rival steps 0-2, monotonic within a round; own steps 0-2 during `Active`.
**Example:** both rival releases land on one grid tick: `RivalPrelimCount` 0 → 2, both steps fill together in 0.5 s with a single tick.

### F10. End banner show time

The banner_show formula is defined as:

`t_floor = max(t_phase, t_plate_show if an own result or defence plate is Pending at t_phase, t_fall if the viewer's own removal is pending at t_phase, t_card + HudRemovalCardInSec if a card is entering)`; `t_want = max(t_floor, t_plate_show + HudBannerMinPlateSec if that plate exists, t_phase + HudObjectiveStepSec if the viewer's team won by the final objective)`; `t_banner = max(t_floor, min(t_want, t_phase + HudBannerMaxDelaySec))`. The floor is never cut: no banner before the viewer's own plate or own fall (ASN-R6, P3); the cap only trims the readability and fill extras (ruling D8). The cluster fade starts at `t_banner`.

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| t_phase | double | — | calculated | Arrival of `Phase = Ended` |
| t_plate_show | double | — | F2 | Show time of the owner's pending plate |
| t_fall | double | — | F4 | Own fall start (`OnRoleChangePresented`) when the own removal is pending |
| HudBannerMinPlateSec | float | 0-1.5 | data file (1.0) | Minimum time the last own plate is readable before the banner |
| HudBannerMaxDelaySec | float | 1.0-2.5 | data file (1.6; CD OQ10 ceiling) | Cap on the extras (readability, final fill); never cuts the floor |

**Output Range:** `t_banner − t_phase` ∈ [0, max(HudBannerMaxDelaySec, t_floor − t_phase)]; at defaults ≤ 1.6 s whenever the floor is within 1.6 s (always for an elimination win, which Round stages to the death write, 0.15 s before contact). Only the owner's own pending feedback delays its own banner, so the delay depends on nothing about any other participant. Bystanders (no own plate or fall) show the banner at `t_phase`. For an elimination win `t_phase` is the arrival of the broadcast that Round staged to the deciding strike's death write, `DeathServerTime − StrikeNetLeadSec` on the server clock (ASN-R23; `DeathServerTime` = `ContactServerTime`), so a bystander's banner can lead the fall it can see by **at most `StrikeNetLeadSec` (0.15 s at defaults)** and never shows before the death write; one-way delay only makes it later, and the listen host, which has no replication delay, sits exactly at that bound. This is the accepted I-ASN-4 residual and the bound AC-ASN-35 asserts (ruling D21, cross-review C-B1): the HUD holds nothing for a strike it was not part of, because it has no contact time for it.
**Example:** a teammate completes the final objective and `Ended` arrives at 300.00 while the attacker's own plate is due at 300.38: plate at 300.38, `t_want` = max(301.38, 300.50) = 301.38, cap 301.60 → banner at 301.38. An elimination win: `Ended` (staged to the death write) arrives at 500.00; the attacker's plate at contact 500.15, banner at 501.15; the victim (last of its team) falls at 500.15 → banner at 500.15, no removal card. A final-objective win at 400.00: the winners' Final step fills to 400.50, banner at 400.50; losers at 400.00. Every other viewer shows the banner at its own `t_phase`.

## Edge Cases

*systems-designer and qa-lead lenses folded into the panel brief.*

**Leak cases (Pillar 1)**
- **If the attacker strikes an NPC in one run and an enemy player (or an enemy informant) in another:** the view-model and widget tree are identical on every frame until `t_show` (F2), including the strike-hint hide during `Striking`, the absence of any brace or notice change, and cue events; afterwards only the plate text and stamp differ for `RivalEliminated` vs `WrongTarget`, and nothing at all differs for an NPC vs an informant victim (HUD-R3; AC-HUD-01, AC-HUD-02).
- **If the tally re-push or the owner's `bExiled` arrives before the plate:** the HUD holds the last shown value; a square never fills before the plate, which would otherwise reveal "wrong target" up to `StrikeContactSec` early (HUD-R7; AC-HUD-03).
- **If a strike is aimed at the viewer:** no HUD element, sound event, haptic or layout change occurs before contact (the cooldown sweep and any tend ring keep running, HUD-R6a); the viewer's first HUD change is its own fall presentation (`OnRoleChangePresented`: hint, prompts and ring hide), or, if braced, the reversal's plate and brace End at the HUD-R6 defender show time (ASN-R6; AC-HUD-05).
- **If the victim is mid-tend or mid-bush-entry when struck:** the server's `FHoldStatus` cancel (at the death write, 0.15 s before contact) is held: the ring keeps its prediction until `OnRoleChangePresented`, then hides with the other interactive elements (HUD-R6a).
- **If a braced defender presses a command between the reversal's slot and the contact (its slot is already `Countering`):** the `Busy` reply is held to the defender show time like the plate (HUD-R6); it never appears earlier.
- **If the exiling attacker's cooldown sweep is running when its third wrong kill resolves:** it keeps running until the card (HUD-R6a); nothing on its HUD differs from a non-exiling `WrongTarget` before the plate (HUD-R3).
- **If the viewer looks at, approaches or stands beside any non-ally pawn (NPC, enemy, enemy informant, concealed or not):** nothing on the HUD changes except the strike hint, whose predicate is identical for every rendered non-ally pawn in reach; for a concealed pawn there is no actor, so no hint (IIF UI-4).
- **If the strike hint's implementation reads a team, controller, kind, concealment or `PlayerState` field:** build failure (include check, AC-HUD-11); the predicate is (rendered ∧ in reach ∧ not a marked ally ∧ own state legal).
- **If a removal line could reach an opponent:** impossible by carrier (C-TeamView is per team); the HUD has no code path that creates a feed line from anything but the viewer's own C-TeamView (AC-HUD-11).
- **If the end banner arrives before the attacker's contact frame of the winning strike:** the attacker's banner waits for its own plate (F10) and the victim's for its own fall (no removal card); other viewers show it on arrival, never before the death write and so up to `StrikeNetLeadSec` before the deciding strike's `DeathServerTime` (the I-ASN-4 residual; F10, AC-ASN-35, ruling D21). The banner carries only `FRoundResult`, already public to all at `Ended` (IVP R3).
- **If a streamer broadcasts their HUD:** it shows only their own and their allies' knowledge (art bible 7.7); ally names are masked by aliases in VS streamer mode. Accepted leak L23 (ally markers and names identify the streamer's team to a watcher).
- **If the rival steps rise while an opponent is visibly tending:** the rise is delayed by 30-195 s on a global grid (Objectives F4); the HUD adds no delay or timing of its own and plays the tick at arrival, so it never sharpens the correlation.
- **If the listen-server host plays:** the host HUD reads the same carriers (HUD-R1) and has no RTT, so the latency icon never shows; result show times follow F2 with a near-zero arrival delay, which is accepted host parity (IVP R10, AC-HUD-20).

**Timing and network cases**
- **If `FStrikeResult` arrives after the local contact frame, or a hitch delays its processing:** the plate shows when processed (F2); it is never dropped for lateness in live play, so the verdict, the tally step and an exile card are never lost (PEN-R16).
- **If `FStrikeResult` arrives before the matching `FActionState`:** no effect on the attacker's plate, which reads `FStrikeResult.ContactServerTime` and shows at its F2 time whatever the `FActionState` timing. Only the fallback paths wait: the defender's `FDefenceResult` (no time in the payload) stays `Pending` until the defender's own Counter write is observed, and a result without a time (malformed) until the strike write is observed (never reading the previous strike's contact time); both show at `t_arrival + HudResultPlateMaxHoldBackSec` if the write never arrives (F2).
- **If the clock offset re-syncs:** the shared offset is slewed, never stepped, so pending show times drift smoothly with the animation clock; a plate already showing is not restarted.
- **If two results arrive with the same or a lower `ClientCommandSeq`:** the duplicate or older one is dropped (F2 ring of 8).
- **If the owner strikes again before the previous plate exits:** impossible within the 1.75 s lockout; the next plate (≥ 1.75 s later) preempts the previous one in the Result slot via `HudPreemptExitSec`.
- **If a reversed attacker's "Reversed" plate is showing when its removal card is due:** the card preempts the plate (HUD-R9); the plate has shown for about 0.8 s.
- **If an elimination of the owner (as a victim) lands while one of its own plates is showing:** the card preempts the plate at F4.
- **If `Phase = Ended` arrives during a removal card:** the card finishes its entrance, then the banner enters (F10) and the card exits over `HudPreemptExitSec` instead of collapsing into the brackets. If `Ended` arrives before the card has entered (the own removal ended the round), no card is shown (HUD-R9).
- **If Round's `EndRoundAll` writes `RoundEnded` to every record at `Ended`:** ignored by the HUD (no feed line, no card); the phase drives the viewer state (HUD-R10). Round writes it in the same server step as the `Ended` publication (R9 round-end step), but it may arrive a net frame before `Phase = Ended`; until the phase arrives the viewer state (`ActiveAlive`, `InformantAwaiting`, ...) and `PresentedOwnState` stay as they were (cross-review S-B2 2026-10-24). A tend ring cancelled at the round-end step simply hides (no failure cue).
- **If `bCalledOff` or a `RoundEpoch` change arrives during any plate, card or feed line:** everything transient is cleared at once (no exit animation), then the called-off line or the rebuilt HUD shows (HUD-R17).
- **If the owner disconnects and rejoins inside the grace:** persistent elements rebuild from the carriers; missed plates are not replayed; the tally shows the C-TeamView owner re-push (Penalty CR-19, approved 2026-10-25), with the client-local copy bridging the frames before it lands (HUD-R7); no card (HUD-R17).
- **If the owner rejoins after the grace (now an informant):** informant presentation (brackets, frozen tally, countdown if awaiting) with no removal card (Open Question 6).
- **If an ally stays gone past the grace (an informant ally, or an `Active` ally killed while disconnected):** `bReconnecting` stays true until a Roster event (D23), so there is no clear and no "back" line for it. The "reconnecting" line showed when the flag was set; when the grace expires the Roster clears the flag with `GraceExpired` and, with no State change, the feed shows "{Ally name} — disconnected", never "back". For the killed `Active` ally the removal line came earlier, at its marker's retirement (HUD-R10), so the sequence reads reconnecting, eliminated for the round, disconnected. An `Active` ally converted by that same expiry shows only the removal line (HUD-R10a).
- **If an ally's superseding rejoin is still loading when the grace would expire:** the flag stays true until the Roster writes its clear: `Rejoined` once the rejoiner is possessed (then "{Ally name} — back"), or `GraceExpired` if the grace ran out first (then the HUD-R10a rule for that cause). "Back" never prints before possession.
- **If a flagged ally is kicked or leaves, or the round ends:** the Roster clears with `Cleared`: no line from the clear (the marker retirement and any removal line follow their own rules; at `Ended`, HUD-R10's round-end rule).
- **If an ally's removal line or marker exit falls due after the round-end step (cross-review S-W2):** the line is dropped and the marker exit completes at once under the banner (HUD-R10, F8, TM-R6 step 6); the banner's summary already counts the ally.
- **If a ping's sender is removed while its ring is live (cross-review S-I1):** the ring stays to expiry, its name label is dropped at the sender's `OnAllyMarkerRetired` (HUD-R11, PNG-R7).
- **If the own death state never arrives (packet loss, or a pawn-loss recovery):** the card enters at `t_state + HudOwnRemovalHoldCapSec + HudRemovalCardDelaySec` (F4).
- **If the countdown reaches 0 and the takeover is delayed by respawn starvation:** "Returning" stays, with no pulse, until the binding; the Camera cut and fade-in then remove the countdown (F7).
- **If an informant's body is lost (Penalty re-pawn):** back to `InformantAwaiting` with a fresh countdown; no card, no feed line (the State did not change).
- **If C-TeamView is late for a teammate's state change:** the feed line simply appears later; nothing is predicted (Roster: state is never client-predicted).
- **If a teammate is removed while the viewer is in the neutral view:** the feed line shows normally (L1 is above the fade).
- **If `RivalPrelimCount` arrives lower than the shown value (out-of-order rebuild):** the shown value never decreases within a round epoch (F9).

**Layout and accessibility cases**
- **If text scale is 200 %:** plates grow and never shrink text; a plate is capped at 60 % of the safe-rect width and wraps to at most 2 lines; the feed keeps 3 entries and at most 6 lines; the bottom-left and top-right stacks reflow outward; the centre stays empty; the 8 % cap may be exceeded (art bible 7.4).
- **If a display name is long or unresolved:** ellipsised at the feed plate width; unresolved reads "Ally". Names never appear in result plates or cards.
- **If Reduce motion is on:** every entrance and exit is instant, the 8 px settle is removed, the cooldown sweep moves in 1 s steps, durations of holds are unchanged (F3).
- **If the input device or a binding changes mid-prompt:** the glyph swaps without re-animating; an unbound action shows "Unbound" in the glyph plate.
- **If UI scale changes mid-round (VS setting):** relayout without replaying animations.
- **If the screen is ultrawide (21:9, 32:9):** the safe rect follows the viewport, but corner clusters are clamped to a centred 16:9 region of the safe rect so the eyes do not travel to the far edges (UX spec confirms; Open Question 9).
- **If the latency icon and the feed compete for the top-right corner:** the icon sits on the safe-rect edge above the feed's first line and never displaces a line.
- **If `NpcKillStrikeLimit` is 1 (warning preset, non-Shipping):** one square, triangle from n = 0 (F6).
- **If a 1v0 development round runs:** rival steps show 0 throughout (Objectives forces the signal off); nothing else changes.

## Visual/Audio Requirements

*art-director lens folded into the combined panel brief (single-agent rule); the art bible (§3.4, 4.6, 4.7, 7.1-7.7) is the approved direction and this section only states what the HUD must honour and where it changes the art bible (CR-HUD-8). `art-director` sign-off on the CR-HUD-8 list is still required before HUD epics.*

**VA-HUD-1 Palette and shape.** Opaque Obsidian Ink plates (100 %), stepped 4 px corners, 2 px stroke, 2 px Obsidian keyline on coloured glyphs; Bone / Bone Dim text. Signal hues each with one job: **Turquoise never appears in any HUD element** (it is the ally marker's, system 17); **Cochineal** only for danger and finality (tally squares, danger triangle, WrongTarget/Reversed triangle, removal card title bar, Defeat bar), never as text below 28 px; **Marigold** for progress and intel (own and rival steps, the tend ring at an own live site). Every coloured element carries a shape or fill backup (art bible 4.7): squares + numeral + triangle; stamp vs triangle vs no glyph; step count + labels; the live-site ring's four cardinal ticks (HUD-R13). Contrast: text below 28 px ≥ 4.5:1 on its plate, larger text and graphics ≥ 3:1 (HUD-R16).

**VA-HUD-2 Typography.** Functional sans (OFL reference class), Regular 400 / Semibold 600, tabular numerals for the tally, cooldown and countdown (≥ 22 px Semibold); display accent only ≥ 28 px for the card title and the banner header; no Light or Thin; all text on opaque plates (art bible 7.4). The display face is not required for MVP (functional face Semibold fallback is allowed).

**VA-HUD-3 Motion.** Slow, level, quiet (art bible 7.6): non-urgent plates 400 ms ease-out + 8 px stepped settle / 600 ms out; urgent entrances one-shot stepped 250 ms (tally square fill 300 ms); objective step 500 ms; cooldown sweep the only linear motion; no pulse faster than 0.5 Hz, no scale pop, shake, bounce or overshoot; nothing moves more than 8 px except cards; Reduce motion instant (F3).

**VA-HUD-4 Photosensitivity.** The removal moment (transient grade, plate, preempt exit, card, camera fade) is budgeted as **one sequence** within 3 luminance reversals per second and 10 % relative luminance change over a large area (art bible 7.6 rule 7). HUD's contribution is bounded by HUD-R9 (one text block after 0.25 s, card ≤ 30 % of the screen, never full-bleed red). The sequence is checked with a photosensitivity analysis tool before VS (OQ-NKP-6, shared with Penalty; AC-HUD-18 ADVISORY).

**VA-HUD-5 Rendering constraints (technical-artist / ue-umg lens; mechanism -> UI ADR).** Flat, resolution-scaled, opaque materials; no bloom, emissive glow or screen-space distortion; drawn after post-process; world-anchored elements at constant pixel size, no depth test, edge-clamped; all widgets pre-created at level load and collapsed when unused, so the widget tree never grows or shrinks in response to the world (supports HUD-R3); HUD cost target ≤ 0.3 ms game thread and ≤ 0.2 ms render thread on the reference GPU (RTX 3060-class, 1080p), ADVISORY until profiled.

**VA-HUD-6 Audio (audio-director owns the sounds; the HUD owns when cue events fire).** All HUD cues are owner-only (or team-only for team lines), 2D on the UI bus, never spatialised, never played on a pawn: tally tick (one per increment) and threshold tick (when `n ≥ L − 1`), chosen by `n` alone, fired with the plate; one result cue per plate fired at `t_show`, with a distinct cue id per outcome (CR-HUD-15; legal because it follows contact), identical for `WrongTarget` whatever the victim kind; exile/removal cue fired with the card, never with the plate; stage tone on an own counted completion; rival tick on a rival-steps rise (team-only, once per update); brace ready-again tone (owner); ping-refusal tone (owner-only, ruling D26: one soft rejection tone, cue id `PingConcealedRefused`, when `IA_Ping` is refused because the owner is concealed; text twin the "Not now" plate; limited by `RejectPlateCooldownSec`; the ping arrival tick is Informant Pings' own, VA-PNG-5); no cue on being targeted, ever (ASN-R6); no round clock sound. Every cue's text twin is its plate, card or line (art bible 7.8). Mood: dry, short, then silence; no buzzer, fanfare or musical sting.

## Game Feel

*Not a reflex game:* the HUD's job is to make the verdict unmistakable and then get out of the way.

| Moment | Target | Number |
|---|---|---|
| Strike to verdict | The plate lands with the blade, never before it; it feels like the strike's own sound | `t_show` = local contact frame (F2); stepped entrance 250 ms; never earlier than contact, at most 1 frame after it when the RPC is on time |
| Wrong read sticks | One square fills with the plate, one tick, numeral changes in one step (no roll-up) | 300 ms square fill at `t_show` |
| Bracing | The press feels acknowledged at once; protection is shown only when real | local pending emphasis ≤ 1 frame after press; Protected on server confirmation (≈ RTT); 4 stepped segments of 1 s |
| Cooling | Calm, honest real time | linear sweep; numeral steps once per second |
| Own removal | A held breath, then a role, not a punishment screen | fall visible ≥ 0.8 s before the card; card fully in before the screen is black (≤ 1.7 s after fall start) |
| Race pressure | Small, late, quiet | rival step fills in 500 ms with one tick; no flash |
| Stage complete | The reward is knowledge | card turns over and own step fills in 500 ms; one owner/team tone |
| Ally down | One plain line, top-right | at the ally marker's exit (F8, HUD-R10) |
| Round end | One instant for everyone except a viewer whose own strike or fall is still landing | banner on `Ended` arrival; attacker's own plate readable ≥ 1.0 s first; a victim's banner at its own fall; winners' Final step fills first (0.5 s); total extra ≤ 1.6 s (F10) |

**Responsiveness:** HUD presentation adds no more than one frame (16.7 ms at 60 fps) to any carrier update except where a rule deliberately holds it (F2, F4, HUD-R7, F10). Input-to-feedback latency for prompts and the brace pending emphasis follows the IIF F5 budget.

## Acceptance Criteria

*qa-lead lens folded into the combined panel brief at authoring and again in the design-review panel (2026-10-24), which rewrote the ACs below; a dedicated qa-lead pass remains a condition before HUD stories (OQ11).* **Determinism rule for every PIE comparison:** fixed step (60 Hz, `-benchmark -fps=60` or the automation fixed-delta equivalent), identical seeds, dumps keyed by tick index with wall-clock fields excluded; "contact" oracles come from the strike, `Death_Fall` or `Counter` anim-notify tick on that client, never from the HUD's own F2 mapping; "unchanged" means equal to a **control run** with the same seed and no strike (a running cooldown sweep or tend ring is compared, not expected to freeze). Tags: type `[Logic]` automated unit test, `[Integration]` automated multi-system or PIE test, `[UI]` retained screenshot evidence in `production/qa/evidence/`; gate `BLOCKING` or `ADVISORY` (coding standards defaults). Test names use the `StealthGame.HUD.*` prefix and live under `Source/StealthPresentation/Private/Tests/HUD/` (the presenter core's module), with multi-client parity tests in `Source/StealthTestHarness/Private/` (ADR-0007/0008). The HUD exposes a serialisable **view-model dump** (every visible element id, text, glyph, fill, slot, alpha, and the cue events fired) per frame for tests; its implementation is ADR material.
