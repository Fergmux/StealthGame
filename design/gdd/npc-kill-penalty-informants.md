## Summary

NPC-Kill Penalty & Informants turns a wrong read into a cost that sticks and an elimination into a reduced role: the server counts NPC kills, exiles a player on the third (user-locked), and gives every eliminated, exiled or killed informant a new body by quietly taking over an unseen citizen near their team's spawn. The player sees only a tally of "wrong target" kills that ticks the same for a citizen and an enemy informant, so a kill never reveals who the victim was. It exists so that killing stays a protection tool rather than a default (P3, P4) while eliminated players stay in the match as watchers who look exactly like the crowd (P1).

## Overview

*Framing: both (data layer and player impact; recommended option for a `Gameplay` system, autonomous run). ADR reference: ADR-0018 (strike and encounter timing) and ADR-0019 (informant lifecycle and takeover) now exist (cross-review C-W9 2026-10-25); "-> ADR" marks name the ADR that carries the implementation choice.*

NPC-Kill Penalty & Informants ("Penalty") is the server-authoritative system that gives a wrong read its lasting cost and gives an eliminated player their reduced role. It owns three things. (1) **Strike accounting**: a server-only, per-participant count of NPC kills, fed only by Assassination's `OnPawnKilled(Victim, Killer, VictimKind, Cause)` and counted for `Cause = Strike ∧ VictimKind = Npc` alone (ASN-R11); on the `NpcKillStrikeLimit`-th NPC kill (default 3, user-locked) it requests `RequestTransition(Killer, Informant, StrikeLimit)` inside the same Round queue slot, and releases the struck-out player's surviving body to the crowd. (2) **The informant pawn lifecycle**: every time a participant needs an informant body (newly eliminated by any reason, killed as an informant, or returning to an `Active` round while awaiting a pawn) Penalty schedules and performs the one respawn path, decided rule C1: after its delay, a takeover of an unseen crowd pawn inside the team's respawn region near its `TeamSpawn`, through the Level Socket Contract's `PickRespawnPawn`, Character Pool's `TakeNpcCard`, Crowd's `TryReleaseNpc` and Shared Character's `TakeOverCrowdPawn`. No player pawn is ever spawned mid-round. (3) **What the penalised player is told**: the Owner-only strike line and the removal message ("Informant" / "Too many wrong targets", HUD OQ5), designed so that the attacker's own feedback never reveals whether a `WrongTarget` victim was an NPC or an enemy informant beyond the one deduction the locked rules make unavoidable (PEN-R14; resolves Assassination OQ-ASN-2): the player sees an Owner-only tally of `WrongTarget` plates, never the true strike count. Penalty never resolves kills (Assassination), never owns the state machine or the counts that decide elimination (Roster, Round), never chooses which pawn is eligible beyond the region rule (Level Socket Contract, Crowd, Shared Character), and never draws UI (HUD). For the player, the system is felt as a cost that is known the moment it is paid, a removal that arrives exactly on the third counted innocent, and a second life as a watcher who walks out of their own side of the city and keeps feeding the team what they see.

## Player Fantasy

**Framing: direct** (a `Gameplay` system the player feels as a cost and then plays as a role; recommended option in autonomous mode). *creative-director consulted; framing 1 of 3 chosen.*

*The city stops your hand, not your eyes.* Three times you were sure, and three times a citizen fell who had done nothing. The third time there is a beat of stillness after the plate, and then the card: **Informant**, "Too many wrong targets" (the design term for this removal is *exile*, the one event the word names; the card itself shows no such word, decision D11, and cross-review D-W10 2026-10-25 reserves "exile" for this removal alone). No buzzer, no fanfare: the held breath goes out of you. Exile is the city's judgement, quiet rather than punitive: it takes your hand at once, and gives you back its streets after a held breath of exile. You come back on your own side of the city, far from where it happened, and the walk back is part of the price. You walk out of your own team's quarter with nothing left to hide and nothing left to do but read: you cannot strike, brace, run, climb, hide or touch an objective, so watching is all that is left, and you tell your team what you see. Elimination by a rival's blade lands you in the same role. The match never lets go of you (P3: "elimination never removes you from the match").

The attacker's side of the fantasy is **uncertainty bought with blood**. Every `WrongTarget` plate ticks the same tally whether the body was a citizen or an enemy watcher, so the hunter who cuts down a suspected informant never learns from the kill itself whether it cost them anything; at most, later, they learn that *one of* their wrong targets was not a strike, never which (PEN-R14). They paid the exposure either way. The game may turn out kinder than the tally, never harsher: exile can never arrive before the tally said it could.

*Alternatives considered:* "The watcher in the crowd" (informant-only; ignores the penalty half) and "Fallen, not gone" (P3 continuity only). Framing 1 covers both halves and suits the "Held Breath" direction.

**Anchor moments** (each maps to acceptance criteria):
1. **The third innocent falls.** Plate, beat, "Informant: too many wrong targets", the frame collapses into the informant frame (AC-NKP-05, AC-NKP-20).
2. **Walking out of your team's quarter as a watcher.** You arrive in a stranger's body near your own side, unseen by any rival, and walk out at crowd pace (AC-NKP-09, AC-NKP-12).
3. **Cut down and walking out again.** Someone took a risk to silence you; a little later you are walking out of your quarter again, still a watcher (AC-NKP-09, AC-NKP-10).
4. **The hunter's doubt.** The tally ticks, and they wonder "was that a watcher?" (AC-NKP-03, AC-NKP-04).

*Pillar service*: P3 (the cost of a wrong read is shown at once and sticks; elimination becomes a reduced role, not removal; the downtime, about 15.5 s of black screen nominally and up to about 35 s when starved, then the walk back from the home quarter (F3, F5), is the largest P3 cost of the role and is intended; voice to the team continues throughout; text chat is Vertical Slice, cross-review D-W10 2026-10-25), P1 (an informant kill and an NPC kill are the same to the killer, kill by kill), P2 (an informant has no advantage left except attention, and shadowing a suspect is itself a visible deviation), Fellowship (MDA 2: the informant's value is what they tell the team).

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

*Specialists consulted (full review mode, folded into two calls per the pipeline's one-agent-at-a-time rule): creative-director (OQ-ASN-2 decision, fantasy, pillar risks), systems-designer + game-designer (rules audit, states, formulas, edge cases, knobs). Change requests to other GDDs are numbered CR-n and listed under Cross-References; this GDD does not edit other documents.*

**A. Scope**

**PEN-R1. What Penalty owns, and what it does not.** Penalty owns: the server-only **strike count** and the Owner-only **WrongTarget tally** per participant; the **exile** (strike-limit removal) request and what happens to the exiled player's surviving body; the **informant pawn lifecycle** (when and how every informant gets a body, decided rule C1); the **informant role contract** as one place that lists what an informant can and cannot do (each restriction enforced by its owner); the content and timing of the **removal message**; and Penalty's Round Event Log rows. Penalty does **not** own: who was killed and how (Assassination), the state machine, bindings and elimination counts (Roster), the queue, the win check and the due-time heap (Round), which pawns are eligible and the uniform draw (Level Socket Contract `PickRespawnPawn`), the NPC's release from the crowd (Crowd `TryReleaseNpc`), the card move (Character Pool), the possession mechanics (Shared Character `TakeOverCrowdPawn`), the camera (Camera), the audiences (Visibility Policy) or any drawing (HUD). All Penalty state is keyed by `ParticipantId`, lives in a server-only store (-> ADR), and is never a replicated property except the Owner-audience values named in PEN-R15.

**B. Strikes, tally and exile**

**PEN-R2. A strike is an NPC kill, and nothing else.** Penalty subscribes to Assassination's `OnPawnKilled(Victim, Killer, VictimKind, Cause)`, which is emitted inside the Attack's Round queue slot (ASN-R10). In that same slot, and before any later item, Penalty resolves the killer with `GetParticipant(KillerPawn)` (the attacker's pawn is still `BoundLive`) and adds one strike when **all** of these hold:
1. `Cause = Strike` (a reversal never charges anyone, ASN-R11);
2. `VictimKind = Npc` (an informant kill is not an NPC kill: user-locked; a `Player` kill is a rival elimination);
3. the killer is a bound participant whose Roster state is `Active` at the slot (ASN-R2 already voids an attacker who left `Active` earlier in the drain; this is an assert);
4. the victim is not a **graced released body** (PEN-R5).

The strike count `StrikeCount[P]` is **ServerOnly**: it never replicates to anyone, including its owner, before `Ended` (amends the Visibility Policy fact table; CR-1). `ServerOnly` protects against clients, not a listen-server host: until the Dedicated Server the host's process holds the store like every other `ServerOnly` fact (IVP R10, leak row L21, accepted for trusted playtests). A post-latch Attack is `Rejected_Latched` and never emits `OnPawnKilled`, so no strike is ever counted after the win latch.

**PEN-R3. The tally is what the player was already told.** Every executed Attack whose `FStrikeResult.Result = WrongTarget` adds one to `WrongTargetTally[P]`, whatever the victim kind and whether or not a strike was added. The tally is the only count the player ever sees (PEN-R15). It is delivered **inside** the plate's own payload, `FStrikeResult {ClientCommandSeq, Result, TallyAfter, bExiled, ContactServerTime}` (CR-2 to Assassination ASN-R19), so it is never **presented** before the local contact frame (the reply is enqueued at the end of the resolving drain and may arrive up to `StrikeContactSec` (plus `BushStrikeEmergeSec` from a bush) before contact; HUD holds it to the contact frame, ASN-R19, R23; wording per Assassination change request 11, reconciliation 2026-10-24) and an informant kill and an NPC kill produce byte-identical replies (Roster AC-PSR-52 is unchanged). **The per-strike tally travels only in `FStrikeResult`.** The owner's part of C-TeamView carries `{WrongTargetTally, LastResultSeq}` (the `ClientCommandSeq` of the last `FStrikeResult` that changed it) and is written **only** on reclaim, rejoin and `OnRosterReset`, never per strike (CR-19), so a C-TeamView update can never beat a plate to the screen. Penalty writes it through the Roster's owner-view feed `SetOwnerTally(P, WrongTargetTally[P], LastResultSeq, ExpectedEpoch)` (Roster R6; `ExpectedEpoch` is the participant's current `RoundEpoch`, so a stale write after a reset is `Rejected_StaleEpoch`); calling it only at those three points is Penalty's discipline (AC-NKP-27), which the Roster cannot police. HUD applies a re-push only when its `LastResultSeq` is at least the sequence of the last plate it has shown, and never lowers the displayed tally within a round (CR-12). **The re-push fields are approved (user decision 2026-10-25; CR-19 applied by Roster R6).** HUD additionally keeps a client-local copy of the last shown tally keyed by `RoundEpoch` as a display cache (it bridges the frames before the re-push lands; it never overrides a re-push that passes the `LastResultSeq` gate); a new `RoundEpoch` (`OnRosterReset`) shows 0 (HUD-R7). `TallyAfter` saturates at 255. Invariant: `StrikeCount[P] <= WrongTargetTally[P]` at all times (F1).

**PEN-R4. Exile on the `NpcKillStrikeLimit`-th NPC kill** (user-locked; `L` latched at `Preparing` by TDC's own latching rule, "TDC rule CR-11 Latching", not to be confused with this GDD's change request "Penalty CR-11"). When PEN-R2 brings `StrikeCount[P]` to `L` and P is `Active`, Penalty, still inside the same slot and depth-first after its own strike write (Round R11 follow-ons):
1. calls `RequestTransition(P, Informant, StrikeLimit, ExpectedEpoch)`; Round's elimination check runs on the resulting Roster event in the same slot, so the exile of a team's last `Active` player wins the round for the other team at once (Roster F4 partition (a); Round edge case);
2. the exiled player's **pawn survives** (it struck; it did not die) and is **released to the crowd in the same Roster call**, never left as a live `Retired` body: a visible, walking citizen that resolves `NotTargetable(AlreadyResolved)` would turn the next strike on it into a `NoContact` whiff, a Pillar-1 tell. This needs Roster R8/R9 to treat `StrikeLimit` as "release, not retire" inside `RequestTransition` (CR-3; `ReleaseToCrowd` today rejects a connected participant). From then on the pawn is `Unbound` and is an NPC: killing it is an NPC kill (subject to PEN-R5 grace);
3. sets `bExiled = true` in the killer's `FStrikeResult` for this Attack (the accepted residual: the owner learns of their own exile at the plate);
4. if the round did not latch in step 1 (Round phase still `Active`), the participant's informant body (PEN-R7) is scheduled with `t_lost` = this slot's server time, **not in this call stack**: the Roster event of step 1 is dispatched in the slot's Roster flush, where Round runs its elimination check and, only if the round did not latch, calls Penalty's `IInformantBodyProvider::OnInformantAwaitingBody(P, StrikeLimit, slot time)` (Round R18 note; ADR-0019 §2); after a latch Round makes no call and nothing is scheduled;
5. writes the Event Log rows (PEN-R17).

The released pawn finishes its strike lockout (`AttackAttemptLockoutSec`) under the crowd AI's restricted driver, then joins the full crowd catalogue at its next leg boundary (Crowd CNB-R10.4). Its card stays `NpcLive` with the same `AppearanceId` (Character Pool CP-12b). The participant's commands after the exile are voided `NotNow` (no pawn).

**PEN-R5. The released body: stale-read grace and exit.** Rivals who watched the exiled body make its strikes may strike it next; their read was right when they made it.
- **Stale-read grace.** A kill of a released body whose slot time is at most `StrikeOutReleaseGraceSec` (2.0 s, inclusive) after its release still produces `WrongTarget` and still ticks the attacker's tally (PEN-R3), but **adds no strike**. The attacker cannot perceive the difference, so the game is only ever kinder than the tally. The grace covers the visible strike clip (`AttackAttemptLockoutSec`, 1.75 s) plus a latency margin, so a rival who strikes the striker while it is still visibly mid-strike never pays for a correct read; it is never longer, because after the clip the body walks the crowd baseline and a kill becomes a gamble on memory, not on present behaviour (creative-director, CD-GDD-ALIGN). Beyond the grace the kill is an ordinary NPC kill (creative-director's "stale read" cost stands).
- **Exit routine.** After its lockout ends, Crowd gives the released body its next ambient leg as an `AmbientExit` (walk into an alley and be absorbed out of sight, LSC R5), so the confessed striker leaves the stage the way any citizen does (CR-4 to Crowd). The prompt exit is kept (creative-director ruling R1c, answers OQ-NKP-9): a real player who has just struck usually walks away too, and it shortens the time a witness can tail a known striker. The exit **takes the next scheduled city-wide exit slot** of Crowd's ambient exit cadence (CNB-R9.1) instead of adding one, so the city's exit rate stays at its baseline (CR-4, AC-NKP-45). The exit obeys Crowd's existing give-up rule while watched (`CrowdExitGiveUpSec`), so a rival who tails it sees an ordinary dawdle. A body in "pending exit" is ineligible for any informant takeover (LSC R5b.2; Crowd reports it with the `AlleyExit` eligibility reason until it is absorbed, CNB-R11, applied by Crowd for CR-4), so a participant can never re-adopt its own released body; `PickRespawnPawn` also takes an explicit `ExcludePawn` backstop (CR-5).

**PEN-R6. Carry-over and reset.** `StrikeCount` and `WrongTargetTally` are round-scoped and keyed by `ParticipantId`: unchanged by disconnect, reclaim, travel within the round or a takeover; cleared **only** on `OnRosterReset` (Roster R11). Once a participant is `Informant` both values are frozen (an informant cannot attack, IIF R7). An informant who was exiled has `StrikeCount = L`; one who was assassinated or reversed keeps whatever it had.

**C. Informant bodies (decided rule C1; confirmed by the user 2026-10-25)**

**PEN-R7. One respawn path for every informant body.** No participant pawn is ever spawned mid-round (Roster R9, SCL R2). Penalty gives an informant a body by **taking over an existing, unseen crowd NPC pawn inside its team's respawn region** near its `TeamSpawn` ("at their team's spawn", user-locked, read as the region of LSC R5b): cells whose walkable path to the team's own anchor is ≤ `InformantRespawnPathCm` **and** shorter than the path to the other used zone's anchor (LSC R5b.2 nearer-anchor rule, also kept by the first starvation widening to 2 × `InformantRespawnPathCm`; the second widening allows any walkable cell not within `InformantRespawnPathCm` of an enemy-used anchor, LSC R5b.5). The same path, with the same rules, serves every cause:

| Cause | `t_lost` (F2) | Body before the takeover |
|---|---|---|
| `Assassinated` / `ReversedAgainst` (Roster event, Assassination) | the kill slot's server time | dies (one body sequence, ASN-R17); the card is vacated and refilled by Crowd (CPA CP-12) |
| `StrikeLimit` (PEN-R4) | the exile slot's server time | survives, released to the crowd (PEN-R4.2) |
| Informant killed (`OnPawnKilled`, `VictimKind = Informant`) | the kill slot's server time | dies; Penalty calls `RetireBinding(VictimPawn, RetireAtServerTime, Reason = InformantKilled)` inline in the slot (Roster "not a transition"; D10: the reason is applied on `RetireBinding` and written only to the Roster's ServerOnly log), so a second strike on the falling body resolves `AlreadyResolved` (a `NoContact` on a falling body, identical for every victim kind) |
| Returning informant awaiting a pawn (Round R18, C1 extension; confirmed by the user 2026-10-25) | kept from the earlier loss (PEN-R11) | none |
| Informant body lost without a kill (KillZ, a defect) | now, with no delay (PEN-R11) | lost |

`Disconnect` grace expiry is **not** a cause: Round releases the pawn to the crowd (Round R17) and the participant is absent; on return it is a returning informant.

**PEN-R8. When: a Round timer, counted from the cause.** For every **`Active -> Informant` cause** in the table (`Assassinated`, `ReversedAgainst`, `StrikeLimit`: an applied Roster transition) Round triggers the scheduling by calling `IInformantBodyProvider::OnInformantAwaitingBody(P, Cause, t_lost)` after its elimination check (never when latched; Round R18 note). **The informant-kill cause is not a Roster transition** (Roster R8 "not a transition"), so Round has no event for it and **Penalty schedules that respawn itself, inline in the kill slot** (States-table row, ADR-0019 §1; cross-review C-W29 2026-10-25): `t_lost` is the slot time and it schedules the same `TimerFired(InformantRespawn, ...)` item by the same F2 rule, with no `OnInformantAwaitingBody` call. A returning informant arrives through `OnInformantReadmitted(P)` after its Session acknowledgement (Round R18; ADR-0019 §1). Penalty then schedules one `TimerFired(InformantRespawn, ParticipantId, ExpectedEpoch)` item in Round's due-time heap (Round R11-R12) at `T_due` (F2): `InformantRespawnDelaySec` (15 s) after `t_lost`, never earlier than the end of the dying pawn's fall plus the camera fade (ASN-R22: possession stays put through the fall). The delay is the same for every cause and every participant. While waiting the participant is "awaiting pawn" (Roster R11) and sees Camera's neutral view (CS-R12.2) with an Owner-only "returning" countdown (PEN-R16).

**A cause on a disconnected participant** (cross-review fixes 2026-10-24, finding S-B3; ruling in the decision log). If the participant's Roster `Connection` is `Disconnected` at the cause's slot (in practice an `Active` player in grace whose AI-driven pawn is assassinated; `Unresponsive` counts as connected, Roster R11's collapse, so an `Unresponsive` victim goes to Awaiting as usual), Penalty records `t_lost` = slot, schedules **no** timer and puts the participant in **Absent**. The pawn dies, falls and retires exactly as for any victim (ASN-R22). On return during `Active` the participant is an `Informant` with no pawn, so Round R18's informant path applies and reclaim from Absent schedules `T_due = max(t_lost + InformantRespawnDelaySec, now)` (PEN-R11): a returner after `T_due` is re-pawned at once, never stranded, and one inside grace goes the same way (its own pawn is dead, so Round's grace return to the own pawn does not apply). Round's pending grace timer for that participant changes nothing when it fires (already `Informant`; Round R17.4 logs it `Voided`).

**PEN-R9. How: the commit sequence, one server call stack.** When the timer item resolves, Penalty:
1. re-validates: the participant is `Informant`, has no live pawn, its Roster `Connection` is `Connected` (not `Disconnected`; Session's `Unresponsive` is included, the same test as PEN-R8's disconnected-cause rule), the epoch matches and the phase is `Active`; otherwise the item is voided (PEN-R10);
2. calls `PickRespawnPawn(Team, RequestSeq, ExcludePawn)` (LSC R5b): uniform over eligible, unseen pawns in the team's region, with the unseen test re-run at the commit tick. **This is the only random draw** (CR-6: Character Pool's `TakeNpcCard` becomes a card move on the chosen pawn, not a second draw);
3. pre-checks eligibility with the side-effect-free `CheckTakeOverEligibility(Pawn)` (the same predicate as `TakeOverCrowdPawn` step 1; ADR-0019 §4, an ADR-0010 amendment); on `NotEligible` the pawn joins the request's exclusion set and the request retries next frame;
4. calls Crowd `TryReleaseNpc(Pawn)` (CNB-R11), which re-checks atomically; Penalty is its **only** caller; on failure the pawn is excluded and the request retries next frame;
5. calls Shared Character `TakeOverCrowdPawn(Pawn, ParticipantId, ExpectedEpoch)` (SCL R2a), which performs the eligibility check and Roster `BindPawn` **on Penalty's behalf** (Penalty never calls `BindPawn` itself) and commits the AI -> player hand-over, executed at the next frame start. A `NotEligible` reply (SCL R2a.1: the pawn started running, traversing, an `ActionPose` or a hide between the pick and the takeover) returns the pawn to the crowd (`AdoptReleasedPawn`, the exact reverse of `TryReleaseNpc`), excludes it and retries next frame; it is an ordinary outcome until the step-3 pre-check exists, a defect afterwards (SCL change request 8, reconciliation 2026-10-24). A `BindPawn` rejection (stale epoch, wrong state) returns the pawn the same way and voids the request;
6. **only if the takeover returned `Applied`**, calls Character Pool `TakeNpcCard(ParticipantId, Pawn)` (card `NpcLive -> BoundLive`, `AppearanceId` untouched), then Roster `RecordAppearance`. No card move is ever committed for a failed takeover, so nothing has to be reverted; a `TakeNpcCard` failure after `Applied` keeps the takeover and reports `LedgerMismatch`.

**One pick per slot (ADR-0019 §4, 2026-10-24 ADR sync).** The pick service allows one pick per server frame, so every retry inside the commit (steps 2-5) is a next-frame retry with a due time strictly after the drain start, reusing the same `RequestSeq` (so the stream continues) and the request's exclusion set (cleared at the next regular `EmergenceRetrySec` retry). *(Order changed from "card, then takeover" to "takeover, then card" by ADR-0019; gameplay unchanged.)*

No actor, component or NetGUID is created; the pawn's `AppearanceId`, velocity and head do not change (SCL R2a.2); the takeover is invisible on the wire to every non-owner (IVP R9c, R14). The participant's teammates see their outline marker appear on the new body through C-TeamView (Teammate Markers TM-R7).

**PEN-R10. Retries, starvation and cancellation.** `PickRespawnPawn` returning `None` is retried every `EmergenceRetrySec` with LSC's widening (`RespawnStarvationSec` 10 s, then 2 × the region, then any walkable cell not near an enemy-used anchor; LSC R5b.5); the unseen test is never relaxed, a bound pawn is never taken, no pawn is ever spawned, and past both widenings the participant keeps waiting (`RespawnStarved` logged). Round's `LateJoinTakeoverTimeoutSec` view-only fallback does **not** apply to informants: Penalty owns their retries (CR-7 to Round R18). A pending or retrying request is cancelled with no effect by `OnRosterRoundEnded`, by `Reset` (a stale item returns `Rejected_StaleEpoch`; a stale `BindPawn` is rejected) and by the participant disconnecting (PEN-R11).

**PEN-R11. Disconnect, return and lost bodies.**
- An embodied informant who disconnects has its pawn released to the crowd at once (Round edge case); its `t_lost` becomes that moment.
- A returning informant (Round R18) is scheduled with `T_due = max(t_lost + InformantRespawnDelaySec, now)`: the delay that was running keeps running, so disconnecting and rejoining never shortens a respawn and is never a way to be moved home faster (F2).
- An informant body lost without a kill (fell out of the world, destroyed by a defect) is re-pawned through PEN-R9 at once with no delay and no penalty (CR-8: Round R17.9 covers only `Active` players).

**D. The informant role**

**PEN-R12. The informant contract** (user-locked; each restriction enforced by the named owner, listed here once so nothing is lost):

Tell Matrix row T7 (owned here).** An informant's body must never deviate from the walking baseline in a way no NPC shares: never seen running, vaulting, climbing, interacting, bracing or hiding (all rejected by IIF R7), with collision, capsule, avoidance and replication identical to any other Shared Pawn (SCL R3, R13). **E. What the players are told**

**PEN-R14. The informant-kill invariant, kill by kill (resolves OQ-ASN-2).** Everything the attacker perceives after one kill is identical for an NPC victim and an informant victim: the clip, audio, plate, tally increment, cues and HUD (Roster R10, AC-PSR-52 unchanged). The true strike count never reaches the attacker, and the attacker never learns anything from the kill itself. Three residuals are permitted, each named here and in Roster R10 (CR-9); creative-director approved all three (CD-GDD-ALIGN; design review rulings R1, R7):
1. **Attacker side: removal timing.** Exile fires on the `L`-th strike (an ungraced NPC kill; user-locked), so an `Active` player whose tally has reached `L` knows that at least one past `WrongTarget` victim was an informant **or** a graced released body (PEN-R5), and at tally `L + m` that at least `m + 1` were, never which. The hint is exact but rare (F4). Before any enemy is eliminated, no informant exists and the tally equals the strike count. An exile also tells the exiled player, after the fact, that all `L` counted victims were NPCs.
2. **Witness side: the struck-out striker.** An opponent who strikes a body they saw strike may receive `WrongTarget` instead of `RivalEliminated` (the body was released at the exile, PEN-R4.2), and may see that body take its prompt exit; they can infer that an enemy left `Active` by the strike limit. This reveals one fact (an enemy's removal, normally `ServerOnly` as an opponent count, IVP) and only to a witness who acted on a correct read. It follows from the locked rules (the body survives; killing it is an NPC kill), so it can be kept small but not removed; `RivalEliminated` for a graced kill was rejected (a false fact in the Event Log and the Post-Round Reveal).
3. **Informant side (Owner only): a long "Returning".** A takeover that keeps picking past the countdown tells the waiting informant, and through voice its team, that its home region's pawns are seen or otherwise ineligible. The cause is noisy (crowd density, pending exits and interaction poses also make pawns ineligible), and the HUD does not amplify it (UI-NKP-4: a static "Returning", no count-up, no widening level).

Any other perceivable difference requires a pillar-safe alternative approved by the creative director. Rejected alternatives: a live exact strike count (an informant kill would confirm "that citizen was an enemy watcher", turning kills into an identification tool), no count at all (feedback becomes a memory test, against the concept's feedback-clarity promise and MDA Challenge), and any deferred or batched count (ruled out by Assassination's gate: a cost must be known to stick).

**PEN-R15. Owner-only data supplied to the HUD.** Penalty supplies, to the owning connection only (IVP Owner audience): `WrongTargetTally` (through `FStrikeResult.TallyAfter` per strike, and the C-TeamView owner re-push with `LastResultSeq` on reclaim, rejoin and reset only, PEN-R3; CR-19 approved by the user 2026-10-25), the latched `NpcKillStrikeLimit` (public tuning), the exile flag (`bExiled`, carried in the same `FStrikeResult` as the causing command's `ClientCommandSeq` (ASN-R19) so HUD can gate the message on the plate; the Roster's Owner-audience state change carries no command sequence), and while awaiting a pawn the countdown end `T_due` (server time; it reveals nothing to anyone else), carried as an Owner-only field of the C-TeamView owner part (Roster R6, IVP R2 Owner row, ADR-0001 §6; user decision 2026-10-25), written when Penalty schedules or reschedules the respawn and cleared at the binding, both through `SetOwnerRespawnDue(P, T_due, ExpectedEpoch)` (Roster R6 owner-view feed; `T_due = 0` clears). Round's end-of-round `TeamSummary[t].WrongTargetCount` is a team aggregate Round derives from the Event Log's `KillResolved` rows (Round R9 (a)), never from Penalty's `StrikeCount`; it equals the sum of the team's `WrongTargetTally` values and is released only at `Ended`. Nothing Penalty owns is ever Team or All audience: teammates learn of an ally's exile only through the Roster's Team-audience state and HUD's team feed line (art bible 7.3 row 12), which reads the same for every removal reason in MVP.

**PEN-R16. The exile moment and the informant's view.** The removal message (title **"Informant"**, subtitle **"Too many wrong targets"**, second line "You watch for your team now."; wording fixed by HUD OQ5, closed 2026-10-25 under decision D11, which answers OQ-NKP-4 and the game concept's open question; "exile" stays the internal name) is shown to the exiled player only, once, never repeated (a reconnect does not replay it).
- **One clock for the exile (design review ruling R3).** The exile has a single anchor: **HUD's card-entrance event**. HUD raises it at the `WrongTarget` plate's show time `s` (HUD F2: the later of the local contact frame and the RPC's arrival, never earlier than contact) plus `ExileBeatSec` (0.8 s), gated on `bExiled` and the causing `ClientCommandSeq` (HUD-R9). Camera's `ViewRelease(StrikeLimit)` is raised **by that event**, not by a timer from the contact frame, so the screen can never be black before the card (CR-10); the exile cue fires from the same event (VA-NKP-5). The plate leaves as HUD-R9 specifies (pushed off over `HudPreemptExitSec`, 0.25 s, stepped); there is no cross-fade.
- **Card-timeout fallback.** If no card entrance occurs within `ExileCardTimeoutSec` (2.0 s) of the owner's own `State → Informant` (`StrikeLimit`) arriving, Camera fades anyway and HUD shows the card when the data lands, so an exile can never hang on a frozen view.
- Until the card enters, Camera keeps the view on the released body, without control (CR-10 to Camera CS-R12.1). The card then collapses into the informant frame (art bible 7.3 rows 10-11).
- **Informant killed (design review ruling R2).** A killed informant has no `State` change, so neither the removal-card path nor a binding-keyed fade applies. Its own view stays on its body through the fall and fades over `NoPawnFadeSec` from `DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec` (the same release as an assassinated player, keyed on the own pawn's death state, not on the binding retirement); **no removal card** (the player watched themselves fall, and that is the message); the countdown appears only after the fade completes (CR-10 reason `InformantKilled`, CR-12).
- For the other removal reasons the removal card wording and timing are HUD's and Assassination's (UI-ASN-5); Penalty only supplies the exile text. While awaiting a body the informant sees the neutral view with the countdown; on the takeover Camera cuts to the new body and fades in (CS-R12.2). An informant's frozen tally stays visible to them.

## Formulas

*systems-designer consulted. Defaults: `NpcKillStrikeLimit` L = 3, `NpcCount` 50, `TeamSize` 5, `InformantRespawnDelaySec` 15, walk 140 cm/s (SCL), `StrikeContactSec` 0.40, `DeathFallSec` 1.2 (Assassination), `NoPawnFadeSec` 0.5 (Camera), `InformantRespawnPathCm` 4,500 and `RespawnStarvationSec` 10 (LSC).*

### F1. Strike, tally and exile

The strike_update formula is defined as:

`t' = min(t + WT, 255)` · `k' = k + [Cause = Strike ∧ VK = Npc ∧ S = Active ∧ ¬G]` · `Exile = (k' ≥ L) ∧ (S = Active)`

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| t | int | 0-255 (saturating) | calculated | `WrongTargetTally[P]`, Owner audience |
| WT | int (0/1) | 0-1 | calculated | 1 when this Attack's `FStrikeResult` is `WrongTarget` (an NPC or an informant was killed) |
| k | int | 0-L | calculated | `StrikeCount[P]`, ServerOnly |
| Cause | enum | Strike, Reversal | calculated | from `OnPawnKilled` |
| VK | enum | Player, Npc, Informant | calculated | `VictimKind` from `OnPawnKilled` |
| S | enum | Active, Informant | calculated | the killer's Roster state at the slot |
| G | bool | true/false | calculated | the victim is a released exile body killed within `StrikeOutReleaseGraceSec` of its release (PEN-R5) |
| L | int | 2-5 (TDC hard range; safe 2-4; 1 only in a test preset with `bTestAllowsStrikeLimitOne`) | data file | `NpcKillStrikeLimit` (latched per round) |

**Output Range:** `0 ≤ k ≤ L` and `k ≤ t` always, so exile can only occur when `t ≥ L` ("the game may be kinder than the tally, never harsher"). Exile happens at most once per participant per round (an exiled participant is `Informant` and cannot attack).
**Example:** kills in order NPC, informant, NPC, NPC. Tally 1, 2, 3, 4; strikes 1, 1, 2, 3; exile on the fourth kill, with the HUD having read "Wrong targets: 3" before it.

### F2. Respawn due time

The respawn_due formula is defined as:

`T_due = max(t_lost + D, t_floor, t_now)` with `t_floor = DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec + NoPawnFadeSec` for a dying body, `t_floor = t_lost` otherwise, and `D = 0` for an informant body lost without a kill

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| t_lost | float (s, server time) | round time | calculated | server time of the kill or exile slot, or of the disconnect that released an embodied informant's pawn (PEN-R7, R11) |
| D | float (s) | 5-60 | data file | `InformantRespawnDelaySec` |
| DeathServerTime | float (s) | t_lost + `T_v` (`T_v` = `StrikeContactSec`, or `StrikeContactSec + BushStrikeEmergeSec` ≤ 1.2 s for a blind strike into a bush, I-ASN-3) | calculated | Assassination ASN-R21 contact write |
| DeathFallSec | float (s) | Assassination range | data file | the fall clip (ASN-R17) |
| NoPawnFadeSec | float (s) | Camera range | data file | the fade to the neutral view (CS-R12.1) |
| t_now | float (s) | round time | calculated | the evaluation time (matters for a reclaim) |

**Output Range:** with defaults `t_floor − t_lost` = 0.4 + 1.2 + 0.5 = 2.1 s. At every knob's hard maximum it is 0.8 + 2.0 + 1.5 = 4.3 s for an open strike and 1.2 + 2.0 + 1.5 = **4.7 s** for the bush variant (I-ASN-3 caps `T_v` at 1.2 s), so for `D ≥ 5` the floor never decides (0.3 s margin) and remains a guard (ASN-R22). TDC checks `1.2 + DeathFallSec + NoPawnFadeSec ≤ InformantRespawnDelaySec` as a cross-field warning (Penalty CR-11). The delay is counted **from the cause**, not from the fall's end, so every cause waits the same time (counting from the fall would give an exiled player, whose body does not fall, a head start of about 1.6 s). A reclaim never shortens it: `t_now` only pushes it later.
**Example:** a kill slot at 100.0 s: floor 102.1 s, `T_due` = max(115.0, 102.1, 100.0) = **115.0 s**. The same informant disconnects at 103 s and reclaims at 120 s: `T_due` = max(115.0, -, 120.0) = 120.0 s, the takeover is attempted at once.

### F3. Informant downtime against Pillar 3

The informant_downtime formula is defined as:

`T_black = (T_due − t_lost) + L_pick` · `T_walk = d / v` · `T_out = T_black + T_walk`

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| T_due − t_lost | float (s) | D (F2) | calculated | the respawn delay actually waited |
| L_pick | float (s) | 0 to unbounded; ≈ 0.5 nominal | calculated | time until `PickRespawnPawn` finds an eligible unseen pawn; widening at `RespawnStarvationSec` (10 s) and 2 × 10 s (LSC R5b.5) |
| d | float (cm) | ≤ `InformantRespawnPathCm` (W = 0), ≤ 2 × it (W = 1), layout-dependent (W = 2) | data file | walk from the takeover point out of the region; LSC measures the takeover point's path to the anchor (`d_anchor`), so the walk out is about `InformantRespawnPathCm − d_anchor` and the table value is an **upper bound**, not a nominal (W = widening level 0-2: level 1 after 10 s, level 2 after 20 s of starvation) |
| v | float (cm/s) | 140 | constant (SCL walk; informants are clamped to walk) | informant speed |
| T_black | float (s) | ≥ D | calculated | time on the neutral (black) view |
| T_out | float (s) | ≥ D | calculated | time until the informant is back out of its quarter |

**Output Range:** nominal `T_black` ≈ D + 0.5; starved until the second widening (W = 2) `T_black` ≈ D + 20. **Budget (P3: "never removes you from the match")**: nominal (median) `T_black ≤ 30 s` and starved (p95) `T_black ≤ 40 s`, which bounds the safe range of D to ≤ 20 s; below about 8 s, silencing an informant buys nothing (creative-director), which bounds it to ≥ 10 s.
Walking out of the informant's own quarter is not itself denial (the informant watches its own side meanwhile); the cost of a kill to the victim's team is F5's `T_deny`, which uses the walk back to where it was, not the walk out of the region.
**Example:** D = 15: `T_black` = 15.5 s; `T_walk` ≤ 4,500 / 140 = 32.1 s; `T_out` ≤ 47.6 s (upper bound). Starved until W = 2: `T_black` ≈ 35 s; a takeover in the W = 1 region gives `T_walk` ≤ 9,000 / 140 = 64.3 s, and at W = 2 the walk is layout-dependent.

### F4. Tally-versus-strike residual (how often the locked removal timing hints at an informant)

The removal_residual formula is defined as:

`q = b·I / (b·I + N)` · `P_res = 1 − (1 − q)^L`

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| I | int | 0 to TeamSize − 1 (0-4) | calculated | embodied enemy informants in the round at the time |
| N | int | 50 | data file | `NpcCount` |
| b | float | 1-3 | constant (assumed, playtest-calibrated) | odds ratio: how much more likely a hunter's suspicion falls on an informant than on an NPC (an informant who shadows people looks suspicious) |
| L | int | 2-5 (TDC hard range; 1 only in a test preset) | data file | `NpcKillStrikeLimit` |
| q | float | 0-1 | calculated | share of `WrongTarget` victims who are informants |
| P_res | float | 0-1 | calculated | chance that a player who reaches tally L has at least one non-strike victim among them and so stays active (the only perceivable residual) |

**Output Range:** 0 to about 0.48 at defaults (N = 50, L = 3, I = 4, b = 3). Across the safe ranges it grows with `L` and falls with `N`: at N = 50, L = 5 (the hard maximum, outside the 2-4 safe range), I = 4, b = 3 it is 0.66; at TDC's clamp floor `NpcCount` = 6 (outside every safe range) with L = 5, I = 4, b = 3 it is 0.996 (q = 0.667), where the residual is near-certain. q counts informants only; graced released bodies (rare: one 2 s window per exile) add to it, so `P_res` is a lower bound. The formula is a planning estimate valid within the safe ranges. The residual names no pawn: it says only "at least one of my wrong targets was a watcher or a graced body". At `L = 1` a non-exiling `WrongTarget` would name its victim, which is why `L = 1` is below TDC's hard minimum of 2 and is reachable only in a non-selectable test preset with `bTestAllowsStrikeLimitOne` (I-NKP-1; user decision 2026-10-25, OQ-NKP-11; CR-11).
**Example:** I = 2, b = 1: q = 2 / 52 = 0.038, `P_res` = 1 − 0.962³ = 0.111. I = 2, b = 3: q = 6 / 56 = 0.107, `P_res` = 0.288.

### F5. Expected cost of hunting a suspected informant (degenerate-strategy check)

The informant_hunt_cost formula is defined as:

`E_strikes = c_N` · `T_deny = D + L_pick + d_back / v` · `R = c_I · T_deny / c_N` (defined for `c_N > 0`)

**Variables:**
| Variable | Type | Range | Source | Description |
|----------|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| c_N | float | 0-1 | calculated | the hunter's true chance that the suspect is an NPC |
| c_I | float | 0-1 | calculated | the true chance that the suspect is an enemy informant (`c_N + c_I ≤ 1`; the rest is an Active rival). A pawn watched continuously since before the enemy's first elimination cannot be a fresh informant (takeover only picks unseen pawns), which lowers `c_I` for long-watched suspects; no rule is needed |
| D, L_pick, v | | | F2, F3 | respawn delay, pick latency, walk speed |
| d_back | float (cm) | layout-dependent | calculated (level benchmark: the layout's median path from a `TeamSpawn` anchor to an objective court) | the walk from the takeover point back to where the informant was watching |
| T_deny | float (s) | ≥ D | calculated | informant time denied to the victim's team by one successful informant kill |
| R | float (s per strike) | ≥ 0 | calculated | informant-seconds denied per expected strike spent: an exchange rate, not a verdict |

**Output Range:** 0 when `c_I = 0`; undefined at `c_N = 0` and growing without bound as `c_N → 0`. A high R near `c_N → 0` is intended: the informant's own behaviour identified it, which is Pillar 2 working. **Example:** `c_N` = 0.6, `c_I` = 0.3, D = 15, `L_pick` = 0.5, `d_back` = 6,000 cm (assumed benchmark): `T_deny` = 15 + 0.5 + 42.857 = 58.357 s; `E_strikes` = 0.6; R = 0.3 × 58.357 / 0.6 = **29.18 s per strike**. F5 does not declare the trade good or poor; the playtest metric is the **informant share of `WrongTarget` victims** compared with F4's base rate q: if it exceeds about 2 × q and correlates with winning, revisit `InformantRespawnDelaySec` (OQ-NKP-3). The strike-free informant kill stays invisible to the hunter kill by kill (PEN-R14).

## Edge Cases

*systems-designer + game-designer consulted (edge-case sweep over Sections C and D, in the same combined call). Same-tick ordering is always Round's key (R11); follow-ons run inline in the causing slot.*

**Same-slot and same-drain races**
- **If a player's `L`-th NPC kill and their own assassination resolve in the same drain:** the lower key acts first. *NPC kill first*: strike, exile, the body is released to the crowd in the same call (PEN-R4); the rival's strike then resolves `VictimKind = Npc` on that body: `WrongTarget`, tally +1, and **no strike** because the body is inside its release grace (PEN-R5). *Assassination first*: the player becomes `Informant (Assassinated)`; its queued Attack is voided `NotNow` at its slot (ASN-R2) and the NPC lives. (Supersedes Roster edge case line "NPC kill first … `AlreadyResolved`", CR-3.)
- **If two exiles, one per team, would empty both teams in one drain:** each needs its own Attack item; the first in key order latches `EliminationWin` for the other team (Roster F4 requires the other team to still have an `Active` player); the second Attack is `Rejected_Latched` and counts nothing.
- **If a team's last `Active` player is exiled:** the exile's Roster event drives Round's elimination check inside the same slot: `EliminationWin` for the other team (`EndReason = Elimination`). The NPC body still falls and the plate still reads `WrongTarget`; no respawn is scheduled (PEN-R4 step 4).
- **If an informant is struck twice in one drain:** the first kill retires its binding inline (PEN-R7); the second resolves `NotTargetable(AlreadyResolved)`: a `NoContact` on a falling body, the same as a second strike on any falling NPC; no tally, no strike.
- **If a takeover candidate is struck in its commit tick:** key order decides. *Attack first*: the pawn is dead, not eligible; `PickRespawnPawn` redraws (LSC R5b.3). *Takeover first*: the strike kills an informant: `WrongTarget`, tally +1 for the attacker, no strike, and a new `T_due` for the informant.
- **If the exiled player's released body is struck after its grace:** an ordinary NPC kill and a strike for the attacker (the creative-director's stale-read cost); the card is vacated and refilled like any NPC's.
- **If a witness strikes the released body within its grace:** `WrongTarget`, tally +1, no strike; the witness may infer the striker was exiled (the witness-side residual, PEN-R14.2, accepted).
- **If an embodied informant is killed:** its own view stays on its body through the fall and fades from `DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec`; no removal card; the countdown appears after the fade (PEN-R16).
- **If the exile's `FStrikeResult` arrives late or never:** HUD's card entrance waits for it (plate show time + `ExileBeatSec`); Camera keeps the held view until the card enters, or fades at `ExileCardTimeoutSec` after the owner's `StrikeLimit` state arrives, and the card is shown when the data lands (PEN-R16).
- **If a participant at k = L − 1 strikes a bracing NPC** (NPCs brace and reverse exactly like players, ASN-R7): the Attack resolves `Reversal`, not `Kill`, so it is not an NPC kill and cannot be the L-th strike; the reversal is `Cause = Reversal`, which never counts (ASN-R11) and is not a `WrongTarget` (no tally change). P is eliminated `ReversedAgainst`, keeps k = L − 1, the NPC lives, and P is scheduled like any eliminated player (AC-NKP-32).

**Timers, rounds and connections**
- **If a respawn timer is pending when the round latches or resets:** `OnRosterRoundEnded` cancels it; a stale item that still fires returns `Rejected_StaleEpoch` and a stale `BindPawn` is `Rejected_WrongState`/`Rejected_StaleEpoch` (Roster edge cases). The participant stays pawnless in `Ended` (neutral view, Round R18).
- **If a disconnected `Active` player (in grace, AI-driven pawn) is assassinated:** the pawn falls like any victim; the participant becomes `Informant` and Penalty puts it in Absent with `t_lost` = slot and no timer (PEN-R8). On return, inside or after the grace, reclaim from Absent applies (F2 with the kept `t_lost`); the later grace expiry is a logged no-op (cross-review S-B3 2026-10-24).
- **If an informant disconnects while awaiting or picking:** the item is cancelled; nothing is bound to an unconnected participant. On reclaim during `Active`, F2 with the kept `t_lost`: the remaining delay is served, never restarted and never skipped.
- **If an embodied informant disconnects and rejoins to be moved home faster:** its pawn is released at the disconnect, `t_lost` = that moment, and on return it waits the full `InformantRespawnDelaySec` from the disconnect (F2). Disconnecting is never faster than walking.
- **If an `Active` player's grace expires (Disconnect):** no strike changes (a crowd-driven pawn never attacks, CNB-R10); the participant is `Absent`; on return it is a returning informant with `t_lost` = the expiry time, so it usually takes over at once.
- **If no eligible unseen pawn exists:** LSC widening after 10 s and 20 s, then an indefinite wait on the neutral view with `RespawnStarved` logged (PEN-R10). No pawn is ever spawned and the unseen test is never relaxed.
- **If two informants of one team are due in the same drain:** they resolve in key order; a pawn claimed by the first is ineligible for the second (LSC claim rule).
- **If an informant's body is lost without a kill (KillZ, destroyed by a defect):** immediate re-pawn through PEN-R9 with `D = 0` (F2), no tally or strike change, `PawnLost` logged (CR-8).
- **If a participant reclaims mid-timer:** `T_due` is unchanged; the neutral view and the countdown resume from C-TeamView's owner push.

**Tuning and rules**
- **If `NpcKillStrikeLimit` = 1 (reachable only behind TDC's dev/test flag since the hard minimum became 2, user decision 2026-10-25):** every NPC kill exiles; a `WrongTarget` that does not exile would name its victim as an informant (or a graced body), breaking "never which". TDC warns on `L = 1` and keeps it out of host-selectable presets and `Default` (Penalty CR-11); test presets may use it.
- **If `NpcKillStrikeLimit` is changed mid-round:** no effect; it is latched at `Preparing` (TDC rule CR-11 Latching).
- **If `NpcKillStrikeLimit` is authored as 0:** TDC clamps it to the hard minimum **2** (user decision 2026-10-25, OQ-NKP-11 closed), so a clamped authored value can never reach the `L = 1` case; `L = 1` is reachable only through TDC's dev/test flag on a non-selectable test preset (the `L = 1` warning still fires there).
- **If a takeover keeps picking past the countdown:** the plate reads a static "Returning" with no count-up or widening level (UI-NKP-4); the informant-side residual of PEN-R14.3 is accepted.
- **If the tally exceeds `L` while the player is still `Active`:** the counter line reads "Wrong targets: 4" with all `L` squares filled and the danger triangle; the limit is never written on the HUD (it lives on the rules card and in onboarding), so the HUD never states the residual in words; the HUD copy must handle `t > L` (CR-12 to HUD).
- **If a player kills an Active rival:** `RivalEliminated`; neither tally nor strike changes.
- **If a player's Attack whiffs or is reversed:** no tally, no strike (`NoContact` / `Reversed` are not `WrongTarget`).

**Degenerate strategies**
- **Deliberate self-exile** (to become an informant on purpose): not profitable: it costs the team an `Active` member, three exposed strikes and `T_out` (F3) of downtime; an informant is strictly weaker than an Active player except that it carries no death risk.
- **Hunting suspected informants to blind the enemy:** costs an expected `c_N` strikes per attempt (F5) at full exposure, and the hunter never learns from the kill itself whether it worked; at most, later, that one of their wrong targets was not a strike, never which (PEN-R14). Accepted; tracked by the F5 playtest metric.
- **Turning a disconnect into an informant conversion:** an `Active` player whose grace expires becomes an informant through Round R17 and, on return, waits out `InformantRespawnDelaySec` from the expiry (F2), usually already served. It costs the team an `Active` member and needs a real network drop for `DisconnectGraceSec`; strictly worse than staying `Active`. Accepted.
- **An informant dropping its connection as a hunter approaches:** its pawn is released to the crowd at once (PEN-R11), so the hunter's strike becomes an NPC kill and a strike, with no grace. Rare, needs a deliberate disconnect, and the informant pays a full new delay. Accepted.
- **The 2 s strike-free window after any exile:** for `StrikeOutReleaseGraceSec` after an exile, striking the visible striker costs no strike; this is the witness-side residual (PEN-R14.2) and is accepted (creative-director R1e).
- **Informants camping the enemy's respawn region:** watchers make the pawns they see ineligible, so the takeover happens out of their sight; widening bounds the added wait to 20 s; they learn nothing because the takeover is invisible (LSC edge case). Accepted.
- **Informant body-blocking a doorway, alley or fixture:** an informant has exactly an NPC's collision and avoidance (PEN-R13); a citizen standing still in a doorway is a readable deviation, and killing it is free if the read is right and costs a strike if wrong. No informant-only restriction is added (it would be a tell). Fixtures and objective paths must not have single-pawn-width chokepoints (CR-13 to Objectives and LSC); blocking incidents are a playtest metric (OQ-NKP-3).
- **An informant relaying the enemy's struck-out body by voice** ("the one who killed three just went into the alley"): legitimate team play; the exit routine (PEN-R5) and its give-up rule keep it an ordinary walk.

## Game Feel

*art-director consulted.* `c` = the attacker's local contact frame (ASN-R16); `s` = the plate's show time (HUD F2: the later of `c` and the RPC's arrival, never earlier than `c`); `e` = HUD's card-entrance event, `s + ExileBeatSec` (PEN-R16).

| Beat | Target |
|---|---|
| Plate and tally tick | At `s` (normally `c`): plate urgent entrance (250 ms stepped), tally square fill 300 ms, numeral changes in one step (no roll-up). Identical for every `WrongTarget`. |
| Exile hold | `s` to `e` (0.8 s): the camera stays on the released body, no control; the transient grade runs its course. The beat is felt as a held breath, not a delay. |
| Exile card | At `e`: the plate is pushed off over `HudPreemptExitSec` (0.25 s, stepped; HUD-R9, no cross-fade); the card enters over 600 ms; Camera's `ViewRelease` fires from `e` and the world fades to black over `NoPawnFadeSec` (0.5 s). Relative to `e`: plate gone at +0.25 s, world black at +0.5 s, card in at +0.6 s; card holds 4 s, then collapses over 800 ms into the informant brackets. Fallback: no `e` within `ExileCardTimeoutSec` of the owner's `StrikeLimit` state, and the camera fades anyway. |
| Informant killed | The own view stays on the falling body (no control), fades over `NoPawnFadeSec` from `DeathServerTime + DeathFallSec`; no card, no exile cue; the countdown appears when the fade completes. |
| Waiting | Neutral view with the countdown in whole-second steps; no pulse, no colour change near zero. |
| Return | Cut to the new body with the informant grade already on, 0.5 s fade-in; control on the cut frame; the head does not move (CS-R12.2). |

**Responsiveness:** the tally is never earlier than the plate and never later than the plate's entrance; the exile card is never earlier than `s + ExileBeatSec`, and the screen is never black before the card has entered (except by the timeout fallback). **Photosensitivity:** grade, plate, fade and card fall within 1.4 s and must fit the art bible 7.6 rule 7 one-sequence budget (verify with the analysis tool before VS; OQ-NKP-6, shared with HUD VA-HUD-4).
